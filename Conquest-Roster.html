    Generate a linked campaign

    Challenge another saved roster, choose quests after acceptance, then generate a three- or four-game shape. Each Mordheim matchup draws from three thematic scenarios and avoids repeats when possible. Battle cards show both players’ quest objectives; recorded results update quest progress, branches, roles, boons, and both rosters’ Battle Records.

    Challenges and progress are stored in this browser only. This static roster app does not send invitations or synchronize between devices; both players need to accept and record results here together.

      Field reference

      Starting limits

      Default order-of-battle limits before territory bonuses: 2 Characters (including Wizards), 1 Special, 1 Rare, 1 Cavalry, 1 War Machine/Chariot, 1 Allied/Mercenary, 0 Monstrous, 0 War Beast/Behemoth, 1 Magic Item. Wizard level starts at 1.

      A named character does not gain renown. The general begins with 5 renown. Veterans gain ranks at 5, 15, and 30 renown; Characters/Wizards use Hero, Rising Hero, Mighty Hero, and Legendary Hero.

      Conquest campaign aid based on the rules recorded in Conquest.md. This is an independent, editable tracker; it does not replace the rules or faction army books.
