    Field reference

    Starting limits

    Default order-of-battle limits before territory bonuses: 2 Characters (including Wizards), 1 Special, 1 Rare, 1 Cavalry, 1 War Machine/Chariot, 1 Allied/Mercenary, 0 Monstrous, 0 War Beast/Behemoth, 1 Magic Item. Wizard level starts at 1.

    A named character does not gain renown. The general begins with 5 renown. Veterans gain ranks at 5, 15, and 30 renown; Characters/Wizards use Hero, Rising Hero, Mighty Hero, and Legendary Hero.
