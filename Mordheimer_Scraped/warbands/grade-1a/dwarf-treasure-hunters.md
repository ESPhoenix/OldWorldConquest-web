# Dwarf Treasure Hunters

**Grade:** 1a<br> **Source:** Town Cryer #4 ([PDF](https://broheim.net/downloads/warbands/official/Dwarf%20Treasure%20Hunters.pdf)), revised in Mordheim Annual 2002<br> **Setting:** Mordheim<br> **Author:** Mark Havener

***Lore***

*Dwarfs are a grim and exceptionally proud people. They respect three things above all else: age, wealth and skill. It is no surprise then that these grim warriors can be found in Mordheim searching for fame and fortune.*

*Occasionally, a Dwarf noble will find himself in desperate times. His family hold may have been overrun by Goblins or Skaven, or he may have somehow disgraced himself and been banished. Other Dwarfs know these warriors as the Dispossessed. Dwarfs are a proud race and it is against a Dwarf’s nature to lose himself in despair. Instead, a noble who finds himself in such dire straits will gather together a group of his closest friends and kin and go treasure hunting, hoping to accumulate a large enough hoard to establish his own holding. At this time, the largest source of wealth in the Known World is rumoured to be a city in the Empire. The city is known as Mordheim…*

## Special Rules

All Dwarfs are subject to the following [special rules:](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/empire-in-flames/new-equipment.md#special-rules)

**Hard to Kill.** Dwarfs are tough, [resilient](../../skills/standard-skills.md#resilient) individuals who can only be taken out of action on a roll of 6 instead of 5-6 when rolling on the Injury chart. Treat a roll of 1-2 as [knocked down](../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down), 3-5 as [stunned](../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned), and 6 as out of action.

**Hard Head.** Dwarfs ignore the special rules for [maces](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#hammer-staff-mace-or-club), [clubs](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#hammer-staff-mace-or-club), etc. They are not easy to knock out!

**[Armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour).** Dwarfs never suffer [movement](../../core-rules/rules/movement.md#movement) penalties for wearing [armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour).

**Hate Orcs and Goblins.** All Dwarfs hate Orcs and Goblins. See the psychology section of the Mordheim rules for details on the effects of [hatred](../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#hatred).

**Grudgebearers.** Dwarfs hold an ancient grudge against Elves from the days when the two races fought for supremacy in the Old World. A Dwarf warband may never include any kind of Elven Hired [Sword](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword) or [Dramatis Personae](../../campaigns/dramatis-personae.md#dramatis-personae).

**Incomparable Miners.** Dwarfs spend much of their lives underground searching for precious minerals, and they are the best in the world at this kind of work. In the city of Mordheim they apply similar skills to the search for wyrdstone. When checking for wyrdstone at the end of a game, add +1 to the number of pieces found for a Dwarf warband.

## Choice of warriors

A Dwarf warband must include a minimum of 3 models. You have 500 gold crowns which you can use to recruit and equip your warband. The maximum number of warriors in the warband is 12.

- **Noble:** Each Dwarf warband must have one Noble – no more, no less!
- **Engineer:** Your warband may include up to 1 Engineer.
- **Troll Slayers:** Your warband may include up to 2 Troll Slayers.
- **Dwarf Clansmen:** Your warband may include any number of Dwarf Clansmen.
- **Dwarf Thunderers:** Your warband may include up to 5 Dwarf Thunderers.
- **Beardlings:** Your warband may include any number of Beardlings.

## Dwarf Special Skills

Dwarf Heroes may use the following Skill table instead of any of the standard Skill tables available to them.

### Master of Blades

This Dwarf’s martial skills surpass those of a normal warrior; he has fought unscathed against hordes of Orcs and Goblins. When using a weapon that has a [Parry](../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#parry) [special rule](../../campaigns/scenarios/town-cryer/gathering-of-the-horde.md#special-rule), this hero parries successfully if he beats or matches his opponents highest ‘to hit’ roll, not just if he beats the roll. In addition, if this warrior is using two weapons that have the [Parry](../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#parry) [special rule](../../campaigns/scenarios/town-cryer/gathering-of-the-horde.md#special-rule), he is allowed to [parry](../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#parry) two attacks (if his two dice match or beat the two highest Attack dice against him) instead of the normal maximum of one. Note that if this Dwarf has two Dwarf axes (as detailed above) he can reroll any failed parries.

### Extra Tough

This Dwarf is notorious for walking away from wounds that would kill a lesser being. When rolling on the Heroes [Serious Injury chart](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/lustria.md#serious-injury-chart) for this Hero after a game in which he has been taken out of action, the dice may be re-rolled once. The result of this second dice roll must be accepted, even if it is a worse result.

### Resource Hunter.

This Dwarf is especially good at locating valuable resources. When rolling on the [Exploration chart](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/khemri/income.md#exploration-chart) at the end of a game, the Hero may modify one dice roll by +1/-1.^❓

### True Grit

Dwarfs are hardy individuals and this Hero is hardy even for a Dwarf! When rolling on the Injury table for this Hero, a roll of 1-3 is treated as [knocked down](../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down), 4-5 as [stunned](../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned), and 6 as out of action.

### Thick Skull

The Hero has a thick skull, even for a Dwarf. He has a 3+ save on a D6 to avoid being [stunned](../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned). If the save is made, treat a [stunned](../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned) result as [knocked down](../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down) instead. If the Dwarf also wears a [helmet](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#helmet), this save is 2+ instead of 3+ (this takes the place of the normal [Helmet](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#helmet) [special rule](../../campaigns/scenarios/town-cryer/gathering-of-the-horde.md#special-rule)).

### 1 Dwarf Noble

**85 gold crowns to hire**

Dwarf Nobles are fortune seekers who have recruited a band of like-minded Dwarfs and set off from their stronghold in search of riches. A Dwarf Noble is well respected by the members of his warband. Often he is a member of one of the noble families of the lost Dwarf strongholds, dreaming of collecting enough treasure to restore the former glory of the Dwarf Kingdoms.

**Leader:** Any models in the warband within 6" of the Dwarf Noble may use his Leadership instead of their own.

### 0 – 1 Dwarf Engineer

**50 gold crowns to hire**

Dwarf Engineers are respected members of Dwarf society. It is they who design and build the complex war machines and devices which have made [the Dwarfs](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning.md#the-dwarfs) famous.

#### SPECIAL RULES

**Expert Weaponsmith:** A Dwarf Engineer is a master of mechanical devices. By using stronger construction materials and time-tested secrets of Dwarf engineering, a Dwarf Engineer can increase the distance the warband's [missile weapons](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/spoils-of-war.md#missile-weapons) can shoot. [Missile weapons](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/spoils-of-war.md#missile-weapons) bought from the warband's basic equipment lists have their range increased by 3" for [pistols](../../weapons-armour/blackpowder.md#pistol) and 6" for [crossbows](../../weapons-armour/missile.md#crossbow) and [handguns](../../weapons-armour/blackpowder.md#handgun). Range increases only apply in battles in which the Engineer takes part (though they do apply even if he is yet to enter [the battlefield](../../campaigns/scenarios/archive-pestilen/the-battle-at-koleshire-keep.md#the-battlefield) or has already been taken *out of action* in this battle). He has no effect on the equipment of Hired [Swords](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword).^✏️

### 0 – 2 Dwarf Troll Slayers

**50 gold crowns to hire**

Troll Slayers are members of the morbid Dwarf cult obsessed with seeking an honourable death in combat. Having committed some unforgivable crime or been dishonoured in some way, a Dwarf will forsake his home and wander off to die fighting the enemies of Dwarfkind. Troll Slayers are insanely dangerous individuals, psychopathic and violent. There are however few better fighters in the Known World, so they are much sought after by Dwarf treasure hunters.

**Weapons/[Armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour):** Troll Slayers may be equipped with weapons chosen from the Dwarf Warrior equipment list. Slayers may never carry or use [missile weapons](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/spoils-of-war.md#missile-weapons) or any form of [armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour).

#### SPECIAL RULES

**Deathwish:** Troll Slayers seek an honourable death in combat. They are completely immune to all psychology and never need to test if fighting alone.

**Slayer Skills:** Troll Slayers may choose a skill from the Troll Slayer Skill table instead of the normal Skill tables when they gain a [new skill](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/khemri/going-underground.md#new-skill).

#### TROLL SLAYER SKILLS

Dwarf Slayers may use the following Skill table as well as any of the standard Skill tables available to them.

**Ferocious Charge:** The Slayer may double his attacks on [the turn](../../core-rules/rules/the-turn.md#the-turn) in which he charges. He will suffer a -1 ‘to hit’ penalty on that turn.

**Monster Slayer:** The Slayer always wounds any opponent on a roll of 4+, regardless of Toughness, unless his own Strength (after all modifiers due to weapon bonuses, etc) would mean that a lower roll than this is needed.

**Berserker:** The Slayer may add +1 to his [close combat](../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat) ‘to hit’ rolls during [the turn](../../core-rules/rules/the-turn.md#the-turn) in which he charges.
