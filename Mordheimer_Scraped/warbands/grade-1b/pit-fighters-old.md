# Pit Fighters (old)

**Grade:** 1b<br> **Source:** Town Cryer #14 ([PDF](https://broheim.net/downloads/towncryer/TownCryer14.pdf#page=36))<br> **Setting:** Mordheim

info

These are the original [Pit Fighter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter) rules from Town Cryer #14

***Lore***

*All across the world you can find gladiatorial pit fights, where hardy fighters take up arms against each other. From the vicious pits of Cutthroat’s Haven to the Black Pits surrounding Mordheim, there can be heard the clash of steel. In most places, local law bans these fights, but the culture itself remains a haven for ex-mercenaries, escaped convicts, and itinerant conmen — and bribes to local officials ensure that this brutal sport continues unabated.*

*There are as many different types of fights as there are venues, from small fistfights in an old barn or a back alley to huge conflicts with many heavily armed combatants. Most fighters are slaves who work for the so-called ‘circuit’. They try to earn their freedom by paying off their owners with a large cut of the winnings. Most successful Pit Fighters live for the sport (and, of course, often die for it also!) because they know little else.*

*The men in this Warband consist solely of professional Pit Fighters who were either able to buy their freedom or have escaped. Now they roam the streets of Mordheim in search of the Wyrdstone that would bring them wealth faster than anyone can imagine.*

*The Pit Fighters are used to fighting in streets and small corridors, and this makes them a much-feared opponent. The Pit King is the most skilled and cunning [Pit Fighter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter) of them all, who leads his band with an [iron fist](../grade-1c/maneaters.md#hand-to-hand-combat-weapons).*



**Appearance:** Pit Fighters are grizzled veteran warriors covered with many scars of battle, they are armed with special Pit Weapons and [Armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour), their bodies well developed and muscular. They wear little clothing and only partial [armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour) known as Pit [Armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour).

## special Rules

**Hired [Swords](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword):** Pit Fighters may hire all Hired [Swords](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword) available except for the [Elf Ranger](../../campaigns/hired-swords/core.md#elf-ranger), who feels working with such dirty and brutish individuals would just not do.

**[Pit Fighter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter):** All Pit Fighters have the [Pit Fighter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter) skill as described in the Strength Skill List except the [Pit Fighter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter) Initiates.([Pit Fighter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter) Skill: +1 WS, +1 A when fighting in ruins, buildings and The Pit.)

**Free the Slaves!** Pit Fighters hate all Slavers. The Pit Fighters will never sell their captured opponents to the slavers.

**In the Pit!** Pit Fighters who capture an opponent may decide to let him fight in the infamous fighting pits of Cutthroat’s Haven (use the rules that appeared earlier in this magazine). The [Pit Fighter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter) Warband may decide to send in one or more of their own fighters to fight the captive, if the [Pit Fighter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter) wins he gains +2 [Experience](../../campaigns/experience.md#experience), the Warband gets all the Captive’s [armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour) and weapons +50ge, if the [Pit Fighter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter) loses then roll to see whether he is dead or injured as normal (ignore all following results: Robbed, Captured, Hardened, Sold to the Pits and Survives Against the Odds), he will not lose his [armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour) or weapons, the Captive will get the 50 gc and the +2 [Experience](../../campaigns/experience.md#experience) when he wins. If the Captive wins the audience decides whether he gains his freedom. Roll a D6 on a 4+ the audience raise their thumbs and he is free. A 1-3 means he remains the Pit Fighters Captive and may be fielded in the pits after future games.

## Choice of warriors

A [Pit Fighter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter) warband must include a minimum of three models. You have 500 gcs to recruit your initial warband. The maximum number of warriors in the warband is 15, though some buildings in the warband’s encampment may increase this.

**Pit King:** Each [Pit Fighter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter) warband must have one Pit King – no more, no less.<br> **[Pit Fighter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter) Troll Slayer:** Your warband may include a single [Pit Fighter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter) Troll Slayer.<br> **[Pit Fighter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter) Veterans:** Your warband may include up to two Veterans.<br> **Ogre [Pit Fighter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter):** Your warband may include a single Ogre [Pit Fighter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter).<br> **[Pit Fighter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter) Initiates:** Your warband may include up to two Initiates.<br> **Pursuers:** Your warband may include up to seven Pursuers.<br> **Pit Fighters:** Your warband may include any number of Pit Fighters.

## Special Skills

#### Pit Fighter skills

**Bulging Biceps:** The [Pit Fighter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter) may ignore the entire ‘heavy’ weapons [special rule](../../campaigns/scenarios/town-cryer/gathering-of-the-horde.md#special-rule) penalty. The Strength bonus will now apply to all rounds in CC. So a [Morning Star](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#morning-star) will give +1 Strength in all turns not only the first one.

**Force of Will:** When the [Pit Fighter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter) loses his last wound and is taken OOA, he must roll a D6 equal or under his Toughness, each following round he has to roll again on his toughness but then with a -1 modifier for each subsequent round. If he succeeds he gets up and may continue to fight, when he gets taken Out of Action a second time he’ll be removed as normal.

**Arms master:** The [Pit Fighter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter) may now ignore all ‘difficult to use’ rules for all weapons, this enables him to combine certain types of weapons, he can combine a [Morning Star](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#morning-star) with [buckler](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#buckler), or even a [Morning Star](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#morning-star) with a [Morning Star](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#morning-star).

**Body Slam:** Instead of making a normal charge the [Pit Fighter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter) may attempt to slam his opponent to the ground, he burrows his shoulder deep into his opponent’s stomach. Instead of making his normal attacks the [Pit fighter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter) may make a single attack resolved as following: +1Str, +1 to Hit, no weapon bonuses or abilities. [Critical hit](../../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#critical-hits) on a 5+.

**Grizzled Veteran:** The [Pit Fighter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter) is used to fighting [fearsome](../../skills/standard-skills.md#fearsome) monsters and being cornered in the Pit when there's no escape. He has been close to death so many times that he has made his peace and the thought of Death no longer bothers him. The [Pit Fighter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter) is immune to all psychology.

#### Troll Slayer Special Skills

Dwarf Slayers may use the following Skill table as well as any of the standard Skill tables available to [the Dwarfs](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning.md#the-dwarfs).

**Ferocious Charge:** The Slayer may double his attacks on [the turn](../../core-rules/rules/the-turn.md#the-turn) that he charges. He will suffer a —1 ‘to hit’ penalty on that turn.

**Monster Slayer:** The Slayer always wounds any opponent on a roll of 4+, regardless of Toughness, unless his own Strength (after all modifiers due to weapon bonuses, etc.) would mean that a lower roll than this is needed.

**Berserker:** The Slayer may add +1 to his [close combat](../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat) ‘to hit’ rolls during [the turn](../../core-rules/rules/the-turn.md#the-turn) which he charges. (may not be used with Ferocious Charge).

## Special Equipment

#### War Chain

**Cost:** 20 gc<br> **Availability:** Common (Pit Fighters only)

*The War Chain is a heavy metal chain up to twelve feet in length often weighted on the end. This is a difficult weapon to use and only after months of practice can a [Pit Fighter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter) become adept with this weapon. The War Chain is often used at a distance to entangle a foe so that the wielder may then close with a [sword](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword) or [dagger](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#dagger) and finish off the prone foe.*

**Range:** 4"<br> **Strength:** As user<br> **[Special Rules:](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/empire-in-flames/new-equipment.md#special-rules)** Cannot be parried, Reach, Entangle

**Cannot be Parried:** As [Steel Whip](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#steel-whip).

**Reach:** As [Steel Whip](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#steel-whip).

**Entangle:** Whenever you hit an opponent on a natural score of a 6 he becomes entangled. Whilst entangled an enemy cannot do anything and can be treated as [stunned](../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned); in his phase he must make a successful Initiative test to free himself or stay down. [Roll to wound](../../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#roll-to-wound) as normal. As long as the opponent is entangled the chain cannot be used, and the [Pit Fighter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter) must use his secondary weapon.

---

#### Spiked Gauntlet

**Cost:** 20 gc<br> **Availability:** Rare 6 (Always bought in pairs)

*Same as Brass Knuckles from TC 7.*

**Range:** [Close combat](../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat)<br> **Strength:** As user +1<br> **[Special Rules:](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/empire-in-flames/new-equipment.md#special-rules)** Pair, Cumbersome

**Pair:** [Spiked Gauntlets](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#spiked-gauntlet) are used in pairs and the warrior fighting with them gains an extra Attack. He may not use any other weapons or items in his hands while doing this. He is free to swap his [spiked gauntlets](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#spiked-gauntlet) for other weapons and items during the battle but may not do this if in combat.

**Cumbersome:** [Spiked Gauntlets](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#spiked-gauntlet) are difficult to use due to the fact that they offer little in the way of range and a warrior must get close up to his opponent before he can strike. For this reason a warrior using [spiked gauntlets](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#spiked-gauntlet) suffers a –2 to Initiative in [close combat](../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat).

**Grudgebearers:** Dwarfs hold an ancient grudge against Elves from the days when the two races fought for the supremacy of the Old World. If the Pit Fighters ever hire any kind of Elven Hired [Sword](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword) he will leave the warband immediately; he refuses to fight alongside these pansy Elves!

**Death Wish:** Troll Slayers seek an honourable death in combat. They are completely immune to all psychology and never need to test if fighting alone.
