# Forest Goblins

**Grade:** 1b<br> **Source:** Nemesis Crown Supplement ([PDF](https://broheim.net/downloads/warbands/supplement/nemesiscrown/Forest%20Goblins.pdf))<br> **Setting:** Nemesis Crown<br> **Author:** Mordheim Nemesis Crown Development Team

***Lore***

*Forest Goblin tribes dwell deep within The Great Forest. They have completely adapted to their surroundings, learning stealth and camouflage to evade their neighbours.*

*Gold motivates the Forest Goblins as it does any warband, albeit for different reasons. It is worthless to them as currency but as a metallic resource, it is valuable nonetheless. Instead of using gold to buy weapons and [armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour), Forest Goblins actually coat their stone equipment with the metal. This has led unscrupulous leaders to send their henchmen into certain ambush, only to arrive later to collect the arrowheads and [spear](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#spear) points from the remains of their former employees.*

*Forest Goblins are able to create poisons with a precision surpassing even the Dark Elves. Their natural surroundings harbour any number of poisonous creatures, including the gigantic spiders they are rumoured to prod into combat with their enemies. Coating their weapons with deadly venom, the Goblins are capable of felling any foe, no matter their size. Led by their Chieftain (usually the Goblin with the loudest voice), the Forest Goblins appear from nowhere and strike with feral ferocity before receding into the trees, leaving injuries as the only proof they were ever there.*

## Special Rules

**Natives:** Used to poking through the underbrush, Forest Goblins suffer no [movement](../../core-rules/rules/movement.md#movement) penalties from moving through any wooded terrain.

**[Animosity](../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#animosity).** Orcs and Goblins enjoy nothing more than a good scrap, unfortunately they’re not always very discerning about who they scrap with! To represent this, at the start of the Orc player’s turn, roll a D6 for each Henchman who is either an Orc or a Goblin. A roll of 1 means that the warrior has taken offense to something one of his mates has done or said. Do not roll for models that are engaged in [hand-to-hand combat](../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#who-can-fight) (they’re already scrappin’!). To find out just how offended the model is, roll another D6 and consult the following chart to see what happens:

***[Animosity](../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#animosity) Result***

| D6 | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **1** | **“I ’Erd Dat!”**<br> *The warrior decides that the nearest friendly Orc or Goblin Henchman has insulted his lineage or personal hygiene and must pay the price!* <br> If there is a friendly Orc or Goblin Henchman or Hired [Sword](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword) within charge reach (if there are multiple targets within reach, choose the one nearest to the mad model), the offended warrior will immediately charge and fight a round of [hand-to-hand combat](../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#who-can-fight) against the source of his ire. At the end of this round of combat, the models will immediately move 1" apart and no longer count as being in [close combat](../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat) (unless one of them fails another [Animosity](../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#animosity) test and rolls this result again). <br> If there are no friendly Orc or Goblin Henchmen or Hired [Swords](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword) within charge reach, and the warrior is armed with a missile weapon, he immediately takes a shot at the nearest friendly Orc or Goblin Henchman or Hired [Sword](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword). If none of the above applies, or if the nearest friendly model is an Orc Hero, the warrior behaves as if a 2-5 had been rolled on this chart. In any case, the warrior in question may take no other action this turn, though he may defend himself if attacked in [hand-to-hand combat](../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#who-can-fight). |
| **2-5** | **“Wud Yoo Say?”**<br> *The warrior is fairly certain he heard an offensive sound from the nearest friendly Orc or Goblin, but he’s not quite sure. He spends [the turn](../../core-rules/rules/the-turn.md#the-turn) hurling insults at his mate.* <br> He may do nothing else this turn, though he may defend himself if attacked in [hand-to-hand combat](../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#who-can-fight). |
| **6** | **“I’ll Show Yer!”**<br> *The warrior imagines that his mates are laughing about him behind his back and calling him silly names. To show them up he decides that he’ll be the first one to the scrap!* <br> This model must move as quickly as possible towards the nearest enemy model, charging into combat if possible. If there are no enemy models within sight, the Orc or Goblin warrior may make a normal move immediately. This move is in addition to his regular move in the [Movement phase](../../core-rules/rules/movement.md#moving), so he may therefore move twice in a single turn if you wish. <br> If the extra move takes the Orc or Goblin warrior within charge reach of an enemy model, the warrior must charge into [close combat](../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat) during his regular [movement](../../core-rules/rules/movement.md#movement). |

## Choice of warriors

A Forest Goblin warband must include a minimum of three models. You have 500 gold crowns which you can use to recruit your initial warband. The maximum number of warriors in the warband may never exceed 20.

**Chieftain:** Each Forest Goblin warband must have one Chieftain.<br> **Braves:** Your warband may include up to four Braves.<br> **Shaman:** Your warband may include one Shaman.<br> **Forest Goblins:** Any number of models may be Forest Goblins.<br> **Red Toof Boyz:** Your warband may include up to five Red Toof Boyz.<br> **Sluggas:** Your warband may include up to five Sluggas.<br> **Gigantic Spider:** Your warband may include one Gigantic Spider.

## Starting experience

A **Chieftain** starts with 17 [experience](../../campaigns/experience.md#experience).<br> A **Brave** starts with 6 [experience](../../campaigns/experience.md#experience).<br> A **Shaman** starts with 6 [experience](../../campaigns/experience.md#experience).<br> **Henchmen** start with 0 [experience](../../campaigns/experience.md#experience).<br> A **Gigantic Spider** does not gain [experience](../../campaigns/experience.md#experience).

## Special Equipment

This warband has access to the following special equipment when purchasing items from the [Trading Post](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post), outside their starting list:

### Blowpipe

**Cost:** [25 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#missile-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Common

*The [blowpipe](../../weapons-armour/missile.md#blowpipe) is a short hollow tube which can be used to shoot poisoned darts. While the darts by themselves are too small to cause significant damage, the poison used can cause searing agony and eventual death. The other advantage of a [blowpipe](../../weapons-armour/missile.md#blowpipe) is that it is silent, and a well-hidden shooter can fire the darts undetected.*

**Range:** 8"<br>**Strength:** 1

##### Special Rules

**Poison:** The needles fired by a [blowpipe](../../weapons-armour/missile.md#blowpipe) are coated in a venom very similar in its effects to the [Black Lotus](../../weapons-armour/miscellaneous-equipment.md#black-lotus) (if you roll a 6 on the To [Hit roll](../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#hit-roll), the victim is automatically wounded). A [blowpipe](../../weapons-armour/missile.md#blowpipe) cannot cause [critical hits](../../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#critical-hits). This weapon has a positive [armour save](../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#armour) modifier, so a model that normally has a save of 5+ will get a save of 4+ against a [blowpipe](../../weapons-armour/missile.md#blowpipe) dart. Even models that normally do not have an [armour save](../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#armour) will get a 6+ save to take into account protection offered by clothes, fur or the like.

**Stealthy:** A model armed with a [blowpipe](../../weapons-armour/missile.md#blowpipe) can fire while hidden without revealing his position to the enemy. The target model can take an Initiative test in order to try to spot the firing model. If the test is successful, the model no longer counts as hidden.

**Weapons/[Armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour):** A Gigantic Spider never needs or uses weapons or [armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour).

#### SPECIAL RULES

**[Fear](../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#fear):** Gigantic Spiders cause [fear](../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#fear) as described in the Mordheim Rulebook.

**Large Monster:** Gigantic Spiders are large targets, and may always be shot at.

**Poisonous:** The Gigantic Spider’s maw drips with vile poisons. When it wounds an enemy and a roll is made on the injury table, 1: [knocked down](../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down), 2-4: [stunned](../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned), and 5-6: Out of Action.

**Native:** Negotiating woodlands is no trouble for spiders. They move through any wooded terrain without penalty.

**Non-sentient:** Spiders are affected by the [stupidity](../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#stupidity) rules in the Mordheim rulebook. In addition, they never gain [experience](../../campaigns/experience.md#experience), as they are animals. Note however that they may climb as normal.

**May Ride:** The Forest Goblin Chieftain, may ride the Gigantic Spider.^✏️ He gains +1 [armour save](../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#armour), and shall move at the Gigantic Spider’s rate of 6. [Shooting](../../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#shooting) attacks hit the Chieftain on a die roll of 1-2, and the Gigantic Spider on a 3-6. In [close combat](../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat), the opponent may choose which to hit. The Gigantic Spider no longer has to check for [stupidity](../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#stupidity) if it is being ridden, as the Chieftain is directing its actions.
