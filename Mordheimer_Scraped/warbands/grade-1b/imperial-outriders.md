# Imperial Outriders

**Grade:** 1b<br> **Source:** Nemesis Crown Supplement ([PDF](https://broheim.net/downloads/warbands/supplement/nemesiscrown/Imperial%20Outriders.pdf))<br> **Setting:** Nemesis Crown<br> **Author:** Mordheim Nemesis Crown Development Team

***Lore***

*As rumours of the discovery of [the Nemesis Crown](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/the-nemesis-crown.md#the-nemesis-crown) spread across the land, the various provinces of the Empire despatch bands of swift horsemen to scout the Great Forest.*

*Theirs is not to take on the enemy in a head-on clash. Instead their role is about stealth. Outpacing and outmanouevring the enemy to leave them empty-handed as they gallop into the distance with the prize.*

## special rules

**Hired [Swords](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword):** The Imperial Outriders may only be accompanied by mounted Hired [Swords](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword). This includes the Freelance Knight from the Mordheim Rulebook and the Roadwarden from [the Empire In Flames](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/empire-in-flames.md#the-empire-in-flames) supplement. The Highwayman keeps himself a safe distance from any official representatives of the Empire and so may not be hired.

## Choice of warriors

An Imperial Outrider warband must include a minimum of three models. You have 500 gold crowns to recruit your initial warband. The maximum number of warriors in the warband may never exceed 12.

**Knight:** Each Outrider warband must have one Knight: no more, no less!<br> **Outriders:** Your warband may include up to 2 Outriders.<br> **Scouts:** Your warband may include up to 2 Scouts.<br> **Chasseurs:** Your warband may include up to 5 Chasseurs.<br> **Hussars:** Your warband may include up to 5 Hussars.<br> **Grooms:** Your warband may include up to 2 Grooms.

## Starting experience

The **Knight** starts with 20 [experience](../../campaigns/experience.md#experience).<br> **Outriders** start with 8 [experience](../../campaigns/experience.md#experience).<br> **Scouts** start with 0 [experience](../../campaigns/experience.md#experience).<br> All **Henchmen** start with 0 [experience](../../campaigns/experience.md#experience)

## Further Mounted Rules

Rules for mounted warriors can be found in the [Mordheim Rulebook](https://mordheimer.net/docs/optional-rules/mounted-combat/mounted-warriors) and in the [Blazing Saddles articles](https://mordheimer.net/docs/optional-rules/mounted-combat/blazing-saddles) in the 2002 Mordheim Annual.

The clarifications below generally work in tandem with these.

### Two Weapon Fighting

Mounted warriors may not fight with two weapons, although a [shield](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#shield) or [buckler](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#buckler) may be used as normal. Two-handed weapons are not permitted. The use of a [pistol](../../weapons-armour/blackpowder.md#pistol) in the first round of [hand-to-hand combat](../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#who-can-fight) replaces the model’s usual weapon.

### Targeting Mounted Warriors

Enemies may not target a ridden horse, whether by missiles or in [hand-to-hand combat](../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#who-can-fight). The rider will always be seen as the greater threat.

Due to their prominence, any mounted warrior may always be chosen as a target by a shooter even if there are enemies closer.

However the shooters do not gain +1 to hit - the rider is still the same size as before.

### Injuries

To determine the effects of wounds on mounted models, use the [Whoa Boy! table](https://mordheimer.net/docs/optional-rules/mounted-combat/blazing-saddles#whoa-boy-table) from the Blazing Saddles article.

### Stunned Riders

[Stunned](../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned) riders will fall from their mounts as indicated on the [Whoa Boy! table](https://mordheimer.net/docs/optional-rules/mounted-combat/blazing-saddles#whoa-boy-table). Mounts which subsequently bolt may be remounted should the warrior make base contact with the horse before it leaves the table. Note that whether the mount leaves the table or not, it will be recovered unscathed [after the battle](../../campaigns/scenarios/other/mordheims-burning.md#after-the-battle).

### Out-Of-Action Models

It is possible for a rider to be killed while his mount survives. In this case the mount may be ridden by the dead model's replacement, reducing his cost by 40gc.

### Dead Horses

Dead horses must be replaced before any other [income](../../campaigns/income.md#income) is spent. All Treasures must be sold to fund this.

Any model without a mount may not take part in the battle.

### Dense Terrain

Imperial Outriders may ignore the normal warband limitation of two mounts in areas of dense terrain.

### Scenarios

Several [scenarios](../../campaigns/scenarios.md#scenarios) involve the moving of models across the board to achieve the objective, either chasing treasure or exiting the board. In these [scenarios](../../campaigns/scenarios.md#scenarios) the players should place sizeable areas of dense terrain such that it is impossible to traverse the board without entering them. This will force the Outriders to dismount and so improve the game.
