# Norse Explorers

**Grade:** 1b<br> **Source:** Town Cryer #13 ([PDF](https://broheim.net/downloads/towncryer/TownCryer13.pdf#page=17)), [Border Town Burning](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning.md#border-town-burning) ([PDF](https://broheim.net/downloads/warbands/unofficial/Norse%20Explorers.pdf))<br> **Setting:** [Lustria](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/lustria.md#lustria)<br> **Author:** Derek Whitman

[Border Town Burning](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning.md#border-town-burning) (1c)

There are two Norse warbands listed on Broheim: one created in Town Cryer for the [Lustria](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/lustria.md#lustria) setting, and then a slightly tweaked version that was in the [Border Town Burning](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning.md#border-town-burning) (BTB) supplement. There is only **one** difference between the two: the stats of the Wulfen/Ulfwerenar; which has an extra wound in the BTB version. The author for the [Lustria](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/lustria.md#lustria) warband confirmed The Wulfen in Town Crier for [Lustria](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/lustria.md#lustria) was also supposed to be 2 wounds. It was a copy paste error by whoever did the layout.

***Lore***

*The land of Norsca lies to the far north of the Empire across the stormy sea of Claws. This is a harsh land of snow and ice with mountains of Frost and Dusk that reach unto the heavens themselves and march right down to the sea.*

*The humans that live here are powerfully built, with large, well muscled frames and long limbs. [The Norse](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning.md#the-norse) are a bloodthirsty people whose society is based entirely around war and fighting. They make or produce very little in their inhospitable homeland and their attitude is to take whatever they need from the lands of others. Many a time their dreaded longships have brought terror and destruction to the lands of men and the other races.*

*[The Norse](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning.md#the-norse) people live in marauder tribes or clans much like the Unberogens who were, centuries earlier, the founders of the Empire. It is for these reasons that [the Norse](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning.md#the-norse) are seen as a very primitive and uncivilised race by others and indeed many Norse tribes openly worship the dark gods of Chaos as their own.*

*All Norse warriors are trained in the art of war at a very young age and become skilled in the use of [sword](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword), [axe](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#axe) and [shield](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#shield) for their’s is a brutal society where only the strongest survive. Probably because of their well founded [fearsome](../../skills/standard-skills.md#fearsome) reputation the Norsemen have few allegiances apart from to their clan and their ancestors and so make excellent mercenaries.*

*Despite their brutish behaviour [the Norse](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning.md#the-norse) make superb natural sailors rivalling even the High Elves and it was indeed [the Norse](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning.md#the-norse) who were the first humans to discover [Lustria](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/lustria.md#lustria) and the South Lands. In their longships, [the Norse](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning.md#the-norse) sailed from the Old World to [Lustria](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/lustria.md#lustria), establishing the first colony, Skeggi. [The Norse](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning.md#the-norse) plague the coast of [Lustria](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/lustria.md#lustria) raiding many coastal settlements but it is their desire for riches and saga that have driven them further inland to pillage the temples of the Slann.*

## special rules

All Norse Warriors are subject to the following [special rules:](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/empire-in-flames/new-equipment.md#special-rules)

**Seafaring:** [The Norse](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning.md#the-norse) are seafaring people and spend much of their time rowing boats. To represent this, all Norse warriors get +2 to Strength when they row a boat.

Seafaring

The usage of the Strength characteristic for rowing only comes up in [the scenario](../../campaigns/scenarios/archive-pestilen/the-forbidden-square.md#the-scenario) [The Script of Sigmar](../../campaigns/scenarios/town-cryer/the-script-of-sigmar.md), presumably this was intended to be expanded upon later but this never happened.

## Choice of warriors

A Norse Warband must include a minimum of three models. You have 500 gold crowns to recruit your initial Warband. The maximum number of warriors in the Warband may never exceed 15.

**Jarl:** Each Norse Warband must have one Jarl to lead it – no more, no less.<br> **Berserker:** Your Warband may include up to two Berserkers.<br> **Bondsmen:** Your Warband may include up to two Bondsmen.<br> **Wulfen:** Your Warband may include up to one Wulfen.<br> **Marauders:** Your Warband may include any number of Marauders.<br> **Hunters:** Your Warband may include up to five Hunters.<br> **Wolves:** Your Warband may include up to five Wolves provided it includes a Wulfen in it.

## Starting experience

A **Jarl** starts with 20 [experience](../../campaigns/experience.md#experience).<br> **Berserkers** & the **Wulfen** start with 11 [experience](../../campaigns/experience.md#experience).<br> **Bondsmen** start with 0 [experience](../../campaigns/experience.md#experience).<br> **Henchmen** start with 0 [experience](../../campaigns/experience.md#experience).

## Norse special skills

Norse heroes may use the following skill list instead of any of the standard skill lists.

### barbarian courage

As Norse warriors strive to die bravely in battle, they learn to [fear](../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#fear) nothing and embrace hardship. This hero never needs to take [all alone tests](../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#all-alone) and may re-roll failed [fear](../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#fear) tests.

### battle tongue

Only a hero with the leader skill may gain this skill. It allows models within 12" of him to use his leadership, rather than the normal 6".

### berserk charge

[The Norse](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning.md#the-norse) are very skilled with weapons that many other races see as primitive and savage. When this hero is armed with an [axe](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#axe) or [double handed weapon](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#double-handed-weapon), he may re-roll all failed to hit rolls when he charges.

### crushing blow

Norse warriors train in almost all of their spare time. They are expert fighters and learn to put all of their strength into very powerful attacks. No enemy may [parry](../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#parry) an attack made by this hero because it strikes with such great power that it pushes right through a [buckler](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#buckler) or [sword](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword).

### shield master

Norse warriors begin training with [shields](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#shield) when they are still children. Some warriors become so skilled with these weapons they can block almost any blow against them. When this hero is armed with a [shield](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#shield) he may [parry](../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#parry) with it in addition to getting a 6+ save.

## heroes

### 1 Jarl

**70 gold crowns to hire**

*The Jarl of a Norse warband is the most battle hardened veteran of his group and often a lesser clan chieftain of his tribe. He has seen many battles and is used to the bloodshed. While some Jarls choose to command their forces with strategy, most just run into the fray with the rest of the warriors. However the Jarl decides to run his warband, all of the other warriors respect him and look up to him.*

### 0 – 1 Wulfen

**90 gold crowns to hire**

*Wulfen are Berserkers with a very rare mutation. When there is a full moon, or when blood starts flowing on [the battlefield](../../campaigns/scenarios/archive-pestilen/the-battle-at-koleshire-keep.md#the-battlefield), the mysterious and deadly Wulfen turn into blood hungry beasts, part man, and part wolf or bear. These creatures are known and greatly feared as werewolves to most Old Worlders. To [the Norse](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning.md#the-norse) they are Werekin, monstrous beasts which can tear through flesh and [armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour) with their huge claws and massive fangs as they go on the rampage.*

#### Special Rules

**[Fear](../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#fear):** Wulfen are terrifying creatures to behold and cause [fear](../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#fear).

**Bestial:** Wulfen are ravenous beasts and therefore *immune to psychology*. Also despite being greatly revered by their barbarian kinsmen, Wulfen are too feral and uncontrolled to become the leader of the warband.

#### Special Rules

**Animals:** Wolves are animals and thus do not gain [experience](../../campaigns/experience.md#experience).

**Pack Leader:** Wolves are feral animals that only respond to the strongest in their pack – the Wulfen. In the event that no Wulfen is included in the warband due to a death or an injury, the wolves cannot be used until the creature is replaced.
