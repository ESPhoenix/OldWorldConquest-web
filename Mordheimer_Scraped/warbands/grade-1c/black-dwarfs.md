# Black Dwarfs

**Grade:** 1c<br> **Source:** [Border Town Burning](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning.md#border-town-burning) ([PDF](https://broheim.net/downloads/warbands/supplement/btb/Black%20Dwarfs.pdf))<br> **Setting:** [Border Town Burning](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning.md#border-town-burning)

***Lore***

*Few scholars know how exactly these Dwarfs came to be the evil and twisted creatures of Chaos they are now. A long and slow warping process has changed them forever. Sundered from others of their kind, trapped in underground holds on the ash-choked Plain of Zharr, darkness swept over them. From a worship born out of desperation, the bull-shaped god Hashut became their saviour and [the Dwarfs](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning.md#the-dwarfs) of Karak Vlag became a perverted mockery of their former selves. Through infernal pacts with their mysterious daemon deity this bitter race has endured and gained an unnatural mastery of foul magic. They practice bloodthirsty rites which involve throwing captives into burning cauldrons of molten metal.*

*[The Black Dwarfs](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-black-dwarfs) constantly trade [armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour), weapons and machines forged out of precious metals with their [hammers](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#hammer-staff-mace-or-club) and vile [sorcery](../../skills/standard-skills.md#sorcery), for fresh supplies and victims. Those condemned souls are brought back to the Dark Lands. Deep beneath the the ziggurat shaped obsidian tower they are sentenced to toil in the labour pits of Mingol Zharr-Naggrund, the City of Fire and Desolation.*

*Fell Sorcerer Priests mastermind tactical raids harvesting prisoners on [the Silver Road](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning.md#the-silver-road) to expand their labour force. Scouting for rich mineral veins and metal ores while scouring the land for wyrdstone deposits, has led detachments of the Children of Darkness to Mordheim.*

## Special Rules

The following special rules apply to all warriors in the warband excluding Informers:^✏️

**Hard to Kill:** Like their uncorrupted brethren, Chaos Dwarfs are tough, [resilient](../../skills/standard-skills.md#resilient) individuals who can only be taken out of action on a roll of 6 instead of 5-6 when rolling on the Injury chart. Treat a roll of 1-2 as [knocked down](../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down), 3-5 as [stunned](../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned), and 6 as out of action.

**Hard Head:** Chaos Dwarfs ignore the special rules for [clubs](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#hammer-staff-mace-or-club), [maces](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#hammer-staff-mace-or-club), etc. They too are not easy to knock out!

**[Armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour):** Chaos Dwarfs never suffer [movement](../../core-rules/rules/movement.md#movement) penalties for wearing [armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour).

**Hired [Swords](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword):** A Chaos Dwarf warband may hire the following Hired [Swords](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword): [Ogre Bodyguard](../../campaigns/hired-swords/core.md#ogre-bodyguard), [Pit Fighter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter), Warlock, Imperial Assassin, and Hobgoblin Scout. They may hire any Hired [Sword](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword) described as *all may hire*, or allowed by Orc warbands and Chaos warbands. They may never hire Elves of any sort!

## Choice of warriors

A Chaos Dwarf warband must include a minimum of three models. You have 500 gold crowns which you can use to recruit and equip your warband. The maximum number of warriors in the warband is 15.

**Sorcerer:** Each Chaos Dwarf warband must have one Sorcerer – no more, no less!<br> **Bull Centaur:** Your warband may include one Bull Centaur.<br> **Gaolers:** Your warband may include up to two Gaolers.<br> **Chaos Dwarfs:** Your warband may include up to five Chaos Dwarfs.<br> **Informers:** Your warband may include any number of Informers.

## Starting experience

A **Sorcerer** starts with 20 [Experience](../../campaigns/experience.md#experience).<br> A **Bull Centaur** starts with 10 [Experience](../../campaigns/experience.md#experience).<br> **Gaolers** start with 8 [Experience](../../campaigns/experience.md#experience).<br> **Henchmen** start with 0 [Experience](../../campaigns/experience.md#experience).

## Chaos Dwarf special skills

Chaos Dwarfs may choose to use the following skill list instead of the standard skill lists.

### extra tough

A Chaos Dwarf with this skill is notorious for walking away from wounds that would kill a lesser warrior. When rolling on the Heroes [Serious Injury chart](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/lustria.md#serious-injury-chart) for this Hero after a game in which he has been taken out of action, the dice may be re-rolled once. The result of this second dice roll must be accepted, even if it is a worse result.

### chaos engineer

The Hero has great technical skill and can use this to craft wicked [armours](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour) and weapons. Whenever a Hero with this skill searches for Chaos [armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour) (including Mechanical Suits) or Obsidian Weapons, he gets +3 on the roll.^✏️ This represents the Engineer’s ability to craft these items himself. The Hero ignores the Rarity and Gift of Chaos special rules and may never wear the Chaos [Armours](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour).

### thick skull

The Hero has a thick skull, even for a Chaos Dwarf. He has a 3+ save on a D6 to avoid being [stunned](../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned). If the save is made, treat a [stunned](../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned) result as [knocked down](../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down) instead. If the Chaos Dwarf also wears a [helmet](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#helmet), this save is 2+ instead of 3+ (this takes the place of the normal [Helmet](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#helmet) [special rule](../../campaigns/scenarios/town-cryer/gathering-of-the-horde.md#special-rule)).

### resource hunter

This Chaos Dwarf is especially good at locating valuable resources. When rolling on the [Exploration chart](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/khemri/income.md#exploration-chart) at the end of a game, the hero may modify one dice roll by +1/-1.

### tyrant

This skill is for the Chaos Dwarf leader only. This Priest of Hashut is renowned for his tyranny. His word is absolute so his own warband fears his cruelty more then the enemy. When making a [Rout test](../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#the-rout-test), and if led by a leader with this skill, the leader may inspire his warband to stick around. This skill allows the leader to re-roll any failed [Rout test](../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#the-rout-test), as long as the leader is not [knocked down](../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down) or [stunned](../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned). If re-rolled, the new result will apply even if the new result is worse. If the leader is taken out of action the warband must make an immediate [Rout test](../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#the-rout-test).

### true grit

Chaos Dwarfs are hardy individuals and this Hero is resolute, even for a Chaos Dwarf! When rolling on the Injury table for this Hero, a roll of 1-3 is treated as *[knocked down](../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down)*, 4-5 as *[stunned](../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned)*, and 6 as *out of action*.

## Special Equipment

This warband has access to the following special equipment when purchasing items from the [Trading Post](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post), outside their starting list:

### Steel Whip

**Cost:** [10 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#hand-to-hand-combat-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Common

*Another weapon unique to the Sisterhood is the [steel whip](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#steel-whip), made from barbed steel chains.*

**Range:** [Close Combat](../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat)<br>**Strength:** As user

##### Special Rules

**Cannot be parried:** The [steel whip](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#steel-whip) is a flexible weapon and the Priestesses use it with great expertise. Attempts to [parry](../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#parry) its strikes are futile. A model attacked by a [steel whip](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#steel-whip) may not make parries with [swords](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword) or [bucklers](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#buckler).

**Whipcrack:** When the wielder charges they gain +1A for that turn. This bonus attack is added after any other modifications. When the wielder is charged they gain +1A that they may only use against the charger. This additional attack will 'strike first'. If the wielder is simultaneously charged by two or more opponents they will still only receive a total of +1A. If the wielder is using two whips at the same time then they get +1A for the additional hand weapon, but only the first whip gets the whipcrack +1A.^✏️
