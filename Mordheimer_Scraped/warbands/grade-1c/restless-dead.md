# The Restless Dead

**Grade:** 1c<br> **Source:** [Border Town Burning](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning.md#border-town-burning) ([PDF](https://broheim.net/downloads/warbands/supplement/btb/The%20Restless%20Dead.pdf))<br> **Setting:** [Border Town Burning](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning.md#border-town-burning)

***Lore***

*A Liche is a necromancer who was wise enough never to expend his own life energy to fuel his spells during his lifetime. As time eats away at his body, he retains his soul which allows him to manipulate magic, which is what separates them from pitiful wraiths. Unfortunately, the undead bodies of [the Liche](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-liche) are not capable of retaining magic forever. They must absorb massive amounts of magic through the use of dark rituals and forbidden spells. The most common ritual involves the devouring of the soul of a human. The soulless human becomes a zombie under [the Liche](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-liche)’s control. This continues until he is discovered.*

*When a Liche is found, great armies are sent against it. [The Liche](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-liche) retaliates by creating armies of undead warriors to defend itself, expending vast amounts of stored magical energy to do so. Win or lose, [the Liche](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-liche) is greatly weakened in this process. More often than not, [the Liche](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-liche) is crippled such that he is incapable of bringing himself to his former strength. In this event, [the Liche](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-liche) has two options. Most of them wander south to the forest of Loren where they seek the wild and infinite magic of the wood. Some wander north towards the Chaos Wastes where they are almost always discovered and destroyed en-route. An even smaller percentage go west in ramshackle boats towards the lands of the High Elves... so far, only a small handful of Liches have ever lived in their undead state for very long; even fewer return to fighting strength after crippling defeat.*

*For those who travel north, the journey is long and arduous; dodging road wardens, rogues and thieves all for their goal to reach the Chaos Wastes, where [the Liche](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-liche)'s power will slowly regenerate from the billowing magical winds spewing from the Realm of Chaos.*

note

In the original source, this warband is referred to as '[The Restless Dead](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-restless-dead)', 'Undead Liche' and 'Undead' interchangeably. This has been unified on this page as [The Restless Dead](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-restless-dead).

## Choice of warriors

A Restless Dead warband must include a minimum of three models. You have 500 gold crowns to recruit your warband. The maximum number of warriors may not exceed 12.

Bone Goliath

Note that [the restless dead](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-restless-dead) have access to a unique Hired [Sword](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword) called the **[Bone Goliath](../../campaigns/hired-swords/grade-1c.md#grade-1c-hired-swords)**, this construct has special rules to be included during [warband creation](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/the-procession-of-morr/campaign-overview.md#warband-creation).

**Liche:** Each Restless Dead warband must include one Liche: no more, no less!<br> **Necromancer:** Your warband may include a single Necromancer if you wish.<br> **Grave Guard:** Your warband may include up to three Grave Guards.<br> **Zombies:** Your warband may include any number of Zombies.<br> **Skeletons:** Your warband may include up to eight Skeletons.<br> **Wights:** Your warband may include up to three Wights.<br> **Scarecrows:** Your warband may include up to two Scarecrows.

## Restless Dead special skills

Liches and Necromancers may choose to use the following skill list instead of any of the standard Skill tables available to them.

### corpse bomb

Special magics and rituals can cause a Zombie to explode when they are near the enemy. Secretly nominate one Zombie at the beginning of the battle to be a Corpse Bomb. If the enemy charges or is charged by the Zombie, it immediately detonates. All models within D6 inches take D3 Strength 4 hits. The detonated Zombie may never be used again as it is splattered in a million pieces! Corpse bombs killed by [shooting](../../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#shooting) do not detonate. Only one Zombie at a time can be a corpse bomb although the skill can be taken by both [the Necromancer](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-necromancer) and [the Liche](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-liche).

### deathspeaker

At the start of the battle, the undead player may deploy D3 Zombies for free. These zombies do not count towards the maximum number of models in the warband, but increase the warband’s rating as normal. Zombies created in this way may not be used as Corpse Bombs. These Zombies only last for the duration of the battle.

### wraith touch

The hero may make a Wraith Touch attack instead of their normal attacks in [close combat](../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat). The hero making a Wraith Touch makes a single unarmed attack, if it hits, it wounds automatically, all rules that apply to unarmed attacks apply to the Wraith Touch. If a Liche uses this skill and wounds, then he may regain one lost wound. This may not take the hero beyond his starting total. Necromancers do not regain wounds with this skill. This skill has no effect on the Possessed or Undead.

### forbidden rite

If the hero with this skill did not search for rare items during their last exploration phase, then they start the next battle with a pool of D3+1 (+1) modifiers they can use to increase their casting rolls. They may use as many of these modifiers at a time as they desire.

### summoner

The maximum warband size is increased by 1.

## heroes

### 1 Liche

**125 gold crowns to hire**

*A Liche is a powerful undead sorcerer. They journey north in a desperate bid to regain their power in the Realm of Chaos after a crippling defeat. Liches are fanatical in their struggle to hang on to their pitiful existence, especially when this close to being forever banished from the realm of the living.*

| Profile | M | WS | BS | S | T | W | I | A | Ld |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | 4 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 4 | 4 | 1 | 8 |

**Weapons/[Armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour):** Liches may not carry any non-magical weapons^❓ and do not suffer any penalties for this. They may wear any [armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour) from [the Restless Dead](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-restless-dead) Equipment list.

#### SPECIAL RULES

**Wizard:** A Liche is a powerful wizard and so is able to use Necromantic magic and starts with two spells randomly generated from the [Necromantic magic list](https://mordheimer.net/docs/magic/necromancy-restless-dead).

note

Note that this Necromancy spell list is different to the original spell list from the Core Rules.

**Cause [Fear](../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#fear):** A Liche is a horrible abomination and causes [fear](../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#fear).

**Immune to Psychology:** A Liche is not affected by psychology and never leaves combat.

**No Pain:** A Liche treats a [stunned](../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned) result on the injury chart as [knocked down](../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down).

**Immune to Poison:** A Liche is not affected by poison.

**Eternal:** A Liche can choose to ignore any result on the hero's [Serious Injury chart](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/lustria.md#serious-injury-chart) except *Killed* by taking a permanent -1 on their starting Wound profile. Obviously, a Liche with 1 Wound remaining on their starting profile does not have this option. A Liche that gets a *Killed* result instead takes a permanent –D3 Wounds on their starting profile. If this takes their starting Wound total to 0 or less, [the Liche](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-liche) is *Killed* as normal.

**Feed Upon Magic:** A Liche can perform spells that, with the consumption of D3 Treasures, can give [the Liche](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-liche) a permanent +1 Wound on their starting profile. This can *only* be used in between battles and cannot be used if [the Liche](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-liche) searches for rare items or was put *out of action* in the previous battle. If the warband does not have enough Treasures to complete the spell, then the Treasures are consumed anyway and [the Liche](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-liche) does not gain the Wound.

Treasures

Treasures is the generic term used for the "post-game loot", what in Mordheim would be Wyrdstone.

**[Warrior Wizard](../../skills/standard-skills.md#warrior-wizard):** [The Liche](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-liche) may wear [armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour) and cast spells. Indeed, it is often the clothing and [armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour) alone that gives [the Liche](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-liche) substance and form!

**Advancement:** If a Liche gets an advance roll of +1 Wound, they may instead pick a [new skill](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/khemri/going-underground.md#new-skill) from their available lists.

#### Special Rules

**Wizard:** Necromancers are wizards being trained by their Liche masters in the art of Necromancy and so are able to use Necromantic magic. They start out knowing one of the two spells known by their Liche masters.^❓

**Apprentices:** Necromancers may only ever know spells known by their Liche masters. Even if they did know spells [the Liche](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-liche) didn’t, they wouldn’t dare use them for [fear](../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#fear) of their master’s response for their arrogance! If [the Liche](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-liche) is ever killed, [the Necromancer](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-necromancer) can continue to learn magic spells as a normal wizard, ignoring the Apprentice rule.

**Gofer:** as an Apprentice, [the Necromancer](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-necromancer) spends much of their time [running](../../core-rules/rules/movement.md#running) errands for their Liche masters and over the years get exceptionally good at it. When a Necromancer searches for rare items, they roll 3D6 and pick the two highest.

#### Special Rules

**Cause [Fear](../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#fear):** The sight of a tall, lanky and silent scarecrow moving is highly unnatural and so causes [fear](../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#fear).

**Flammable:** The scarecrow is primarily ratty cloth, straw and preserved body parts so as a result they burn very easily. Scarecrows count as being flammable. In addition, a scarecrow will be 'caught fire' on the roll of a 3+ as opposed to the normal 4+.

Flammable

While it is not specified what being "flammable" means, the consensus is to use the rules from [the Tomb Guardian warband](../grade-1b/tomb-guardians.md#special-rules) and [The Mummy scenario in Town Cryer 15](../../campaigns/scenarios/town-cryer/the-mummy.md#kahotep-the-mummy), being that the scarecrow takes double damage from fire based attacks.

**Construct:** The scarecrow is an undead construct and may re-roll any rolls on the injury table except for wounds caused by fire.

**Animated Construct:** Powerful constructs such as scarecrows require much more concentration from their summoners than normal skeletons or zombies to remain animated. The scarecrow is controlled by either [the Liche](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-liche) or [the Necromancer](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-necromancer) (note down on the warband roster which), but each may only control one at a time, so only a warband with both a Liche and a Necromancer may ever take two Scarecrows. If [the Liche](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-liche) or [the Necromancer](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-necromancer) is for some reason unable to participate in a battle, their Scarecrow will also be unable to participate.

Whenever the scarecrow's controller loses a wound he must pass an unmodified [Leadership test](../../core-rules/rules/characteristics.md#leadership-tests) or the magical bond is broken and the scarecrow is immediately taken out of action.
