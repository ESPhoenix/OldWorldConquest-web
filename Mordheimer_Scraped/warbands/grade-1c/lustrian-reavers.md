# Lustrian Reavers

**Grade:** 1c<br> **Source:** Facebook (2024) ([PDF](https://broheim.net/downloads/warbands/facebook/Lustrian-Reavers-V1.2.pdf)) **Author:** Tuomas Pirinen<br> **Setting:** [Lustria](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/lustria.md#lustria)

***Lore***

*Countless expeditions of ambitious and greedy invaders from the Old World have been lost in [Lustria](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/lustria.md#lustria). Some to diseases, others to Jungle Beasts, and many more to Lizardmen, the stern defenders of their ancient civilization. But despite all these hardships, the tales of the [Cities of Gold](../../campaigns/campaign-settings.md#cities-of-gold) lure more and more adventurers from beyond the sea.*

*Against the odds, some survive. The toughest, most skilled and the most resourceful often band together after the crews they arrive with succumb to the endless perils of the New World. These groups are known as Reavers: the most ruthless, skilled and dangerous of the invaders beyond the ocean. They have claimed the jungle their home, and they thrive within it thanks to their savagery. They are armed and equipped with the very best wargear salvaged from their fallen comrades and entire companies lost to the pitiless jungles of [Lustria](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/lustria.md#lustria).*

*They scorn any newcomers as unwelcome competition for the riches the Reavers still feverishly look for, and only accept a few prospective new members into their small bands. These prospects tend to be young survivors of newly-arrived expeditions or settlers whose abodes were attacked by Lizardmen, Amazons or one of the many primordial monsters prowling the jungles of the New World. Most of these fresh recruits die well before they realize their dream of joining the Reavers as full-fledged members.*

*When one of the Reavers die, the warband spares no effort on recovering the lost equipment, weapons and [armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour), as a new Reaver is selected by the surviving Heroes. The newcomer is welcomed to the ranks of Reavers, and dons the panoply of the predecessor in a ceremony that often involved copious amounts of Estalian wine.*

## Special Rules

#### Rare Heroes

You can only ever buy one of each type of Heroes during the existence of your Warband, representing the extreme rarity of these the toughest of survivors of [Lustria](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/lustria.md#lustria).

#### Promotions

When you lose one of the Heroes for whatever reason, you cannot buy a new one. However, you retain all the equipment of the fallen Hero^❓, and you may now promote one of the Prospects into the role of the lost hero, as if that Henchman just successfully rolled Lad’s Got Talent on the Henchmen Advancement Table: You may choose two skill lists available to Heroes in your warband. These are the skill types your new Hero can choose from when the model gains [new skills](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/new-rules.md#new-skills). The new Hero can immediately make one roll on the Heroes Advance table.

The new Hero then gains the [armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour), weapons and equipment of the fallen Hero and assumes their position the Warband. The Prospect’s own equipment may be added to the Warband stash. If you have no Prospect currently in your Warband, you can promote the next one you hire.

Hero Position

When the prospect takes the Hero position, this means he becomes a warrior of that type, gaining the special rules and wargear access. For all intents and purposes, he is no longer a prospect but becomes the hero of the relevant type, albeit with access to only 2 skill lists.

#### Hired Swords

The Lustrian Reavers are proud and suspicious, and therefore rarely use Hired [Swords](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword). They may hire an [Ogre Bodyguard](../../campaigns/hired-swords/core.md#ogre-bodyguard), Dwarf Trollslayer, Tilean Marksman or a Big Game [Hunter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#hunter).

## Choice Of Warriors

A Lustrian Reaver warband must include a minimum of three models. You have 500 gold crowns available to spend. The maximum number of warriors is 10, though some items like the [Halfling Cookbook](../../weapons-armour/miscellaneous-equipment.md#halfling-cookbook) may increase this number as normal.

maximum warband size

Note that the War Beasts from the Beastmaster also count towards your maximum number of models in the warband!

**Heroes:** Each Lustrian Reaver warband must have at least one Hero to act as a leader. Choose any of your Heroes as the Leader who benefits from the rules for Leader. You can only ever hire 1 of each type of Hero.<br> **Prospects:** Your warband may include up to five Prospects.

## Heroes

### 0 – 1 Conqueror

**150 gold crowns to hire**

*Conquerors are behemoths of men, unstoppable giants wearing the nigh-impenetrable Estalian Masterwork [armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour) and wielding the dreaded [Bec de Corbin](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#close-combat-weapons). They are incredibly tough to take out in battle, but their dedication to the skills at arms means they are often sullen and morose loners who do not work well with others, and despise newcomers to their warbands.*

| Profile | M | WS | BS | S | T | W | I | A | Ld |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Conqueror | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 2 | 3 | 2 | 7 |

**Starting Equipment:** [Masterwork Heavy Armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#heavy-armour), [Bec de Corbin](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#close-combat-weapons), [Helmet](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#helmet). Further weapons, [armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour) and equipment can be bought from the Reavers Equipment list.

#### Special Rules

**Survivor**: Cannot be taken out of Action unless the model is already [Knocked Down](../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down) or [Stunned](../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned). Treat Out of Action as [Stunned](../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned) if the Conqueror is standing when receiving the Wound.

In addition, the first time a Conqueror suffers a Dead result on the [Serious Injury Chart](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/lustria.md#serious-injury-chart), treat it as Multiple Injuries instead. This ability can save the Conqueror only one time.

### 0 – 1 Saurus Slayer

**135 gold crowns to hire**

*Saurus-slayers act as the champions of the Reavers, warriors who have survived where hundreds have fallen. They specialize in dueling Saurus warriors. All Lizardmen are deadly and ferocious opponents and only the mightiest of human warriors can best them in a single combat. Saurus-Slayers are men who have become accustomed to taking on and defeating these mighty guardians of [Lustria](../../campaigns/campaign-settings/lustria.md#lustria), and adorn themselves with grisly trophies of their victories. These spoils of war often offer additional benefits and mystical protection to these supreme champions of Reaver Warbands.*

### 0 – 1 Jungle Shadow

**90 gold crowns to hire**

*Jungle Shadows are survivors of desperate Jungle Warfare against skinks. Where their comrades died, the Shadows survived through woodcraft, cunning, ruthlessness, and stealth. They are the hunted who have become hunters. Many of them were mystics that escaped the persecution of the religious authorities in the Old World.*

| Profile | M | WS | BS | S | T | W | I | A | Ld |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jungle Shadow | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 1 | 4 | 1 | 8 |

**Starting Equipment:** [Javelins](../grade-1b/amazons-lustria.md#missile-weapons), [Light Armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#light-armour), two [Daggers](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#dagger). Further weapons, [armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour) and equipment can be bought from the Reavers Equipment list.

At the cost of +30 Gold Crowns, you can make Jungle Shadow a Wizard. Jungle Shadow uses the [Lesser Magic](https://mordheimer.net/docs/magic/lesser-magic) list. Generate a single Spell as standard for a wizard. If you make the Jungle Shadow a Wizard, remove the [Light Armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#light-armour) from the equipment list when starting out, and generate 1 Lesser Magic spell they can use.

#### SPECIAL RULES

**Silent [Hunter](../../skills/standard-skills.md#hunter)**: Jungle Shadow is always considered to be [Hidden](../../core-rules/rules/movement.md#hiding) if the model is touching any piece of terrain of 1” high. [Shooting](../../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#shooting) does not reveal Jungle Shadow’s position, though spellcasting does.

**Surprise attack**: If the Shadow attacks an enemy that has its back turned to the Jungle Shadow (i.e. the shot is coming from 180 degree angle from behind its facing), the Shadow can re-roll missed to hit rolls.

**Canopy Walker**: Jungle Shadow automatically passes all [climbing](../../core-rules/rules/movement.md#climbing), [jumping](../../core-rules/rules/movement.md#jumping-down) and [falling](../../core-rules/rules/movement.md#falling) off ledges tests.

### 0 – 1 Trapmaster

**140 gold crowns to hire**

*Combining the hunting skills of the Old World trappers with the fiendish skills of their skink enemies, the Trapmasters are essential for the Reaver warbands in controlling [the battlefield](../../campaigns/scenarios/archive-pestilen/the-battle-at-koleshire-keep.md#the-battlefield), sniping at enemies from distance, and laying snares and traps to catch unwary foes.*

| Profile | M | WS | BS | S | T | W | I | A | Ld |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Trapmaster | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 1 | 4 | 1 | 8 |

**Starting Equipment:** [Heavy Armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#heavy-armour), [Sword](../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword), Tilean [Hunting Rifle](../../weapons-armour/blackpowder.md#hunting-rifle) (Treat as [Hochland Long Rifle](../grade-1b/gunnery-school-of-nuln.md#missile-weapons)), [Firepots](lustrian-reavers.md#special-equipment), Leaf Coat (counts as an [Elven Cloak](../../weapons-armour/miscellaneous-equipment.md#elven-cloak)). Further weapons, [armour](../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour) and equipment can be bought from the Reavers Equipment list.

#### SPECIAL RULES

**Deadeye**: Trapmaster suffers no penalties for [shooting](../../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#shooting) at long range.

**Traps**: The Trapmaster starts each game with 1 Trap and can buy up to 5 further of the following Traps before the start of the game at the cost of 5 GC each. Each trap is one use only. Traps are used in the [Shooting Phase](../../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#who-can-shoot) instead of [shooting](../../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#shooting), 1 trap at a time, and cannot be used in Melee combat. When you use a trap, put 2 1” diameter Trap counters (one false, one real) anywhere within 3” of the Trapmaster, but at least 3” away from any other models. Any model that moves within 2” of the counter must flip it over. If it is a false one, nothing happens. If it is a real one, the model suffers D3 S5 Hits immediately. Traps do not cause Criticals. Remove [the counters](../../campaigns/scenarios/archive-pestilen/the-forbidden-square.md#the-counters) when the trap is sprung.
