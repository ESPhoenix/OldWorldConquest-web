# Recovery Phase

During the [recovery phase](recovery.md#recovery-phase) you may attempt to rally any of your models who have lost their nerve (see the [Recovery Phase](recovery.md#recovery-phase) rules). To take a Rally test, roll 2D6. If the score is equal to or less than the model’s Leadership value the model stops fleeing and has rallied; turn it to face in any direction you wish. The model cannot move or shoot for the rest of [the turn](the-turn.md#the-turn), but models able to do so can cast spells. If the test is failed, the model will continue to flee towards the closest table edge.

Note that a model **cannot** rally if the closest model to him is an enemy model (*fleeing*, *[stunned](wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned)*, *[knocked down](wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down)* and *hidden* models are not taken into consideration for this).

During the [recovery phase](recovery.md#recovery-phase), warriors in your warband who have been *[stunned](wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned)* become *[knocked down](wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down)* instead and warriors who have been *[knocked down](wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down)* may stand up (see the [Injuries section](wounds-and-injuries.md)).
