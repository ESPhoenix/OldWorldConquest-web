# The Turn

In Mordheim you are in a charge of a warband and your opponent is in charge of another.

The warriors and scenery in the game are [set up](../../campaigns/scenarios/town-cryer/the-hunters-become-the-hunted.md#set-up) on the tabletop in a way that is appropriate for the encounter you are fighting. Do not worry about this for now as these things will be explained in full in the [Scenarios section](../../campaigns/scenarios.md).

To play, one side takes a turn, then the other side, then the original side and so on, much like in a game of draughts or chess. When it is your turn, you may move all your models, shoot with any warriors able to do so, and fight in [hand-to-hand combat](close-combat.md#who-can-fight). Once your turn is complete, it is your opponent’s turn to move, shoot and fight.

Turn sequence

### 1. Recovery

During the [recovery phase](recovery.md#recovery-phase) you may attempt to rally individuals who have lost their nerve and recover models that are [knocked down](wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down) or [stunned](wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned).

### 2. Movement

During the [movement phase](movement.md#moving) you may move the warriors of your warband according to the rules given in the [Movement section](movement.md).

### 3. Shooting

In the [shooting phase](shooting.md#who-can-shoot) you may shoot with any appropriate weapons as described in the rules for [shooting](shooting.md#shooting).

### 4. Hand-to-hand combat

During the [hand-to-hand combat](close-combat.md#who-can-fight) phase all models in [hand-to-hand combat](close-combat.md#who-can-fight) may fight. Note that both sides fight in the [hand-to-hand combat](close-combat.md#who-can-fight) phase, regardless of whose turn it is.

## phases

To keep track of who is doing what and when, each turn is split into four phases. This is called the *Turn sequence*.
