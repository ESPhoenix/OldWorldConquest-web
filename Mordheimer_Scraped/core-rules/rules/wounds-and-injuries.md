# Wounds & Injuries

## injuries

Most warriors have a Wounds characteristic of 1, but some have a value of 2 or more. If the target has more than 1 wound then deduct 1 from his total each time he suffers a wound. Make a note on the roster sheet. So long as the model has at least 1 wound remaining he may continue to fight.

As soon as a fighter’s Wounds are reduced to zero, roll to determine the extent of his injuries. The player who inflicted the wound rolls a D6 for the wound that reduced the model to zero wounds and for every wound the model receives after that. If a model suffers several wounds in one turn, roll once for each of them and apply the highest result.^❓

### 0 – 2 knocked down

A fighter who has been [knocked down](wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down) falls to the ground either because of a jarring blow he has sustained, because he has slipped, or because he has thrown himself to the ground to avoid injury.

Turn the model face up to show that he has been [knocked down](wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down). [Knocked down](wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down) models may crawl 2" during the [movement phase](movement.md#moving), but may not fight in [hand-to-hand combat](close-combat.md#who-can-fight), shoot or cast spells. If he is in base-to-base contact with an enemy, a [knocked down](wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down) model can crawl 2" away only if the enemy is engaged in [hand-to-hand combat](close-combat.md#who-can-fight) with another opponent, otherwise he has to stay where he is. In combat he cannot strike back and the enemy will have a good chance of putting him out of action (see the [Warriors Knocked Down section](wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down).

A warrior who has been [knocked down](wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down) may stand up at the start of his next turn. In that turn he may move at half rate, shoot and cast spells, he cannot charge or run. If he is engaged in [hand-to-hand combat](close-combat.md#who-can-fight), he may not move away and will automatically strike last, irrespective of weapons or Initiative. After this turn the fighter moves and fights normally, even though he has zero wounds left. If the model takes any further wounds, then roll for injury once more, exactly as if the model had just sustained its last wound.

### 3 – 4 stunned

When a warrior is [stunned](wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned), he is either badly injured or temporarily knocked out. Turn the model face down to show that he has been [stunned](wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned). A fighter who is [stunned](wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned) may do nothing at all.

A player may turn the model face up in the next [recovery phase](recovery.md#recovery-phase), and the warrior is then treated as [knocked down](wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down).

### 5 – 6 out of action

A warrior who is out of action is also out of the game. Remove the model from the tabletop. It’s impossible to tell at this point whether the warrior is alive or dead, but for game purposes it makes no difference at this stage. [After the battle](../../campaigns/scenarios/other/mordheims-burning.md#after-the-battle) you can test to see whether he survives and if he sustains any serious lasting injuries as a result of his wounds (see [Serious Injuries](../../campaigns/campaigns-overview.md#serious-injuries) for details).

## attacking stunned and knocked down warriors in hand-to-hand combat

### warriors knocked down

If an enemy is fighting a warrior who is [knocked down](wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down) in [hand-to-hand combat](close-combat.md#who-can-fight), he may attack him to put him out of his misery. All [hand-to-hand combat](close-combat.md#who-can-fight) attacks against a warrior who is [knocked down](wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down) hit automatically. If any of the attacks wound the [knocked down](wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down) model and he fails his [armour save](close-combat.md#armour), he is automatically taken out of action as explained previously. A [knocked down](wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down) model may not [parry](close-combat.md#parry).

### warriors stunned

A [stunned](wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned) warrior is at the mercy of his enemies. A [stunned](wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned) model is automatically taken out of action if an enemy can attack him in [hand-to-hand combat](close-combat.md#who-can-fight).

*Note that a model with multiple attacks may not stun/knock down and then automatically take a warrior out of action during the same [hand-to-hand combat](close-combat.md#who-can-fight) phase. The only way you can achieve this is to have more than one of your models attacking the same enemy. So, if the enemy is [stunned](wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned)/[knocked down](wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down) by the first warrior, he can be hit and put out of action by the next warrior to attack.*

*If your model is engaged in [close combat](close-combat.md#close-combat) with an enemy who is still standing, he cannot attack any other models that are [stunned](wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned) or [knocked down](wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down), since in reality they will not pose an immediate threat to him and their companions will try to protect them.*
