# Scenarios

## Starting the game

### pre-battle sequence

Although you can simply decide with your opponent which scenario you want to play, most players prefer to generate their [scenarios](scenarios.md#scenarios) randomly. To do this, work your way through the following sequence before the battle.

1. The player with the lowest [warband rating](campaigns-overview.md#warband-rating) rolls on [the Scenario](scenarios/archive-pestilen/the-forbidden-square.md#the-scenario) table to determine which scenario is played. In the [scenarios](scenarios.md#scenarios) where there is an attacker and a defender, the same player may choose which he is.
2. Roll for warriors with old battle wounds to see whether they can take part or not.
3. [Set up](scenarios/town-cryer/the-hunters-become-the-hunted.md#set-up) the terrain and warbands according to the rules for [the scenario](scenarios/archive-pestilen/the-forbidden-square.md#the-scenario) you are playing. The more buildings the better, so you should place all the terrain you have.

### Chaos on the Streets Scenarios

Town Cryer #5 introduced a set of [scenarios](scenarios.md#scenarios) designed specifically for more than two players. To determine which scenario will be used for multiplayer games, players may either choose to play a particular scenario or roll on the following chart:

| 2D6 | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | The player with the lowest [warband rating](campaigns-overview.md#warband-rating) may choose which scenario is played. |
| 3 | Play [Scenario 7: Monster Hunt](scenarios/town-cryer/monster-hunt-tc5.md) |
| 4 | Play [Scenario 4: The Wizard's Mansion](scenarios/town-cryer/wizards-mansion-tc5.md) |
| 5-6 | Play [Scenario 1: Treasure Hunt](scenarios/town-cryer/treasure-hunt-tc5.md) |
| 7 | Play [Scenario 2: Street Brawl](scenarios/town-cryer/street-brawl-tc5.md) |
| 8-9 | Play [Scenario 6: Ambush!](scenarios/town-cryer/ambush-tc5.md) |
| 10 | Play [Scenario 5: The Pool](scenarios/town-cryer/pool-tc5.md) |
| 11 | Play [Scenario 3: The Lost Prince](scenarios/town-cryer/lost-prince-tc5.md) |
| 12 | The player with the lowest [warband rating](campaigns-overview.md#warband-rating) may choose which scenario is played. |

See [Chaos on the Streets](https://mordheimer.net/docs/optional-rules/multiplayer-games) for more information on multiplayer games.
