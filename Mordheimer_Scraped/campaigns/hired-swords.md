# Hired Swords

## recruiting hired swords

This section introduces [Hired Swords](hired-swords.md#hired-swords) – professional mercenaries – to Mordheim campaign games. Taverns in the settlements and shanty towns around Mordheim are good recruitment centres for warriors who do not belong to any particular warband or retinue, but instead hire out their services to the highest bidder.

A player can recruit [Hired Swords](hired-swords.md#hired-swords) when he creates his warband, or during the campaign phase after a game.

[Hired Swords](hired-swords.md#hired-swords) do not count towards the maximum number of warriors or Heroes a warband may have on its roster and don’t affect your [income](income.md#income) from [selling wyrdstone](income.md#selling-wyrdstone). However, [Hired Swords](hired-swords.md#hired-swords) do count as part of the warband for purposes of [Rout tests](../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#the-rout-test), etc whilst in battle.^✏️ A player cannot buy extra weapons or equipment for a Hired [Sword](../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword), and he cannot sell the Hired [Sword](../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword)’s weapons or equipment. To reflect their rarity, you can only have one of each type of Hired [Sword](../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword) in your warband. You may not use the Leadership of any of the [Hired Swords](hired-swords.md#hired-swords) for [Rout tests](../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#the-rout-test).

## hire fee

When a warband recruits a Hired [Sword](../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword), you must must pay his [hire fee](dramatis-personae.md#hire-fee). Subsequently, after each battle he fights, including the first, you must pay his upkeep fee if you want him to remain with the warband. If the Hired [Sword](../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword) is killed, or you no longer require his services, you don’t have to pay any upkeep! These costs are indicated in the entries for each Hired [Sword](../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword).

The money paid to [Hired Swords](hired-swords.md#hired-swords) comes from the warband’s treasury in the same way as buying new weapons or recruiting new warriors. If you don’t have enough gold to pay for the Hired [Sword](../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword), or want to spend it on other things, he leaves the warband. Any [experience](experience.md#experience) he has gained will be lost, even if you hire a new Henchman of the same type.

## injuries

If a Hired [Sword](../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword) goes out of action during the game, roll for his injuries as you would roll for a Henchman after a battle (i.e, 1-2 = Lost; 3-6 = Survives).

## hired swords and experience

[Hired Swords](hired-swords.md#hired-swords) gain [experience](experience.md#experience) in exactly the same way as Henchmen. Refer to the [scenarios](scenarios.md#scenarios) to find out how much [experience](experience.md#experience) [Hired Swords](hired-swords.md#hired-swords) gain after each game.

Write the name and profile of a Hired [Sword](../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword) on your roster sheet in one of the Henchman group slots.

Once the Hired [Sword](../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword) gains enough [experience](experience.md#experience) for an advance, roll on the Heroes Advancement table (as opposed to Henchmen) to determine which advance he gains. Skills available to the [Hired Swords](hired-swords.md#hired-swords) are listed under their entries.

#### Clarification of grades

Core: *Published in the original Mordheim Rulebook.*<br> Grade 1a: *GW/Fanatic Rules deemed "official" in the 2005 Rules Review.*<br> Grade 1b: *Unofficial, but released through GW/Fanatic. Professional quality.*<br> Grade 1c: *Experimental, not released through GW/Fanatic. Approved by people who previously submitted grade 1a/1b material and vouch for it's quality.*<br> Grade 2a: *Reliable, created and tested by fans and gaming groups. Will likely blend well with grade 1 warbands.*

Further grades can be found at [broheim.net](https://broheim.net/dramatispersonae.html).
