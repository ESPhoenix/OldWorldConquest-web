# Scenarios (Lustria)

Use these scenario tables instead of the one from the rulebook. We have included a separate table for [multi-player scenarios](../empire-in-flames/scenarios.md#multi-player-scenarios) as this setting works very well with these games. As usual, the winner of a scenario may roll one extra die in the exploration phase. Roll 2D6 to work out which scenario to play.

An interesting scenario to try is '[kidnapped](../../scenarios/town-cryer/kidnapped.md)' from WD 234, where a Lizardmen player takes over the role of the Possessed in sacrificing a captive. If the Lizardmen manages to sacrifice the captive the Warband earn 3D3 exp to distribute among their heroes.
