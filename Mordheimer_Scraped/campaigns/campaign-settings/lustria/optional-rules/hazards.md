# Hazards (Lustria)

*By Robert Lowery*

> "The teeming landscape of [Lustria](../../lustria.md#lustria) whispers of dangers, only the foolhardy would brave."<br> —Roberto Fortuna, Pathfinder

Before the start of a game, a D6 is rolled to determine the number of hazards plaguing the area. Once play begins, each player will roll a D6 for the [Heroes and Henchmen](../../empire-in-flames/warbands.md#heroes-and-henchmen) groups in the Warband. This roll is made before any [movement](../../../../core-rules/rules/movement.md#movement) occurs, and will therefore exclude any model unable to move.

If a ‘1’ is rolled, the model or models have stumbled into harm’s way! Now roll 3D6 and consult the table below.

## Hazard Descriptions

### 3. Tar Pit

*The unfortunate Warband member has fallen into a tar pit, and will sink until submerged completely.*<br> He must be rescued by another model in D3 turns or else he will drown, taking him out-of-action permanently! Any rescuer must pass a Strength test in order to pull the model free.

*A deadly nest of vipers or lizards has been awoken by the warrior’s careless misstep.*<br> Roll D6+2 for the number of cold-blooded menaces.

*Stumbling into the rope-like strands of web has alerted the deadly occupant.*<br> The giant arachnid will attack any models within 3" of its lair as well as the initial offender.

### 6. Coatl

*The warrior has surprised one of [Lustria](../../lustria.md#lustria)’s most enigmatic inhabitants, the Coatl.*<br> Possessing a snake-like body measuring up to ten feet in length and brightly colored feathery wings, Coatl are often worshiped by the Lizardmen as a living embodiment of Sotek.

**[Special Rules:](../../empire-in-flames/new-equipment.md#special-rules)**

- Causes [Fear](../../../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#fear)

- **Flight** – As flying creatures, Coatl are able to traverse the jungles with ease.
- **Magic Aura** – Being part magical, the Coatl has a natural save against hostile magic of 4+.

*Attracted by the promise of fresh meat, a loathsome Troll has attacked the hapless warrior.*<br> Consider the Troll having charged for the purpose of combat.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | 6 | 3 | 1 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 1 | 3 | 4 |

**[Special Rules:](../../empire-in-flames/new-equipment.md#special-rules)**

- Causes [Fear](../../../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#fear)

### 9. Quicksand

*The jungle floor is full of surprises, but none as subtle as quicksand.*<br> The same rules govern this hazard as the tar pit, except that the model has D6 turns in which to be saved.

### 10. Giant Constrictor

*Tales of snakes reaching gargantuan proportions have not been exaggerated!*<br> This predator will attack any additional models within 2" of the victim using its constricting coils.

| Profile | M | WS | BS | S | T | W | I | A | Ld |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | 6 | 3 | 0 | 5 | 4 | 2 | 4 | 2 | 5 |

**[Special Rules:](../../empire-in-flames/new-equipment.md#special-rules)**

- Causes [Fear](../../../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#fear)
- **Constricting Coils** – The snake may sacrifice its normal attacks in an attempt to engulf its prey within its massive coils. Though its attack has a -1 ‘to hit’, it gains +1S, and may wrap up to 3 models consecutively. Anyone so held by the snake is unable to move and suffers a -2 ‘to hit’ in [close combat](../../../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat).
