# Weather Variables (Lustria)

*Source: Town Cryer #11*

*'The day was wet as we started out into the jungle. Visibility was poor. My men and I cautiously hacked our way through the under-growth listening and watching for those accursed Lizardmen. Through the drizzle we saw a clearing up ahead and decided to take a rest there, that was when they attacked. Skinks leaped from the undergrowth, a third of our [pistols](../../../../weapons-armour/blackpowder.md#pistol) didn't fire due to the dampness and our crossbowmen couldn't see far enough to get a good shot. We drew our [swords](../../../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword) and axes and fought for our lives.'*

Roll on this chart at the beginning of a scenario to see how the weather is going to effect your play.

| 2D6 | Hazard |
| --- | --- |
| 2-6 | Nice and Dry - normal play |
| 7 | Windy |
| 8-9 | Raining |
| 10 | Heavy Rain |
| 11 | Mist |
| 12 | Hot and Humid |

### 7. Windy

*The mystical jungle has thrown up a howling gale making it difficult to shoot and [leap](../../../../skills/standard-skills.md#leap) across buildings/terrain features.*

Roll 1D6: 1-3 = -1 to hit with [missile weapons](../../border-town-burning/spoils-of-war.md#missile-weapons) and to [leap](../../../../skills/standard-skills.md#leap) across buildings, 4-6 = -2 for [shooting](../../../../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#shooting) and leaping.

### 8-9. Raining

*Rain is very common in the lush 'rain' forest of [Lustria](../../lustria.md#lustria).*

All missile and powder weapons are at -1 to hit. Those wishing to fire a Black Powder weapon must first roll 4+ on 1D6 otherwise the powder is too wet to fire.

### 10. Heavy Rain

*The tropical monsoon beats down upon the warriors making [movement](../../../../core-rules/rules/movement.md#movement) through the jungle slow and hazardous.*

There is -1 to all [movement](../../../../core-rules/rules/movement.md#movement). All [missile weapons](../../border-town-burning/spoils-of-war.md#missile-weapons) ranges are halved. Black Powder weapons may not fire at all.

### 11. Mist

*A thick mist has rolled in from the coast.*

Roll 1D6. A roll of 1-5 results in all [shooting](../../../../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#shooting) reduced to half range due to poor visibility. On a '6' warriors can't see where they are going and are disoriented. Using a scatter dice (40k) roll for each character/Henchmen unit. They may move (walking) in that direction. If they run into another warrior they may attack them, if they are friendly roll 1D6, on a 5+ they attack them thinking they are the enemy.

### 12. Hot and Humid

*Part of the reason that [Lustria](../../lustria.md#lustria) remains largely unexplored is because of the extreme heat because of the tropical climate.*

All warriors except Undead are at -1 to WS, BS and I for the duration of the game.
