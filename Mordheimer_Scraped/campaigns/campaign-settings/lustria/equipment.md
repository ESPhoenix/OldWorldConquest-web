# Equipment (Lustria)

The Tilean noble family of Di Ranza was forced out of Magritta after bloody family feuds. They were involved in [trading](../../trading.md#trading) exotic goods and decided to move their business to the source. They built a fortified [trading](../../trading.md#trading) post on [Lustria](../lustria.md#lustria)'s Scorpion coast. This post is used as a staging base for many of the smaller warbands exploring [Lustria](../lustria.md#lustria)'s jungles. Their younger sons, Marius and Donato, adopted the name Ranzato and travelled to the Empire to sell their services to the three Emperors. As it happened, Marius has participated in several successful expeditions that were sent to Mordheim while Donato offered his service to anyone that could afford him in Mordheim. Rumour has it that Donato is more interested in chasing after girls than duelling while his brother Marius is a master [pistolier](../../../skills/standard-skills.md#pistolier) and highly sought after by the Reiklander captains.

The High Elves have had a fortress on the southern rip of [Lustria](../lustria.md#lustria) for a very long time. It is called the Citadel of Dusk and guards the western sea route to Cathay. The High Elves rule the seas and are constantly opposing the Estalian and Tilean [trading](../../trading.md#trading) fleets but they also despatch expeditions into [Lustria](../lustria.md#lustria)'s jungles for their own secretive reasons. Skeggi, [the Norse](../border-town-burning.md#the-norse) colony is easily accessible for most Old Worlders coming co [Lustria](../lustria.md#lustria) for the first time. Whilst [the Norse](../border-town-burning.md#the-norse) are not known to be friendly to outsiders they are always interested in trade. Although at a high price, a warband can buy enough supplies and weapons to make it back to their ship. The Lizardmen and Skaven trade at their ruined temple cities and never venture near the other posts, save for the occasional scouting parties.

You will notice that the availability rolls differ from those in the Mordheim book, e.g. black powder weapons are more rare but Elven weapons and [armour](../../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour) are more readily accessible. This represents that Elven items are more numerous due to the number of Elf warbands in [Lustria](../lustria.md#lustria). All rules from the rulebook apply but use this equipment table instead of the one in the rulebook.
