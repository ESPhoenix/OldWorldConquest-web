# Warbands and Hired Swords (Khemri)

## Warbands

This setting is written with a specific theme in mind and while you can use any warband in this setting some of them are not appropriate. The greed of humanity is too well known and all are willing to risk their lives in search of treasure. Norse and Pirate warbands may be a stretch in [the Land of the Dead](../../campaign-settings.md#the-land-of-the-dead) as they prefer the open seas or coastal raids. Halfling warbands are more at home in the Old World. Lustrian warbands, such as Amazons, aren’t likely to leave their rainforest for a desolate desert.

## Hired Swords

There are plenty of [swords](../../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword) for hire in [the Land of the Dead](../../campaign-settings.md#the-land-of-the-dead); many of them are the last survivors of a raid on a Necropolis, while others are professional mercenaries. In [the Land of the Dead](../../campaign-settings.md#the-land-of-the-dead) you can only use the [Hired Swords](../../hired-swords.md#hired-swords) listed in this section. [The Land of the Dead](../../campaign-settings.md#the-land-of-the-dead) is a long way from Mordheim and even further from [Lustria](../lustria.md#lustria), and not every Hired [Sword](../../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword) will be available.

You can use the following [Hired Swords](../../hired-swords.md#hired-swords) from Town Cryer magazine and the Mordheim rulebook: [Halfling Scout](../../hired-swords/core.md#halfling-scout), Tilean Marksman, [Ogre Bodyguard](../../hired-swords/core.md#ogre-bodyguard) and [Pit Fighter](../../../skills/standard-skills.md#pit-fighter).
