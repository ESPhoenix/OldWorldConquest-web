# Scenarios (The Nemesis Crown)

Each of these new [scenarios](../../scenarios.md#scenarios) is designed to tie in with a specific region within [the Nemesis Crown](../the-nemesis-crown.md#the-nemesis-crown) campaign map. That said, players should feel free to adapt these as they wish.

| **Territory** | **Suggested Scenario** |
| --- | --- |
| The Draken Downs | Scenario 1: [Nightfall](scenarios/nightfall.md) |
| The Drakwald Deeps | Scenario 2: [The Necromancer's Tower](scenarios/enter-the-necromancers-tower.md) |
| The Reik's Marches | Scenario 3: [The Warmachine](scenarios/the-warmachine.md) |
| The Howling Height | Scenario 4: [The Lost Mines of Khrazi Drudd](scenarios/the-lost-mines-of-khrazi-drudd.md) |
| The Rauberthal | Scenario 5: [Last Orders](scenarios/last-orders.md) |
| The Great Confluence | Scenario 6: [The Flood Plain](scenarios/the-flood-plain.md) |
| The Talabec Borders | Scenario 7: [The Shifting Paths](scenarios/the-shifting-paths.md) |
| The Barren Hills | Scenario 8: [Fowl Play](scenarios/fowl-play.md) |
| The Taalford Lowlands | Scenario 9: [The Wild Wood](scenarios/the-wild-wood.md) |

You won't find instructions for [running](../../../core-rules/rules/movement.md#running) a Nemesis Crown based campaign here - that bit's up to you. However we hope this healthy slew of new [scenarios](../../scenarios.md#scenarios) will serve to inspire your imagination.

While each of the [scenarios](../../scenarios.md#scenarios) will stand alone, there's no reason why you can't use them to steer your campaign in a particular direction. Many of them refer to intriguing locales, characters or special items. An imaginative moderator should have no problem making use of one of these as story hooks to propel the campaign forward.

Remember that the area covered by the map is equivalent to a European country. It follows that [the warbands](../../scenarios/archive-pestilen/the-square-of-the-snake.md#the-warbands) are likely to spend some time investigating and exploring each of the territories before moving onto the next. Artefact hunting is a time-consuming business.
