# Spoils of War (Border Town Burning)

The weapons, [armour](../../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour) and [miscellaneous equipment](../empire-in-flames/price-chart.md#miscellaneous-equipment) in this chapter are added to the equipment from the Mordheim rulebook. Note that few of the items are only relevant when using the [Border Town Burning](../border-town-burning.md#border-town-burning) campaign rules.

The [price chart](spoils-of-war.md#price-chart) at the end of this section lists the prices and rarity values when playing battles in [the borderlands](../border-town-burning.md#the-borderlands) of Cathay, the [Mountains of Mourn](perilous-phenomenon.md#mountains-of-mourn), [the Steppes](perilous-phenomenon.md#the-steppes) and [the Northern Wastes](forces-of-nature.md#the-northern-wastes). Use this [price chart](spoils-of-war.md#price-chart) instead of the original one from Mordheim rulebook when playing a [Border Town Burning](../border-town-burning.md#border-town-burning) campaign.
