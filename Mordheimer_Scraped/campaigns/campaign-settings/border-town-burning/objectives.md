# Objectives (Border Town Burning)

Warbands in [the Cathayan borderlands](forces-of-nature.md#the-cathayan-borderlands) are wrestling for power, striving for fame & fortune or struggling for survival. Each has its own agenda. Objectives are used to determine goals that players work toward achieving throughout the course of a campaign.

It does not matter whether there are [Marauders of Chaos](appendix/questions-and-answers.md#marauders-of-chaos) and Battle Monks warbands in your campaign or not. Neither is it required that any player fights for the forces of good (or vice versa). [Border Town Burning](../border-town-burning.md#border-town-burning) [campaigns](../../campaigns-overview.md#campaigns) can be played with all concerned infighting – exacting works of evil or all disputing law & order. Of course it is generally more fun if roles are distributed evenly but it hardly matters if they are not.

## choosing an objective

Depending on the warband type each warband has its own objective that it aims to complete throughout the campaign. This objective is determined at the beginning of the campaign. All players may always have complete insight concerning other players’ objectives. There are six objectives being introduced in the [Border Town Burning](../border-town-burning.md#border-town-burning) supplement.

A warband may switch to another legal objective in between battles. However, this results in a penalty of losing –D6 [campaign points](scenarios/blockade.md#campaign-points).

## campaign achievements

Mark a check box for every Campaign Point (CP) the warband gets – similar to gaining [Experience](../../experience.md#experience) points. Whenever you reach a thick-bordered box check the Achievement chart for the Objective to see what happens.

You can find an adapted warband roster with a field for tracking your [Campaign Points](scenarios/blockade.md#campaign-points) at the back of this supplement or you can download one from our website.

If [campaign points](scenarios/blockade.md#campaign-points) are lost for any reason, then this has no bearing on [campaign achievements](objectives.md#campaign-achievements) already reached. Any lost [campaign points](scenarios/blockade.md#campaign-points) must be regained before a warband can progress to its next campaign achievement.

## starting over

When a player chooses to disband his warband, their progress and achievements are lost along with the warband members. He then selects a new objective for his new warband just like at the beginning of the campaign.

To make up for the time lost (similar to the underdog bonus for lesser [warband rating](../../campaigns-overview.md#warband-rating)) the warband immediately gains an amount of [campaign progress points](scenarios/the-horde-approaches.md#campaign-progress-points) equal to the next lowest amount of the other participating warbands minus D3.

*Example: A Reiklanders warband is reformed. Other warbands participating in the campaign are [Marauders of Chaos](appendix/questions-and-answers.md#marauders-of-chaos) ([The Scion of Chaos](appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-scion-of-chaos): 12 CP), Battle Monks ([The Celestial Protectorate](appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-celestial-protectorate): 8 CP) and [Orcs & Goblins](warbands-and-hirelings.md#orcs--goblins) ([The Scourge of the Realm](objectives.md#the-scourge-of-the-realm): 9 CP). The Reiklanders player rolls a D3, which scores a 2. Thus the mercenaries starts with 8 – 2 = 6 CP.*

## winning the campaign

As soon as a warband fulfils its objective they win the campaign. An objective is complete when all check boxes for the objective are filled and any further requirements are met.

## The Scion of Chaos

*The darkest of tribes are seeking four artefacts of Chaos. If one Chieftain proves the more skilful leader in finding their whereabouts, then followers of Chaos shall flock to his banner. As the aspirant’s warband grows to become a horde he shall wreak destruction and spread Chaos across the world. The Chieftain may even ascend to become Chosen of Chaos.*

**Warbands:** [Marauders of Chaos](appendix/questions-and-answers.md#marauders-of-chaos), the Possessed, Beastmen Raiders and Norse Explorers may choose this objective.

**No Alliance with:** [The Scourge of the Realm](objectives.md#the-scourge-of-the-realm), [The Celestial Protectorate](appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-celestial-protectorate)

### Objective progress

**+1 Playing a Scenario.** Whenever the warband plays a scenario it gets +1 CP.<br> **+1 Winning a Scenario.** Whenever the warband wins a scenario it gets +1 CP.<br> **+D6 Prophecy Fulfilled.** The warband gets +D6 CP for every Chaos Artefact it can get hold of. Note that each bonus applies only once per artefact.<br> **–D3 Prophecy Foiled.** The warband loses –D3 CP for every Chaos Artefact which falls into the hands of an enemy warband. Note that each bonus applies only once per artefact.<br> **+1 Rival Out Of Action.** The warband gets +1 CP for taking the leader of a warband with [the Scourge of the Realm](objectives.md#the-scourge-of-the-realm) objective or the leader of another warband with [the Scion of Chaos](appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-scion-of-chaos) objective out of action.

## The Scourge of the Realm

*Rumours have spread that the hordes of Chaos are gathering to be led against Cathay – by an aspiring [Champion of Chaos](beasts-of-the-east.md#champion-of-chaos). This warrior is said to be the fiercest and most skilful fighter in [the Northern Wastes](forces-of-nature.md#the-northern-wastes) who can best any man from the Old World. The very notion is an insult because everyone knows that no one poses a greater threat than [the scourge of the realm](objectives.md#the-scourge-of-the-realm)!*

**Warbands:** Maneaters, [Orcs & Goblins](warbands-and-hirelings.md#orcs--goblins), Chaos Dwarfs, Dark Elves, Undead warbands and Skaven warbands may choose this objective.

**No Alliance with:** [The Scion of Chaos](appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-scion-of-chaos)

### Objective progress

**+1 Playing a Scenario.** Whenever the warband plays a scenario it gets +1 CP.<br> **+1 Winning a Scenario.** If the warband wins a battle it gets +1 CP.<br> **+1 Traces to Chaos.** Whenever the warband encounters a warband or non-player group of Marauders (Chaos Marauders, Norse, Chaos Warriors) or Beastmen (Ungors, Gors, Bestigors, Centigors or Minotaurs), roll a D6: 1-3 nothing happens, 4-5 the leader is subject to [frenzy](../../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#frenzy) for this battle, 6 the warband gets +1 CP [after the battle](../../scenarios/other/mordheims-burning.md#after-the-battle).<br> **+1 Accomplished Leader.** The warband gets +1 CP whenever the leader's total [Experience](../../experience.md#experience) reaches the following: 30, 45, 60, 75 and 90. Note that each bonus applies only once per warband.<br> **+1 Scion Out Of Action.** The warband gets +1 CP for taking the leader of a warband with [the Scion of Chaos](appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-scion-of-chaos) objective out of action.

### Achievements

| CP | Achievement |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | **I am the Scourge**: The warband's leader hates every other leader with [the Scion of Chaos](appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-scion-of-chaos) objective. He must charge them when they are in reach. |
| 10 | **Remarkable Duellist**: The warband’s leader is a well-trained fighter. When using a weapon that has a [Parry](../../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#parry) [special rule](../../scenarios/town-cryer/gathering-of-the-horde.md#special-rule), he parries successfully if he beats or matches his opponents highest ‘to hit’ roll, not just if he beats the roll. |
| 15 | **Irrepressible Hunger**: The warband’s leader is able to take on several opponents at once. If he fights against more than one enemy at a time, he gains one extra Attack per enemy model that is not [knocked down](../../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down) or [stunned](../../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned) in each [hand-to-hand combat](../../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#who-can-fight) phase (e.g. +3 Attacks while fighting three enemy models). In addition, the warrior is immune to ‘[All Alone](../../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#all-alone)’ tests while fighting Marauders or Beastmen. Note that he must always assign at least one Attack to each enemy model in [close combat](../../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat). |
| 20 | **Furious Charge**: The warband’s leader gets a +1 bonus on his ‘to hit’ rolls in the first turn of a [hand-to-hand combat](../../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#who-can-fight) when charging. |
| 25 | **Furious Assault**: The warband’s Heroes get a +1 bonus on their ‘to hit’ rolls in the first turn of a [hand-to-hand combat](../../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#who-can-fight) when charging. |
| 35 | **Infamy**: The warband’s Heroes each cause [fear](../../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#fear) in enemy models. |
| 40 | **Duel to the Death**: The warband has found [the Scion of Chaos](appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-scion-of-chaos). From now on whenever the warband’s leader and the enemy chieftain are moved into base contact [the scenario](../../scenarios/archive-pestilen/the-forbidden-square.md#the-scenario) “ends” and they fight a duel to death instead. All other models can be removed from the board.<br>Resolve the combat as normal, using rules for [knocked down](../../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down), [stunned](../../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned) or out of action as usual (remember which turn it is). Note that Old battle wounds do not work at this point. After applying the Serious Injury the model counts as [knocked down](../../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down) and the duel continues until one of them dies permanently.<br>Note that neither side may escape from combat willingly. The only way to leave the duel without killing the opposing model is at the beginning of the first turn after a Serious Injury has been applied to the model (after the [Recovery phase](../../../core-rules/rules/recovery.md#recovery-phase) but before the [Shooting phase](../../../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#who-can-shoot)) and only if he is neither [knocked down](../../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down) nor [stunned](../../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned). If one side chooses to end the duel this way, their warband counts as having failed a [Rout test](../../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#the-rout-test) and they lose the game (but not the campaign).<br>If [the Scion of Chaos](appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-scion-of-chaos) is killed, this warband wins the campaign. If the Scion wins the duel, his warband wins the current scenario. The defeated warband must determine a new leader and try to initiate another duel in the next scenario.<br>If there is no warband with [the Scion of Chaos](appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-scion-of-chaos) objective participating in the campaign, use the rules for a Chaos Champion from the Bestiary. As soon as the last check box is filled and after the next battle in which the contender wasn’t taken out of action, let them fight. If there is more than one warband with [the Scion of Chaos](appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-scion-of-chaos) objective the Scourge has to fight the leader from the warband with the most CPs. On a tie compare the warband ratings. |

## The Celestial Protectorate

*Superstitious elderly peasant folk of [the borderlands](../border-town-burning.md#the-borderlands) tell all manner of far-fetched stories gathered from ancient scrolls and some of these tales from old manuscripts they call prophecies. That a saviour shall rise against the tide of darkness, that foreigners shall come to the aid of civil border dwelling folk, that the forces of light can turn back the tide of darkness and win the day.*

**Warbands:** [Battle Monks of Cathay](appendix/questions-and-answers.md#battle-monks-of-cathay), Shadow Warriors, [Sisters of Sigmar](warbands-and-hirelings.md#sisters-of-sigmar), [Witch Hunters](warbands-and-hirelings.md#witch-hunters), Maneaters, Pit Fighters, Dwarf warbands and Mercenary warbands may choose this objective.

**No Alliance with:** [The Scion of Chaos](appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-scion-of-chaos)

### Objective progress

**+1 Playing a Scenario.** Whenever the warband plays a scenario it gets +1 CP.<br> **+D3 Emissary's Report.** After a won battle if [the Emissary](appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-emissary) was not taken out of action he may miss the next battle (determine new leader for that battle) to make a report in the border town thus gaining +D3 CP. Because of the long journey [the Emissary](appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-emissary) may not search for rare items, [Dramatis Personae](../../dramatis-personae.md#dramatis-personae), etc. It would be too risky for [the Emissary](appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-emissary) to take the [Chaos Artefacts](appendix/advanced-rules.md#chaos-artefacts) with him so they must remain with another Hero from the warband while he makes a report.^✏️<br> **+D6 Chaos Artefact Found.** The warband gets +D6 CP for every Chaos Artefact it can get hold of. Note that the bonus applies only once per artefact.<br> **+1 Chieftain Out Of Action.** The warband gets +1 CP for taking the leader of the following warbands out of action: Marauders, Beastmen and Norse.<br> **+D3 Dead Servant to Chaos.** Whenever a Hero of a Marauder, Beastman, Norse, Carnival of Chaos, Chaos Dwarf or Possessed warband taken out of action by the warband dies permanently after a battle the warband gets +D3 CP.

After every scenario they fought in [the Cathayan borderlands](forces-of-nature.md#the-cathayan-borderlands) the warband counts as one size category smaller than they actually are. ## The Lure of Fortune

*Black market traders moving ‘hot’ product, registered merchant houses boasting opulence in quantity, and cutthroat denizens who crawl from their smugglers' haunts in the dead of the night to solicit the latest in contraband. Tilean Merchant Princes and Strigany peddlers are likeminded enough to recognise the potential for profit that lies at the end of [the Silk Road](../border-town-burning.md#the-silk-road)..*

**Warbands:** Merchant Caravans, Maneaters, Chaos Dwarfs*, Norse Explorers, Shadow Warriors, Dwarf warbands, Pirate warbands, and Mercenary warbands may choose this objective.

**No Alliance with:** –

### Objective progress

**+1 Playing a Scenario.** Whenever the warband plays a scenario it gets +1 CP.<br> **+1 Winning a Scenario.** If the warband won the battle, it gets +1 CP.<br> **+D6 Enterprise Expanded.** Whenever the warband introduces an additional coach or wagon to its land train during the campaign, it gets +D6 CP. Replacing destroyed or lost wagons does not count.<br> **+1 Riches Accumulated.** The warband gets +1 CP whenever their amount of stored Cathayan Silk items reaches the following: 3, 6, 9, and 12. Note that each bonus applies only once per warband.<br> **+D3 Chaos Artefact Found.** The warband gets +D3 CP for every Chaos Artefact it can get hold of. Note that the bonus applies only once per artefact.

## The Silent Threat

*Most honourable warriors would choose to prove their might in arms while others prefer to hire sell-[swords](../../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword) or goad mercenary thugs into getting the job done for them. In the twisted reality of a fiendish mastermind with little or no conscience, there are many other ways to find a means to an end. What might start off as an innocent bit of eavesdropping might quickly escalate? Before you know it you have minions creeping around in the still of the night slitting the throats of enemies. Acts of dishonour such as petty theft and doping become increasingly more common upon forming a cabal and rightly so if they’re for good reason and you’re not getting caught! Power attained in cultism through underground larceny and secret plotting is more than a match for all but the mightiest of arms.*

**Warbands:** Carnival of Chaos, The Possessed, Dark Elves, Skaven warbands, Undead warbands and Mercenary warbands may choose this objective.<br> **No Alliance with:** [The Celestial Protectorate](appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-celestial-protectorate), [The Damned Shall Burn!](objectives.md#the-damned-shall-burn)

### Objective progress

**+1 Playing a Scenario.** Whenever the warband plays a scenario it gets +1 CP.<br> **+1 Winning a Scenario.** If the warband won the battle, it gets +1 CP unless [the scenario](../../scenarios/archive-pestilen/the-forbidden-square.md#the-scenario) played was “The Heist”.<br> **+D3 Winning “The Heist” Scenario.** If the warband won this special scenario it gets +D3 CP.<br> **+D3 Target Eliminated.** Whenever an opposing Hero is taken out of action by a warband member with none of his allies around within 6" the warband gets +D3 CP.<br> **+1 Poisoned Out Of Action.** The warband gets +1 CP for taking a Hero of any warband out of action using poisoned weapons^❓ unless the Hero dies.<br> **+D3 Death by Poison.** Whenever a Hero that was poisoned went out of action and dies permanently after a battle, the warband gets +D3 CP instead of +1 CP.

### Achievements

| CP | Achievement |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | **Saboteurs**: [Black Lotus](../../../weapons-armour/miscellaneous-equipment.md#black-lotus), [Dark Venom](../../../weapons-armour/miscellaneous-equipment.md#dark-venom), Spider Spittle, and Manticore Spoor become less difficult to acquire. ## The Damned Shall Burn!

*Sometimes a fanatical group of [Witch Hunters](warbands-and-hirelings.md#witch-hunters) or similarly self-proclaimed executors of their gods will, choose to travel north to persecute heretics. The zealous will do battle with ‘old night’ itself to pursue their ultimate goal of eradicating evildoers once and for all. Whether it comes in the form of a supernatural menace or a human child touched by the Dark Gods, it is of no consequence for all shall burn.*

**Warbands:** [Witch Hunters](warbands-and-hirelings.md#witch-hunters), [Sisters of Sigmar](warbands-and-hirelings.md#sisters-of-sigmar), Amazons* and Lizardmen* may choose this objective.<br> **No Alliance with:** All Chaos and Undead warbands

### Objective progress

**+1 Playing a Scenario.** Whenever the warband plays a scenario it gets +1 CP.<br> **+1 Winning a Scenario.** If the warband won the battle, it gets +1 CP.<br> **+1 Chieftain Out Of Action.** The warband gets +1 CP for taking the leader of a Chaos ([Marauders of Chaos](appendix/questions-and-answers.md#marauders-of-chaos), Beastmen, Carnival of Chaos, etc.) or Undead (The Undead, Restless Dead, etc.) warband out of action.<br> **+1 Slain Heretic.** Whenever a Hero of any Chaos or Undead warband taken out of action by the warband dies permanently after a battle the warband gets +1 CP.<br> **+1 Foiled Prophecy.** The warband gets +1 CP for every Chaos Artefact it can wrest from the clutches of [The Scion of Chaos](appendix/questions-and-answers.md#the-scion-of-chaos). Note that each bonus applies only once per artefact.
