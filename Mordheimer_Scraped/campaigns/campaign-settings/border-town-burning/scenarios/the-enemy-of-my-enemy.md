# Scenario 8: The Enemy of My Enemy

When a party of warriors is found to be growing too strong even bitter enemies may bury the hatchet for a short while to gang up on the mutual threat. Even in alliance they never truly trust each other. After all there is no proof for either side that they are not walking straight into a trap.

## Terrain

The player who chose this scenario may also choose the territory where it is set. Each player takes it in turn to place a piece of terrain, either a ruined building, forest section, or other features appropriate for the region. We suggest that the terrain is [set up](../../../scenarios/town-cryer/the-hunters-become-the-hunted.md#set-up) within an area roughly 4' x 4'.

## Warbands

This is a multiplayer scenario and uses the rules from the “Chaos in the Streets” article (see *Mordheim Annual 2002*). [The scenario](../../../scenarios/archive-pestilen/the-forbidden-square.md#the-scenario) requires three warbands. The player who chose this scenario may choose which warband is the defender and which two are the attackers.

The defender deploys first within 8" of one table edge. Then the attacker with the lower [warband rating](../../../campaigns-overview.md#warband-rating) sets up within 8" of the opposite edge. Finally the second attacker deploys in the same area as the first attacker.

## Starting the Game

The attacker with the least [warband rating](../../../campaigns-overview.md#warband-rating) takes the first turn, followed by the defending player and finally the second attacker.

## Special Rules

The attacking warbands are paired up from the beginning of the game. They are not allowed to shoot missiles at, attack or cast harmful spells on each other due to their alliance. Any attacker can have their warband leader take a [Leadership test](../../../../core-rules/rules/characteristics.md#leadership-tests) at the beginning of their turn to ignore the alliance. If the leader is out of action, test on the next highest Leadership for a warband member. If the test is passed, the player may attack his partner this turn and if this happens it immediately breaks the alliance. Once the initial alliance has ended, further alliances are formed and broken following the normal multiplayer rules.

Note that the alliance restrictions from the warband Objectives are ignored for the alliance described above. They apply only after the attacker’s initial alliance has been broken.

## Ending the Game

The game ends when all warbands except one fail their [Rout test](../../../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#the-rout-test). The routers automatically lose. An alliance of two or more warbands may choose to share the victory and end the game.

## Experience

- **+1 Survives** : If a Hero or a Henchman group survives the battle they gain +1 [Experience](../../../experience.md#experience).
- **+1 Winning Leader** : The leader of the winning warband gains +1 [Experience](../../../experience.md#experience).
- **+1 Per Enemy Out of Action** : Any Hero earns +1 [Experience](../../../experience.md#experience) for each enemy he puts out of action.
