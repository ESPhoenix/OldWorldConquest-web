# Lesser Artefact Table (Border Town Burning)

| 2D6 Roll | Artefact | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2-5 | Magical Scroll | A Hero uncovers a magical scroll from the esteemed colleges of magic. Roll another D6 to determine the type of scroll. Can be read during the [shooting phase](../../../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#who-can-shoot) and crumbles after use.<br>**1 - Inferno Scroll:** Burns every model within 3" with a Strength 4 fire hit and sets them on fire on a 4+. The reader suffers a S2 hit. <br>**2 - Jade Scroll:** A friendly model within 6" restores all lost wounds and recovers from being [knocked down](../../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down) or [stunned](../../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned). <br>**3 - Blizzard Scroll:** Causes heavy snowfall and strong wind for D3+1 turns. If it was raining, apply Heavy Rain after the effect ends. <br>**4 - Sunlight Scroll:** Enemies suffer an additional -1 ‘to hit’ penalty this turn. Undead and possessed models take a S2 hit with no [armour](../../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour) saves allowed. Also negates adverse weather effects for [the turn](../../../core-rules/rules/the-turn.md#the-turn). <br>**5 - Amethyst Scroll:** An enemy model within 8" suffers D6 Strength 3 hits. <br>**6 - Metal Scroll:** All models wearing [armour](../../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour) within 10" must pass an Initiative test or be unable to strike in [close combat](../../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat) this turn. |
| 6 | Cloak of Tranquillity | Grants immunity to penalties from [Bitter Cold](forces-of-nature.md#bitter-cold) or ice-based magic and prevents becoming soaked with water. |
| 7 | Skinkskin Boots | Grants the Aquatic [special rule](../../scenarios/town-cryer/gathering-of-the-horde.md#special-rule) and immunity to slipping on Muddy Soil. |
| 8 | Fortune Ring | Allows altering one die roll by +1/-1 per battle. |
| 9 | Steelcloth | Provides +1 to [armour save](../../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#armour) when worn under normal [armour](../../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour), or a 6+ save on its own. Destroyed if the wearer is set on fire. |
| 10 | Ice Gem | During the [shooting phase](../../../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#who-can-shoot), deals D3 Strength 1 hits to an enemy within 8". Prevents the wearer from being set on fire. |
| 11 | Firefly | Acts as a [lantern](../../../weapons-armour/miscellaneous-equipment.md#lantern) and can be used once per battle to fire a Strength 4 searing bolt at an enemy within 12". |
| 12 | Chaos Artefact | Roll a D6: 1-4 Ghartok's Skull, 5 Chaos [Broadsword of Damnation](chaos-artefacts.md#broadsword-of-damnation), 6 [Chaos Dragon](beasts-of-the-east.md#chaos-dragon) Scale [Armour](../../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour). If already found, reroll. |
