# Scenarios (Albion)

Below are four [scenarios](../../scenarios.md#scenarios) to play in the [Albion](../albion.md#albion) setting. Roll a 2D6 on the table below to discover which scenario you are playing. Alternatively, you can play the [scenarios](../../scenarios.md#scenarios) in sequential order to form a narrative campaign.

| **D6 Roll** | **Scenario** |
| --- | --- |
| 2-4 | Play scenario 1: [Death in the Mists](../../scenarios/town-cryer/death-in-the-mists.md) |
| 5-8 | Play scenario 2: [Gift of the Truthsayers](../../scenarios/town-cryer/gift-of-the-truthsayers.md) |
| 9-12 | Play scenario 3: [The Ogham Stones](../../scenarios/town-cryer/the-ogham-stones.md) |

## Experience

Unless noted, the following [experience](../../experience.md#experience) points modifiers apply to all [scenarios](../../scenarios.md#scenarios):

- **+1 Survives:** If a Hero or Henchman group survives the battle, they gain +1 [Experience](../../experience.md#experience).
- **+1 Winning Leader:** The leader of the winning warband gains +1 [Experience](../../experience.md#experience).
- **+1 Per Enemy Out of Action:** Any Hero earns +1 [Experience](../../experience.md#experience) for each enemy they put out of action.
