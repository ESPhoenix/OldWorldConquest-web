# Scenarios (Empire in Flames)

Use these Scenario tables instead of the one on page 126 of the rulebook. There is a separate table for [multi-player scenarios](scenarios.md#multi-player-scenarios). As usual, the winner of a scenario gets to roll one more Exploration dice than normal. Roll 2D6 to determine which scenario to play. Obviously, terrain should be [set up](../../scenarios/town-cryer/the-hunters-become-the-hunted.md#set-up) that should reflect the wilderness nature of Empire in Flames (see the new Empire in Flames [scenarios](../../scenarios.md#scenarios) for an idea of what sort of terrain to [set up](../../scenarios/town-cryer/the-hunters-become-the-hunted.md#set-up)).
