# New Equipment (Empire in Flames)

While *Empire in Flames* is a new setting for *Mordheim* games, most of the equipment (including prices and rarities) remains the same... this is still within the Empire after all! However, there are a few [new items](../../scenarios/town-cryer/lost-temple-of-the-slann.md#new-items) of equipment to be had. These are detailed below.

## Lock Picks

**Rare 8**<br> **Cost:** 15 GC

A standard piece of kit for less scrupulous characters. A set of [lock picks](new-equipment.md#lock-picks) may be used by those who rely more on skill-at-arms and speed of thought than brute strength to open doors that others have secured. A model equipped with a set of [lock picks](new-equipment.md#lock-picks) may make his test to open doors on his Initiative rather than his Strength characteristic if he wishes. This is done at the end of his [Movement phase](../../../core-rules/rules/movement.md#moving) as if the model were ripping the door off its hinges, though he uses his Initiative rather than Strength, there is no -1 modifier, and there is no chance that the door is too damaged to be locked again later.

## Torch

**Common**<br> **Cost:** 2 GC

Warriors lacking the funds for a [lantern](../../../weapons-armour/miscellaneous-equipment.md#lantern) may have to make do with torches. Torches act exactly as [lanterns](../../../weapons-armour/miscellaneous-equipment.md#lantern), adding +4" to the range the model may spot hidden enemies, but have a few other special rules as well. A torch will only last one game. A model armed with a torch counts as causing [fear](../../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#fear) in animals (Hunting Dogs, all riding steeds, Bears, Wolves, etc.), and may use a torch as a makeshift [club](../../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#hammer-staff-mace-or-club). When used in combat, a torch is treated as a normal [club](../../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#hammer-staff-mace-or-club), though with a -1 to hit modifier. Any models that have a Regeneration [special rule](../../scenarios/town-cryer/gathering-of-the-horde.md#special-rule) (like Trolls) will not be able to regenerate wounds caused by a torch during the battle.

*Torches may also cause buildings to catch fire – see ‘Let the Damned Burn’, from Town Cryer 8.*

## Barding

**Rare 11**<br> **Cost:** 30 GC

[Barding](../../../weapons-armour/armour.md#barding) is [armour](../../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour) for a horse in the same way that light and [heavy armour](../../../weapons-armour/armour.md#heavy-armour) is for a human. It covers the mount’s hide and, in some cases, the head. A model mounted on a barded horse receives an additional +1 [Armour save](../../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#armour) (+2 instead of +1 for being mounted). In addition, a mount wearing [barding](../../../weapons-armour/armour.md#barding) will only be killed on a D6 roll of a 1 if the model is taken out of action.<br> *(Warhorses only).*

## Horseman’s Hammer

**Rare 10**<br> **Cost:** 12 GC

This is a great [hammer](../../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#hammer-staff-mace-or-club) similar to the ones used by the Knights of the White Wolf. Far too bulky to use in one hand, a [horseman’s hammer](new-equipment.md#horsemans-hammer) is best suited to mounted combat, when the impetus of the horse may be used to add to the power of the weapon.

**Range:** [Close Combat](../../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat)<br> **Strength:** As user +1

#### Special Rules:

**Two-Handed**: A model armed with a [horseman’s hammer](new-equipment.md#horsemans-hammer) may not use a [shield](../../../weapons-armour/armour.md#shield), [buckler](../../../weapons-armour/armour.md#buckler), or additional weapon in [close combat](../../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat). If the model is equipped with a [shield](../../../weapons-armour/armour.md#shield), he will still get a +1 bonus to his [Armour save](../../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#armour) against [shooting](../../../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#shooting).

**Cavalry Charge**: A model armed with a [horseman’s hammer](new-equipment.md#horsemans-hammer) may use the speed of his charge to increase the might of his attacks. A model on a steed with a [horseman’s hammer](new-equipment.md#horsemans-hammer) gains a further +1 Strength bonus when he charges. This bonus only applies for that turn.
