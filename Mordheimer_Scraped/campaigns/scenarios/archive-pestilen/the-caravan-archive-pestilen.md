# 105. The Caravan

**Source:** Archive Pestilen [PDF](https://broheim.net/downloads/scenarios/thecaravanalt.pdf)<br> **Setting:** Mordheim<br> **Author:** Unknown<br> **Notes:** Guard or plunder a caravan of gold before it gets to the other side. (note: we played this four times and found it very hard for the defending player. We opted to make [the merchant](the-caravan-archive-pestilen.md#the-merchant) T4, give him a [shield](../../../weapons-armour/armour.md#shield), play the "lucky bastard" and "stop token" variants and rule that the defenders will not fail a [rout test](../../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#the-rout-test) except voluntarily.)

*The lure of wyrdstone draws all kinds to Mordheim. A wealthy merchant and his hired guards are travelling through the city, bearing a cargo of wyrdstone. They're a tempting target for robbery.*

## Terrain

[Set up](../town-cryer/the-hunters-become-the-hunted.md#set-up) all the buildings in a single street with no outlets, similar to the "Street Fight" scenario in the Mordheim rulebook. Place [the merchant](the-caravan-archive-pestilen.md#the-merchant) model at one end of the street, facing towards the other end.

## Warbands

Both players roll a D6, whoever rolls highest chooses whether to guard [the merchant](the-caravan-archive-pestilen.md#the-merchant) or try to rob him. The guarding player sets up first within 6" of the caravan. The robbers may [set up](../town-cryer/the-hunters-become-the-hunted.md#set-up) anywhere on the street as long as all of his models are at least 14" from the caravan and its guards.

## The Merchant

| Profile | M | WS | BS | S | T | W | I | A | Ld |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | Special | 2 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 1 | 3 | 1 | 10 |

At the beginning of each player's [movement phase](../../../core-rules/rules/movement.md#moving), move [the Merchant](the-caravan-archive-pestilen.md#the-merchant) D3" inches along the road. His mind is occupied with the hefty profit he'll take for his cargo, and he wants to reach a suitable buyer as soon as possible. He takes the easiest path, scorning [cover](../../../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#cover) and ignoring attackers (Note that [the merchant](the-caravan-archive-pestilen.md#the-merchant) moves forward even if he was engaged in [close combat](../../../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat) the previous turn). He never charges other models and passes all psychology tests automatically. The only way to stop his [movement](../../../core-rules/rules/movement.md#movement) is to knock him down, stun him, or take him out of action.

[The Merchant](the-caravan-archive-pestilen.md#the-merchant) wears no [armour](../../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour) and is armed with a single [dagger](../../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#dagger).

## The Cargo

[The merchant](the-caravan-archive-pestilen.md#the-merchant) carries a small chest that contains D3+1 shards of wyrdstone. If he is taken out of action, place the appropriate number of [wyrdstone counters](the-forbidden-square.md#wyrdstone-counters) in the space he used to occupy. A warrior may pick up the wyrdstone by moving into contact with it.

## Starting the Game

The robbing warband takes the first move.

## Ending the Game

- If one warband fails a [rout test](../../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#the-rout-test), the game ends, and the routing warband wins.
- If [the Merchant](the-caravan-archive-pestilen.md#the-merchant) and his cargo make it to within 2" of the opposite table edge, the guards win.
- If [the Merchant](the-caravan-archive-pestilen.md#the-merchant) is taken out of action but the guards carry his cargo to within 2" of the opposite table edge, the game ends in a tie. Neither side receives [experience](../../experience.md#experience) points for victory.
- If the robbers steal [the cargo](the-caravan-archive-pestilen.md#the-cargo) and carry it to within 2" of any table edge, they win the battle.

## Wyrdstone

If your warband ends the game in possession of [the cargo](the-caravan-archive-pestilen.md#the-cargo), it may keep all of the wyrdstone. If [the merchant](the-caravan-archive-pestilen.md#the-merchant) successfully keeps [the cargo](the-caravan-archive-pestilen.md#the-cargo), the guarding warband receives 1 shard plus D6x5 gold crowns for their trouble.

## Experience

- **+1 Survives:** If a hero or henchman group survives the battle, they receive 1 [experience](../../experience.md#experience) point.
- **+1 Winning Leader:** The leader of the winning warband receives 1 [experience](../../experience.md#experience) point.
- **+1 Cargo:** If a hero or henchman is carrying [the cargo](the-caravan-archive-pestilen.md#the-cargo) at the end of the battle, he receives 1 [experience](../../experience.md#experience) point.
- **+1 Per Enemy Out of Action:** Any hero gains 1 [experience](../../experience.md#experience) point for every enemy he puts out of action.

### "Lucky Fool" Variant

[The Merchant](the-caravan-archive-pestilen.md#the-merchant) possesses incredible luck and ignores the first wound he suffers in every turn. This is not a "[lucky charm](../../../weapons-armour/miscellaneous-equipment.md#lucky-charm)" and may not pass to any other character.

### "Two's a Crowd" Variant

In this variant, [the merchant](the-caravan-archive-pestilen.md#the-merchant) has his own guards, and both players are trying to rob him. The Caravan contains a number of guards chosen by a D6 roll from the table below:

- **1-3:** 6 Mercenary Warriors with [sword](../../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword) and [shield](../../../weapons-armour/armour.md#shield)
- **4:** 4 Mercenary Warriors with [light armour](../../../weapons-armour/armour.md#light-armour) and 2 axes or [hammers](../../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#hammer-staff-mace-or-club)
- **5:** 4 Mercenary Warriors with [light armour](../../../weapons-armour/armour.md#light-armour) and 2-handed weapons
- **6:** 4 special henchmen (Mercenary Swordsmen, Beastmen, Sigmarite Sisters, Verminkin, etc) with 1 piece of [armour](../../../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour) and an appropriate weapon.

When not in combat, the guards do their best to keep up with [the merchant](the-caravan-archive-pestilen.md#the-merchant). They will attack any warband that obstructs their path or attacks the caravan, charging the nearest model and passing all necessary psychology tests. Unlike [the Merchant](the-caravan-archive-pestilen.md#the-merchant), guards may not break off combat to continue on their journey. Warbands may [set up](../town-cryer/the-hunters-become-the-hunted.md#set-up) anywhere which is both 14" from the Caravan and 6" away from each enemy model. The game ends when one warband carries [the cargo](the-caravan-archive-pestilen.md#the-cargo) to within 2" of any table edge. If [the merchant](the-caravan-archive-pestilen.md#the-merchant) makes it to within 2" of the opposite table edge, the game ends in a tie, and neither side may claim [experience](../../experience.md#experience) points for victory.

### "Greedy Bastard" Variant

Warriors may steal [the Merchant](the-caravan-archive-pestilen.md#the-merchant)'s cargo by moving into contact with him when he is [knocked down](../../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down) or [stunned](../../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned). If they do so, he will follow the carrier, moving D6" in every [movement phase](../../../core-rules/rules/movement.md#moving). If he moves into the carrier, [the Merchant](the-caravan-archive-pestilen.md#the-merchant) counts as charging and attacks with both [hatred](../../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#hatred) and [frenzy](../../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#frenzy). He will ignore other models unless they block his path entirely.

### "A Friend in the Business" Variant

This variant is used only in [campaigns](../../campaigns-overview.md#campaigns). [After the battle](../other/mordheims-burning.md#after-the-battle), the caravan guards may have earned a reputation among the city's merchants. If the guards were successful, they will receive a 20% discount on all equipment purchased between this battle and the next. If they were unsuccessful, all equipment will cost 20% more than normal. Alas, fame is fleeting in the city of Mordheim, and these discounts apply only until the start of your next battle. A tie does not affect equipment prices one way or another.

## Suggestions for the Guarding Warband

Specifically, I'm worried about the guarding warband. Their task seems to be exceedingly difficult. Someone suggested I put [the Merchant](the-caravan-archive-pestilen.md#the-merchant) under his guards' control, but I'm pretty much married to the idea of an independent model. Here are a few possible solutions:

1. (My Favorite) Give the guarding player three "stop tokens" (Poker Chips). The guarding player can spend them during any [movement phase](../../../core-rules/rules/movement.md#moving) to delay [the Merchant](the-caravan-archive-pestilen.md#the-merchant). One token will stop [the Merchant](the-caravan-archive-pestilen.md#the-merchant) for a complete turn (2 [movement](../../../core-rules/rules/movement.md#movement) phases). But [the Merchant](the-caravan-archive-pestilen.md#the-merchant) is not a patient man. After three stops, he will brook no further delays and will hurry along the road without stopping.
2. Make the "Lucky Bastard" variant a permanent part of [the scenario](the-forbidden-square.md#the-scenario). [The merchant](the-caravan-archive-pestilen.md#the-merchant) won't move any slower, but his incredible luck will make him much harder to kill.
3. Put him in a slow-moving chariot or palanquin and spread enemy hits among several targets. This was McAber's suggestion. It's a good idea and very appropriate for a merchant. Unfortunately, my gaming [club](../../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#hammer-staff-mace-or-club) doesn't have enough chariots to go around.
