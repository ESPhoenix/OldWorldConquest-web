# 100. Rescue

**Author:** Andrew "Boss Orc"<br> **Source:** Archive Pestilen [PDF](https://broheim.net/downloads/scenarios/rescue.pdf)<br> **Setting:** Mordheim<br> **Notes:** Rescue captive warrior. Defender in centre, attacker on edges.

*Your enemy has captured a member, or members, of your warband and you need to rescue them before they can be sacrificed, turned into slaves, or forced to watch Teletubbies. You choose.*

## Terrain

The center of the board should be [set up](../town-cryer/the-hunters-become-the-hunted.md#set-up) to represent the hide out of the defender's gang. This should only be 1' square at most. The rest of the table can be [set up](../town-cryer/the-hunters-become-the-hunted.md#set-up) normally.

## Warbands:

The defender may nominate one member of the attacker's warband (not the leader) to be their prisoner. The defender must [set up](../town-cryer/the-hunters-become-the-hunted.md#set-up) inside his base and have a warrior in base contact with the captured prisoner at all times. The attacker may [set up](../town-cryer/the-hunters-become-the-hunted.md#set-up) within 8" of the edge of the table.

## Starting the Game:

Each player rolls a D6, the highest scorer goes first.

## Ending the Game:

The battle ends when either the attacker loses half his models or manages to get the prisoner off the board, resulting in a win for the defender or the attacker, respectively. Alternatively, the defender loses three-quarters of his models.

## experience

**+1 Survives.** If a Hero or Henchman group survives the battle they gain +1 [Experience](../../experience.md#experience).<br> **+1 Winning Leader.** The leader of the winning warband gains +1 extra [Experience](../../experience.md#experience).<br> **+1 Per Enemy Out of Action.** Any Hero earns +1 [Experience](../../experience.md#experience) for each enemy model he puts out of action. This includes a Champion who puts the other Champion out of action in Single Combat.
