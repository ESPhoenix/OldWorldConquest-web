# 97. Raid

**Author:** Andrew "Boss Orc"<br> **Source:** Archive Pestilen [PDF](https://broheim.net/downloads/scenarios/raid.pdf)<br> **Setting:** Mordheim<br> **Notes:** Attack on warband's camp. Defender in centre, attacker on edges.

*Not all battles are conducted in proper conditions. Sometimes a warband will try and surprise their rivals when they least expect it, and this most often done while the are settling down for a bit of R-and-R.*

## Terrain

The center of the board should be [set up](../town-cryer/the-hunters-become-the-hunted.md#set-up) to represent the hide out of the defender's gang. This should only be 1' square at most. The rest of the table can be [set up](../town-cryer/the-hunters-become-the-hunted.md#set-up) normally.

## Warbands:

The defender must deploy all his warriors within his base. The attacker may deploy his warriors within 8" of any table edge.

## Starting The Game:

The attacker makes the first turn.

## Ending The Game:

The battle ends when, either the attacker loses half of his models, or the defender loses three-quarters of his models. The other player is the winner.

## experience

**+1 Survives.** If a Hero or Henchman group survives the battle they gain +1 [Experience](../../experience.md#experience).<br> **+1 Winning Leader.** The leader of the winning warband gains +1 extra [Experience](../../experience.md#experience).<br> **+1 Per Enemy Out of Action.** Any Hero earns +1 [Experience](../../experience.md#experience) for each enemy model he puts out of action.
