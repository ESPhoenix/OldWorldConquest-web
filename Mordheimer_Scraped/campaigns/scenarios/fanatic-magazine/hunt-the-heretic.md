# 83. Hunt the Heretic

**Source:** Fanatic Magazine #9 [PDF](https://broheim.net/downloads/scenarios/hunttheheretic.pdf)<br> **Setting:** The Empire<br> **Author:** Nick Kyme<br> **Notes:** Four members of the defenders’ warband attempt to protect a tooled-up Warlock HS from the attackers plus an accompanying Witch [Hunter](../../../skills/standard-skills.md#hunter) Captain. The defenders must roll for reinforcements as the game progresses. Due to their position of defending a central tower, the defenders’ rout threshold is 50%. Last to rout wins. If the Warlock goes Out Of Action, the attackers win. If the Witch [Hunter](../../../skills/standard-skills.md#hunter) goes Out Of Action, the attackers must take an immediate additional [rout test](../../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#the-rout-test).

*The rural provinces of the Empire, like Stirland, are a haven for warlocks, necromancers and other foul creatures. Too easily in the wild and untamed backwaters do dark deeds go unchecked and unchallenged. Such men are hunted though, by men of courage and steel — those who would not sit idly while evil was wrought...*

One warband has been hired to aid a Witch [Hunter](../../../skills/standard-skills.md#hunter) who has tracked down a nefarious Warlock of some local repute. However, the Warlock has allies of his own and the hunt for his head won’t be as straightforward as was first supposed...

## terrain

There is a ruined tower in the centre of the board which represents the Warlock’s lair and the place to which the Witch [Hunter](../../../skills/standard-skills.md#hunter) and his allies have tracked him. Other than that players take it in turns to place a piece of scenery; a rocky outcrop, scrub or anything to represent the wild and distant outskirts of a town or village. The battle is fought over an area roughly 4' x 4.

## set-up

Each player rolls a D6. The highest roll may choose to fight for the Witch [Hunter](../../../skills/standard-skills.md#hunter) or the Warlock. If one warband is overtly ‘evil’ (see the previous scenario) then they will automatically fight for the Warlock, and if a warband is overtly ‘good’ eg, [Witch Hunters](../../campaign-settings/border-town-burning/warbands-and-hirelings.md#witch-hunters), Dwarfs, etc (but not [Sisters of Sigmar](../../campaign-settings/border-town-burning/warbands-and-hirelings.md#sisters-of-sigmar)) then they will fight for the Witch [Hunter](../../../skills/standard-skills.md#hunter). (Note: if you want to use [Sisters of Sigmar](../../campaign-settings/border-town-burning/warbands-and-hirelings.md#sisters-of-sigmar) in this scenario they will fight for the Warlock, having been duped and believing him to be some wandering hedge wizard, wrongly accused. A fact they are all too familiar with).

The Warlock and up to four members of the warband protecting him are [set up](../town-cryer/the-hunters-become-the-hunted.md#set-up) first inside or within 3" of the ruined tower.

The Witch [Hunter](../../../skills/standard-skills.md#hunter) and his allied warband are then [set up](../town-cryer/the-hunters-become-the-hunted.md#set-up) within 8" of any table edge.

## special rules

**Witch [Hunter](../../../skills/standard-skills.md#hunter):** The Witch [Hunter](../../../skills/standard-skills.md#hunter) counts as a Witch [Hunter](../../../skills/standard-skills.md#hunter) Captain. He may use his Leader skill along with the warband’s leader for the [Leadership tests](../../../core-rules/rules/characteristics.md#leadership-tests) of the allied warband. He has +1 Wound, +1 Attack and +1 Leadership. He wears [light armour](../../../weapons-armour/armour.md#light-armour) and carries a [sword](../../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword), a torch (see Empire in Flames page 16) and a brace of [pistols](../../../weapons-armour/blackpowder.md#pistol). He has the skills [Fearsome](../../../skills/standard-skills.md#fearsome) and [Resilient](../../../skills/standard-skills.md#resilient) and also carries a Holy Relic and three vials of [Blessed Water](../../../weapons-armour/miscellaneous-equipment.md#blessed-water).

**Warlock:** The Warlock counts as a Warlock hired [sword](../../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword). He has +1 Wound and +1 Leadership. He carries a [sword](../../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword) and [staff](../../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#hammer-staff-mace-or-club) and has a [Tome of Magic](../../../weapons-armour/miscellaneous-equipment.md#tome-of-magic) and the poison [Black Lotus](../../../weapons-armour/miscellaneous-equipment.md#black-lotus) (which is upon his [sword](../../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword)). He has the skill [Sorcery](../../../skills/standard-skills.md#sorcery) and knows the following three spells from the Lesser Magic list: Fires of U’Zhul, Dread of Aramar and Silver Arrows of Arha.

**The trap is sprung:** The Warlock, knowing the Witch [Hunter](../../../skills/standard-skills.md#hunter) was on his trail, has lured him and his allies into a trap. Starting with the second turn roll a D6 at the start of each of the Warlock player’s turns. On a roll of 4+ the rest of the warband protecting him arrives from any table edge. They may move onto the board as normal, but are unable to charge that turn. On the third turn the reinforcements will arrive on a 3+, the fourth turn a 2+ and on the fifth turn automatically.

**[Rout tests](../../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#the-rout-test):** As it is the Witch [Hunter](../../../skills/standard-skills.md#hunter)’s will that drives the allied warband, they must take an immediate [Rout test](../../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#the-rout-test) if he is taken out of action, regardless of casualties. This is in addition to any normal [Rout tests](../../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#the-rout-test) that need to be taken.

The Warlock’s warband are effectively cornered in the tower and so will only have to take a [Rout test](../../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#the-rout-test) when 50% of the warband have been taken out of action.

## starting the game

Each player rolls a D6 with the player rolling the highest choosing whether to go first or second.

## ending the game

The game ends as soon as one warband fails its [Rout test](../../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#the-rout-test), with that warband losing. The game will end immediately if the Warlock is taken out of action with a win for the Witch [Hunter](../../../skills/standard-skills.md#hunter)’s allied warband.

## experience

- **+1 Survives.** If a Hero or Henchman group survives the battle they gain +1 [Experience](../../experience.md#experience).
- **+1 Winning Leader.** The leader of the winning warband gains +1 [Experience](../../experience.md#experience).
- **+1 Per Enemy *Out of Action*.** Any Hero earns +1 [Experience](../../experience.md#experience) for each enemy he puts out of action.

## reward

If the Witch [Hunter](../../../skills/standard-skills.md#hunter)’s warband win they gain D6x15 gold crowns as payment and D3 vials of [Blessed Water](../../../weapons-armour/miscellaneous-equipment.md#blessed-water).

If the Warlock’s warband win he concocts D3 doses of any poison or drug of their choice.
