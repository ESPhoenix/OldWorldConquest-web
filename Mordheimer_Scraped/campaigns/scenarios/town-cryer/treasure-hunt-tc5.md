# 11. Treasure Hunt

**Source:** Town Cryer #5 [PDF](https://broheim.net/downloads/scenarios/treasurehunt%2C%20streetbrawl.pdf)<br> **Setting:** Mordheim<br> **Author:** Mark Havener<br> **Notes:** Multiplayer. Similar to Wyrdstone Hunt.

*Often, multiple warbands will hear the same rumour about a wyrdstone deposit and decide to explore that section of the city at the same time. A battle often results with the winners taking the lion's share of the shards home with them.*

info

This is a Multiplayer scenario designed to be used with the [Chaos in the Streets multiplayer rules](https://mordheimer.net/docs/optional-rules/multiplayer-games).

## terrain

Each player takes it in turns to place a piece of terrain, either a ruined building, tower or other similar item. We suggest that the terrain is [set up](the-hunters-become-the-hunted.md#set-up) within an area roughly 4'x6'.

## set-up

Once you have placed the terrain, place D3 wyrdstone shards for each warband involved in the game on the tabletop to represent where the shards are.

Each player takes it in turn to place a wyrdstone counter. Each player rolls a D6 to determine who places first, second, third etc. [The counters](../archive-pestilen/the-forbidden-square.md#the-counters) must be placed before deciding which edge [the warbands](../archive-pestilen/the-square-of-the-snake.md#the-warbands) will play from.

Player use the normal warband [set-up](the-caravan.md#set-up) rules for multi-player.

## special rules

Warriors can pick up [the counters](../archive-pestilen/the-forbidden-square.md#the-counters) by simply moving into contact with them. A warrior cannot transfer their wyrdstone to another warrior. If the warrior who is carrying a counter is taken *out of action*, place the counter back on the table where it fell.

## starting the game

Players each roll a D6 to determine who goes first. Play proceeds clockwise around the table from there (based on where players placed their warbands).

## ending the game

The game ends when all warband but one have failed their [Rout test](../../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#the-rout-test). The routers automatically lose. If two or more warbands have allied, they may choose to share the victory and end the game.

## experience

- **+1 Survives.** If a Hero or a Henchman group survives the battle they gain +1 [Experience](../../experience.md#experience).
- **+1 Winning Leader.** The leader of the winning warband gains +1 [Experience](../../experience.md#experience).
- **+1 Per Enemy *Out of Action*.** A Hero earns +1 [Experience](../../experience.md#experience) for each enemy he puts out of action.
- **+1 Per Wyrdstone Counter.** If a Hero or Henchman is carrying a Wyrdstone counter at the end of the battle, they receive +1 [Experience](../../experience.md#experience).

## wyrdstone

Your warriors earn one shard of wyrdstone for each counter in their possession at the end of the battle.
