# 38. Gift of the Truthsayers

**Source:** Town Cryer #15 [PDF](https://broheim.net/downloads/scenarios/deathinthemists%2C%20giftofthetruthsayers%2C%20theoghamstones.pdf)<br> **Setting:** [Albion](../../campaign-settings/albion.md#albion)<br> **Author:** Nicodemus Kyme<br> **Notes:** Scrap over a magic item with random boggy happenings.

*Rumours are abound of a potent artefact, said to be wrought by one of the enigmatic Truthsayers, lost in the depths of the marshes. Naturally such rumours draw warbands eager for plunder like moths to a flame...*

## terrain

In this battle the entire board is fenland terrain with only two or three modest areas of [open ground](../../../core-rules/rules/movement.md#open-ground). In the centre of the board there should be a jutting crag, cairn or some other significant piece of terrain. This is where [the artefact](../../campaign-settings/border-town-burning/scenarios/the-heist.md#the-artefact) lies. Other terrain such as rocks, crags, briars, trees etc..can be placed in a mutually agreeable manner but no buildings may be placed, marshes are not the best places to build a dwelling!

##special rules

**[The Artefact](../../campaign-settings/border-town-burning/scenarios/the-heist.md#the-artefact):** [The artefact](../../campaign-settings/border-town-burning/scenarios/the-heist.md#the-artefact) lies in the crags in the centre of the board the first warrior to reach it may take it. If that warrior is *[stunned](../../../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned)* or taken *out of action* then the attacking player may take [the artefact](../../campaign-settings/border-town-burning/scenarios/the-heist.md#the-artefact) and as such several players may carry [the artefact](../../campaign-settings/border-town-burning/scenarios/the-heist.md#the-artefact) by the end of the game.

## warbands

Each player rolls a dice. Whoever rolls highest chooses who sets up first. The first player then chooses which table edge to [set up](the-hunters-become-the-hunted.md#set-up) on, placing all his warriors within 8" of that edge. His opponent then sets up within 8" of the opposite edge.

## starting the game

Both players roll a D6. The highest scoring player takes the first turn.

## ending the game

The game ends when one warband fails its [Rout test](../../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#the-rout-test). The routers automatically lose.

## experience

- **+1 Survives.** If a Hero or a Henchman group survives the battle they gain +1 [Experience](../../experience.md#experience).
- **+1 Winning Leader.** The leader of the winning warband gains +1 [Experience](../../experience.md#experience).
- **+1 Per Enemy *Out of Action*.** Any Hero earns +1 [Experience](../../experience.md#experience) for each enemy he puts out of action.
- **+2 Artefact.** If a hero is carrying [the artefact](../../campaign-settings/border-town-burning/scenarios/the-heist.md#the-artefact) at the end of the game he earns +2 [experience](../../experience.md#experience) points.

## rewards

The warband in possession of [the artefact](../../campaign-settings/border-town-burning/scenarios/the-heist.md#the-artefact) at the end of the game may examine it to discover its worth. Roll a 2D6 on the table below:

| D6 Roll | Artefact |
| --- | --- |
| 1-4 | [The artefact](../../campaign-settings/border-town-burning/scenarios/the-heist.md#the-artefact) is valuable and worth 5D6 gold coins. |
| 5-6 | [The artefact](../../campaign-settings/border-town-burning/scenarios/the-heist.md#the-artefact) is an enchanted Totem of Light, which renders its bearer immune to psychology and [all alone tests](../../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#all-alone). If given to the warband’s leader it improves his leadership by +1. |
| 7-8 | [The artefact](../../campaign-settings/border-town-burning/scenarios/the-heist.md#the-artefact) is a Silver Sickle, which acts like a normal [sword](../../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword) but increases Weapon Skill by +1 and adds +1 to the user’s Strength vs Daemons, Possessed and Undead. |
| 9 | [The artefact](../../campaign-settings/border-town-burning/scenarios/the-heist.md#the-artefact) is a Talisman of Light bearing the mystical Triskele symbol. It wards hostile magic and will nullify any harmful spell cast at the wearer on a roll of 4+ |
| 10-11 | [The artefact](../../campaign-settings/border-town-burning/scenarios/the-heist.md#the-artefact) is a Tome of the Truthsayers, a book of magic. It enables the user to cast a single randomly determined spell of the Lore of Light once per battle without the need to roll for difficulty. |
| 12 | [The artefact](../../campaign-settings/border-town-burning/scenarios/the-heist.md#the-artefact) is a Vambrace of Silver, which has the power to deflect missiles. Any ranged weapon that hits the wearer will be deflected away harmlessly on a roll of 5+. |
