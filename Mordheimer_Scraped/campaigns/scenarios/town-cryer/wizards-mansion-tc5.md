# 14. The Wizard's Mansion

**Source:** Town Cryer #5 [PDF](https://broheim.net/downloads/scenarios/thewizardmansion.pdf)<br> **Setting:** Mordheim<br> **Author:** Mark Havener<br> **Notes:** Multiplayer. Similar to Skirmish with items at stake.

*Not all of the ruined buildings in the city are ordinary houses. Some notable structures were the abodes of important town leaders or rich merchants. Tales are told of hidden rooms filled with treasure in such homes. [The warbands](../archive-pestilen/the-square-of-the-snake.md#the-warbands) have discovered the location of one such building. Rumours about that the former owner also dabbled in the arcane arts, which may explain why his home was almost untouched by the devasation.*

info

This is a Multiplayer scenario designed to be used with the [Chaos in the Streets multiplayer rules](https://mordheimer.net/docs/optional-rules/multiplayer-games).

## terrain

Each player takes it in turns to place a piece of terrain, either a ruined building, tower or other similar item. We suggest that the terrain is [set up](the-hunters-become-the-hunted.md#set-up) within an area roughly 4'x6'.

The first building should be placed in the center of the table, and the objective of this scenario is to take control of this building.

## set-up

One player is the defender, determined as normal, representing the warband that reached the mansion first. The defender is deployed first inside or within 6" of the objective building. Then the attacking warbands are [set up](the-hunters-become-the-hunted.md#set-up) as normal.

In addition, for each attacking warband beyond the first, the defender rolls once on the table below to determine the [additional equipment](encampment-raid.md#additional-equipment) his warband gets to start with. Each item may only be found once. If the same number is rolled more than once, re-roll. These items represent the findings of the warband thus far and are meant to give the warband a chance against multiple opponents.

| D6 | Item found |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Wooden Man |
| 2 | 1D3 doses of [Mandrake Root](../../../weapons-armour/miscellaneous-equipment.md#mandrake-root) |
| 3 | 1D3 dodes of [Crimson Shade](../../../weapons-armour/miscellaneous-equipment.md#crimson-shade) |
| 4 | [Lucky Charm](../../../weapons-armour/miscellaneous-equipment.md#lucky-charm) |
| 5 | [Holy (Unholy) Relic](../../../weapons-armour/miscellaneous-equipment.md#holy-unholy-relic) |
| 6 | [Cathayan Silk Cloak](../../../weapons-armour/miscellaneous-equipment.md#miscellaneous-equipment) |

See below for the Wooden Man special rules.

## starting the game

Each player rolls a D6. The player rolling the highest has the first turn and order of play proceeds clockwise around the table (based on where players placed their warbands).

## ending the game

The game ends when all warbands but one have failed their [Rout test](../../../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#the-rout-test). The routers automatically lose. If one of more warbands have allied, they may choose to share the victory and end the game.

## experience

- **+1 Survives.** If a Hero or a Henchman group survives the battle they gain +1 [Experience](../../experience.md#experience).
- **+1 Winning Leader.** The leader of the winning warband gains +1 [Experience](../../experience.md#experience).
- **+1 Per Enemy *Out of Action*.** A Hero earns +1 [Experience](../../experience.md#experience) for each enemy he puts out of action.

## wizard's treasure

At the end of the game the winning warband finds all of the items on the table above which were not found before the battle.

In addition, roll on the following chart to see what additional items the warband finds in the mansion. Note that you'll roll for each item separately, apart from the gold crowns, which are always found. For example, you will need to roll a 4+ to find a tomb of magic. Then roll to see if you find the gromril [sword](../../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword) and so on.

| Item | D6 Needed |
| --- | --- |
| 5D6 gc | Automatic |
| D3 gems worth 10 gc each | 5+ |
| [Tome of magic](../../../weapons-armour/miscellaneous-equipment.md#tome-of-magic) | 4+ |
| [Gromril sword](../../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#gromril-weapon) | 5+ |
| Athame | 4+ |
| D3 doses of [healing herbs](../../../weapons-armour/miscellaneous-equipment.md#healing-herbs) | 4+ |
| Dispel scroll | 5+ |

## new items

**Wooden Man:** Use the stats and special rules for a [zombie](https://mordheimer.net/docs/warbands/core-warbands/undead#zombies) with +1S and +1T. The Wooden Man is an artificial construct in the shape of a man but made of wood. It will follow the commands of the defending warband but will not leave the wizard's mansion for any reason, even after the game. The Wooden Man may obviously not be traded.

**Athame:** An Athame is a special silver [dagger](../../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#dagger) used in magical rituals. If used in combat, it will not hold its edge. For the first attack in a game, it will count as a normal [dagger](../../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#dagger). However, for the rest of the game it will count as a [fist](../../../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#fist) attack. The Athame is worth 10 gold crowns if traded.

**Dispel Scroll:** This scroll contains a powerful counterspell. It may be read aloud immediately after an opponent has successfully cast a spell (but before results have been determined) to negate its effects. When used roll a D6. The enemy spell is cancelled on a roll of 4 or more. After one use, the scroll will disintergrate and is useless. It may be traded for 25+2D6.
