# Skills

The Skill lists are used to pick skills earned as advances. Your warrior is restricted to specific Skill lists depending upon his warband type and what kind of warrior he is. Each warband entry includes a list of skills available to the Heroes of that particular warband.

All warbands have their own strengths and weaknesses which relate to the skills available to them. For example, the Possessed are good fighters and very strong, but they are not very academically minded.

Some warbands also have access to a unique Skill list, which is clearly indicated in their entry. Choosing such a warband in the dropdown below will also show their skills.

## Combat Skill s

### Strike to Injure

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

The warrior can land his blows with uncanny accuracy. Add +1 to all injury rolls caused by the model in [hand-to-hand combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#who-can-fight).

### Combat Master

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

The warrior is able to take on several opponents at once. If he fights against more than one enemy at a time, he gains an extra Attack in each [hand-to-hand combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#who-can-fight) phase as long as he is fighting two or more enemy models. In addition, the warrior is immune to '[All Alone](../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#all-alone)' tests.

### Weapons Training

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

A warrior with this skill is adept at using many different weapons. He may use any [hand-to-hand combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#who-can-fight) weapon he comes across, not just those in his equipment options.

### Web of Steel

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

Few can match the ability of this warrior. He fights with great skill, weaving a [web of steel](standard-skills.md#web-of-steel) around him. The model gains +1 to all his rolls on [Critical Hit](../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#critical-hits) tables in [hand-to-hand combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#who-can-fight).

### Expert Swordsman

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

This warrior has been expertly taught in the art of swordsmanship. He may re-roll all missed attacks if he is using a [sword](../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword) in the hand-to-hand phase of [the turn](../core-rules/rules/the-turn.md#the-turn) that he charges. Note that this only applies when they are armed with normal [swords](../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword) or [weeping blades](../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#weeping-blades), and not with doublehanded [swords](../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#sword) or any other weapons.^✏️

### Step Aside

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

The warrior has a natural ability to avoid injury in combat. Each time he suffers a wound in [close combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat) he may make an additional saving throw of 5+. This save is never modified and is taken after all other [armour](../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour) saves.

## Shooting Skill s

### Quick Shot

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

*Only with [bow](../weapons-armour/missile.md#bow) or [crossbow](../weapons-armour/missile.md#crossbow) (not [crossbow pistol](../weapons-armour/missile.md#crossbow-pistol))*

The warrior may shoot twice^❓ per turn with a [bow](../weapons-armour/missile.md#bow) or [crossbow](../weapons-armour/missile.md#crossbow) (but not a [crossbow pistol](../weapons-armour/missile.md#crossbow-pistol)).^✏️

### Pistolier

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

The warrior is an expert at using all kinds of [pistols](../weapons-armour/blackpowder.md#pistol). If he is equipped with a brace of [pistols](../weapons-armour/blackpowder.md#pistol) of any type (including [crossbow pistols](../weapons-armour/missile.md#crossbow-pistol)), he may fire twice in the [Shooting phase](../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#who-can-shoot)^❓ (though note that normal reloading rules apply). If he has a single [pistol](../weapons-armour/blackpowder.md#pistol) then he may fire it in the same turn it was reloaded.^✏️

### Eagle Eyes

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

The warrior's sight is exceptionally keen. He adds +6" to the range of any missile weapon he is using.

### Weapons Expert

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

The warrior has been trained to use some of the more unusual weapons of the known world. He may use any missile weapon he comes across, not just the weapons available from his warband's list.

### Nimble

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

*Cannot be combined with [Quick Shot](standard-skills.md#quick-shot)*

The warrior may move and fire with weapons that are normally only used if the firer has not moved. Note that this skill cannot be combined with the [Quick Shot](standard-skills.md#quick-shot) skill.

### Trick Shooter

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

The warrior can shoot through the tiniest gap without it affecting his aim. He ignores all modifiers for [cover](../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#cover) when using [missile weapons](../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/spoils-of-war.md#missile-weapons).

### Hunter

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

The warrior is an expert at getting his weapon loaded and ready. He may fire each turn with a [handgun](../weapons-armour/blackpowder.md#handgun) or [Hochland long rifle](../warbands/grade-1b/gunnery-school-of-nuln.md#missile-weapons).^✏️

### Knife-Fighter

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

*Cannot be combined with [Quick Shot](standard-skills.md#quick-shot)*

The warrior is an unrivalled expert at using [throwing knives](../weapons-armour/missile.md#throwing-knivesstars) and [throwing stars](../weapons-armour/missile.md#throwing-knivesstars). He can throw a maximum of three of these missiles in his [shooting phase](../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#who-can-shoot) and may divide his shots between any targets within range as he wishes. Note that this skill cannot be combined with the [Quick Shot](standard-skills.md#quick-shot) skill.

## Academic Skill s

### Battle Tongue

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

*Only for leaders, Undead leaders cannot use*

This skill may only be chosen by a leader. The warrior has drilled his warband to follow short barked commands. This increases the range of his Leader ability by 6". Note that Undead leaders may not use this skill.

### Sorcery

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

*Only for spellcasting Heroes, [Sisters of Sigmar](../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/warbands-and-hirelings.md#sisters-of-sigmar) and Warrior-Priests cannot use*

This skill may only be taken by Heroes capable of casting spells. A warrior with this skill gains +1 to his rolls to see whether he can cast spells successfully or not. Note that [Sisters of Sigmar](../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/warbands-and-hirelings.md#sisters-of-sigmar) and Warrior-Priests may not use this skill.

### Streetwise

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

A warrior with this skill has good contacts and knows where to purchase rare items. He may add +2 to the roll that determines his chances of finding such items (see the [Trading section](../campaigns/trading.md)).

### Haggle

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

*Once per [post-battle sequence](../campaigns/campaign-settings/khemri.md#post-battle-sequence), minimum cost 1gc*

The warrior knows all the tricks of bargaining and haggling. He may deduct 2D6 gold crowns from the price of any single item (to a minimum cost of 1gc) once per [post battle sequence](../campaigns/campaigns-overview.md#post-battle-sequence).^✏️

### Arcane Lore

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

*Cannot be taken by [Witch Hunters](../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/warbands-and-hirelings.md#witch-hunters), [Sisters of Sigmar](../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/warbands-and-hirelings.md#sisters-of-sigmar) or Warrior-Priests*

[Witch Hunters](../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/warbands-and-hirelings.md#witch-hunters), [Sisters of Sigmar](../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/warbands-and-hirelings.md#sisters-of-sigmar) and Warrior-Priests may not have this skill. Any warrior with this skill may learn [Lesser Magic](https://mordheimer.net/docs/magic/lesser-magic) if he owns a [Tome of Magic](../weapons-armour/miscellaneous-equipment.md#tome-of-magic).

### Wyrdstone Hunter

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

The warrior has an uncanny ability to find hidden shards of wyrdstone. If a Hero with this skill is searching the ruins in the exploration phase you may re-roll one dice when rolling on the [Exploration chart](../campaigns/campaign-settings/khemri/income.md#exploration-chart). The second result stands.

### Warrior Wizard

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

*Only for spellcasters*

This skill may only be taken by spellcasters. The mental powers of the wizard allow him to wear [armour](../weapons-armour/armour.md#armour) and cast spells.

## Strength Skill s

### Mighty Blow

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

The warrior knows how to use his strength to maximum effect and has a +1 Strength bonus in [close combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat) (excluding [pistols](../weapons-armour/blackpowder.md#pistol)). As his Strength is used for [close combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat) weapons, the bonus applies to all such weapons.

### Pit Fighter

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

The warrior has learned how to fight in enclosed spaces from his time in the dangerous fighting pits of the Empire. He is an expert at fighting in confined areas and adds +1 to his WS and +1 to his Attacks if he is fighting inside buildings or ruins. It's a good idea to define which bits of your terrain collection count as 'buildings or ruins' at the start of a battle to avoid confusion later.^✏️

### Resilient

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

The warrior is covered in battle scars. Deduct -1 Strength from all hits against him in [close combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat). This does not affect [armour save modifiers](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#armour-save-modifiers).

### Fearsome

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

Such is the reputation and physique of the model that he causes [fear](../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#fear) in opposing models.

### Strongman

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

The warrior is capable of great feats of strength. He may use a [double-handed weapon](../weapons-armour/close-combat.md#double-handed-weapon) without the usual penalty of always striking last. Work out order of battle as you would with other weapons.

### Unstoppable Charge

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

When he charges, the warrior is almost impossible to halt. He adds +1 to his Weapon Skill when charging.

## Speed Skill s

### Leap

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

The warrior may [leap](standard-skills.md#leap) D6" in the [movement phase](../core-rules/rules/movement.md#moving) in addition to his normal [movement](../core-rules/rules/movement.md#movement).^❓ He may move and [leap](standard-skills.md#leap), run and [leap](standard-skills.md#leap), or charge and [leap](standard-skills.md#leap), but he can only [leap](standard-skills.md#leap) once per turn. A leaping warrior may jump over opposing man-sized models, including enemies, and obstacles 1" high, without penalty. The [leap](standard-skills.md#leap) may also be used to [leap](standard-skills.md#leap) over gaps, but in this case you must commit the warrior to making the [leap](standard-skills.md#leap) before rolling the dice to see how far he jumps. If he fails to make it all the way across, he falls through the gap (see [Falling](../core-rules/rules/movement.md#falling)).

### Sprint

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

The warrior may triple his [Movement](../core-rules/rules/movement.md#movement) rate when he runs or charges, rather than doubling it as normal.^❓

### Acrobat

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

The warrior is incredibly supple and agile. He may fall or jump from a height of up to 12" without taking any damage if he passes a single Initiative test, and can re-roll failed [Diving Charge](../core-rules/rules/movement.md#diving-charge) rolls. He can still only make a [diving charge](../core-rules/rules/movement.md#diving-charge) from a height of up to 6".

### Lightning Reflexes

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

If the warrior is charged he will 'strike first' against those that charged that turn. As the charger(s) will also normally 'strike first' (for charging), the order of attack between the charger(s) and the warrior with this skill will be determined by comparing Initiative values.^✏️

### Jump Up

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

The warrior can regain his footing in an instant, springing to his feet immediately if he is [knocked down](../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down). The warrior may ignore [knocked down](../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down) results when rolling for injuries, unless he is [knocked down](../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down) because of a successful save from wearing a [helmet](../weapons-armour/armour.md#helmet) or because he has the No Pain [special rule](../campaigns/scenarios/town-cryer/gathering-of-the-horde.md#special-rule).^✏️

### Dodge

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

A warrior with this skill is [nimble](standard-skills.md#nimble) and as fast as quicksilver. He can avoid any hits from a missile weapon on a D6 roll of 5+. Note that this roll is taken against missiles as soon as a hit is scored to see whether the warrior dodges it or not, before rolling to wound, and before any effects from other skills or equipment (such as [lucky charms](../weapons-armour/miscellaneous-equipment.md#lucky-charm)).^✏️

### Scale Sheer Surfaces

*Source: Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

A warrior with this skill can scale even the highest wall or fence with ease. He can climb up or down a height equal to twice his normal [Movement](../core-rules/rules/movement.md#movement), and does not need to make Initiative tests when doing so.
