# Blackpowder Weapons

[Blackpowder weapons](../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/advanced-rules.md#blackpowder-weapons) are only a very recent invention in the Warhammer world. They are often unreliable and temperamental but they do hit exceedingly hard and [armour](armour.md#armour) offers almost no protection, as their lead bullets can penetrate even the strongest breastplate or [shield](armour.md#shield).

Most [blackpowder weapons](../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/advanced-rules.md#blackpowder-weapons) are bought from Dwarfs at great expense, though some of the forges of Nuln and Altdorf have started to experiment with making [handguns](blackpowder.md#handgun).

### Blunderbuss

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [30 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#blackpowder-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Rare 9

*A [blunderbuss](blackpowder.md#blunderbuss) is a primitive blackpowder weapon, which fires a hail of lead balls, rusty bolts, bent nails, and other assorted scrap metal. It is a powerful, if erratic, weapon and takes such a long time to load that most warriors discard it after the first shot.*

**Range:** Special<br>**Strength:** 3

##### Special Rules

**Shot:** When your model fires the [blunderbuss](blackpowder.md#blunderbuss), draw a line 16" long and 1" wide in any direction from the firer (the line must be absolutely straight). Any and all models in its path are automatically hit by a Strength 3 hit.^❓

**Fire Once:** It takes a very long time to load a [blunderbuss](blackpowder.md#blunderbuss) so it may only be fired it once per battle.

### Duelling Pistol

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [30 gc (60 gc for a brace)](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#blackpowder-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Rare 10

*A [duelling pistol](blackpowder.md#duelling-pistol) is a work of art, and a gunsmith labours long and hard to produce a single example. They are often carried by Imperial nobles to solve disputes over love and honour, and many a noble has died at dawn in a duel over some grievance.

[Duelling pistols](blackpowder.md#duelling-pistol) are prohibitively expensive weapons and common warriors rarely have them. Even if they do manage to steal or buy one, the ammunition is prohibitively expensive.

Some of the wealthiest warriors in Mordheim carry [duelling pistols](blackpowder.md#duelling-pistol) as status symbols, commanding great respect, admiration and envy.*

**Range:** 10"<br>**Strength:** 4

##### Special Rules

**Accuracy:** A [duelling pistol](blackpowder.md#duelling-pistol) is built for accuracy as a skilled duellist is able to hit a coin from twenty paces. All shots and [close combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat) attacks^❓ from a [duelling pistol](blackpowder.md#duelling-pistol) have a +1 bonus to hit.^✏️

**Prepare shot:** A [duelling pistol](blackpowder.md#duelling-pistol) takes a complete turn to reload^❓, so your model may only fire every other turn. If he has a brace of [duelling pistols](blackpowder.md#duelling-pistol) he may fire every turn.

**Save modifier:** [Duelling pistols](blackpowder.md#duelling-pistol) are even better at penetrating [armour](armour.md#armour) than their Strength 4 suggests. A warrior wounded by a [duelling pistol](blackpowder.md#duelling-pistol) must make his [armour save](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#armour) with a -2 modifier.

**Hand-to-hand:** [Duelling pistols](blackpowder.md#duelling-pistol) can be used in [hand-to-hand combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#who-can-fight) as well as for [shooting](../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#shooting). A model armed with a [duelling pistol](blackpowder.md#duelling-pistol) and another [close combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat) weapon gains +1 Attack^❓, which is resolved at Strength 4 with a -2 save modifier. This bonus attack can be used only once per combat. If you are firing a brace of [duelling pistols](blackpowder.md#duelling-pistol), your model can fight with 2 Attacks in the first turn of [close combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat). These attacks are resolved with a model's Weapon Skill like any normal [close combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat) attack and likewise may be parried.^✏️ Successful hits are resolved at Strength 4 and with a -2 save modifier, regardless of the firer's Strength.

### Handgun

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [35 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#blackpowder-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Rare 8

*A [handgun](blackpowder.md#handgun) is a simple firearm. The quality of construction varies ranging from the crude wooden ‘hakbuts' of the artillery school of Nuln, to the more sophisticated Dwarf firearms that have levers and springs which hold the burning match, and triggers which release the firing mechanism and fire the gun. [Handguns](blackpowder.md#handgun) are not terribly reliable weapons: the gun barrel occasionally tends to explode violently or the powder fails to ignite. But the weapon has a great range and tremendous penetrating power, making a mockery of even the thickest [armour](armour.md#armour). In Mordheim, [handguns](blackpowder.md#handgun) are rare and expensive, but a warband which can boast such a weapon will command respect from all its rivals.*

**Range:** 24"<br>**Strength:** 4

##### Special Rules

**Prepare shot:** A [handgun](blackpowder.md#handgun) takes a complete turn to reload^❓, so you may only fire it every other turn.

**Move or fire:** You may not move and fire a [handgun](blackpowder.md#handgun) in the same turn, other than to pivot on the spot to face your target or stand up.

**Save Modifier:** [Handguns](blackpowder.md#handgun) are even better at penetrating [armour](armour.md#armour) than their Strength 4 suggests. A warrior wounded by a [handgun](blackpowder.md#handgun) must take its [armour save](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#armour) with a -2 modifier.

### Hunting Rifle

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [200 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#blackpowder-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Rare 11

*Hochland is a province famed for its hunters, and the preferred weapon of its nobility when they go hunting is a long-ranged rifle. They are extremely rare and precious weapons, and only the most experienced weaponsmiths are capable of manufacturing them.*

**Range:** 48"<br>**Strength:** 4

##### Special Rules

**Move or fire:** You may not move and fire a [Hochland long rifle](../warbands/grade-1b/gunnery-school-of-nuln.md#missile-weapons) in the same turn, other than to pivot on the spot to face your target or stand up from [knocked down](../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down).

**Prepare shot:** A [Hochland long rifle](../warbands/grade-1b/gunnery-school-of-nuln.md#missile-weapons) takes a complete turn to reload^❓, so you may only fire it every other turn.

**Pick target:** A model armed with a [Hochland long rifle](../warbands/grade-1b/gunnery-school-of-nuln.md#missile-weapons) can target any enemy model in sight, not just the closest one.

**Save modifier:** Hochland long rifles are even better at penetrating [armour](armour.md#armour) than their Strength 4 suggests. A warrior wounded by a [long rifle](../warbands/grade-1c/court-of-the-profane-pleasures.md#missile-weapons) must make his [armour save](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#armour) with a -2 modifier.

### Pistol

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [15 gc (30 gc for a brace)](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#blackpowder-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Rare 8

*A [pistol](blackpowder.md#pistol) is a small, simple blackpowder weapon fired by a spring mechanism. Most [pistols](blackpowder.md#pistol) are expensive, unreliable, and poorly constructed.*

**Range:** 6"<br>**Strength:** 4

##### Special Rules

**Prepare shot:** A [pistol](blackpowder.md#pistol) takes a whole turn to reload^❓, so you may only fire every other turn. If you have a brace of [pistols](blackpowder.md#pistol) (ie, two) you may fire every turn.

**Save modifier:** [Pistols](blackpowder.md#pistol) are even better at penetrating [armour](armour.md#armour) than their Strength 4 suggests. A warrior wounded by a [pistol](blackpowder.md#pistol) must make his [armour save](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#armour) with a -2 modifier.

**Hand-to-hand:** [Pistols](blackpowder.md#pistol) can be used in [hand-to-hand combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#who-can-fight) as well as for [shooting](../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#shooting). A model armed with a [pistol](blackpowder.md#pistol) and another [close combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat) weapon gains +1 Attack^❓, which is resolved at Strength 4 with a -2 save modifier. This bonus attack can be used only once per combat. If you are firing a brace of [pistols](blackpowder.md#pistol), your model can fight with 2 Attacks in the first turn of [close combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat). These attacks are resolved with a model's Weapon Skill like any normal [close combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat) attack and likewise may be parried.^✏️ Successful hits are resolved at Strength 4 and with a -2 save modifier, regardless of the firer's Strength.

[Warplock pistols](blackpowder.md#warplock-pistol) shoot ammunition made of magically treated warpstone and wounds caused by [warplock pistols](blackpowder.md#warplock-pistol) are horrible to behold and often cause infections.*

**Prepare shot:** A [pistol](blackpowder.md#pistol) takes a whole turn to reload^❓, so you may only fire every other turn. If you have a brace of [pistols](blackpowder.md#pistol) (ie, two) you may fire every turn.

This bonus attack can be used only once per combat.
