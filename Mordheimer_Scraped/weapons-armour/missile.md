# Missile Weapons

### Blowpipe

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [25 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#missile-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Rare 7 ( Skaven, Lizardmen and Forest Goblins only. Common for Forest Goblins. )

*The [blowpipe](missile.md#blowpipe) is a short hollow tube which can be used to shoot poisoned darts. While the darts by themselves are too small to cause significant damage, the poison used can cause searing agony and eventual death. The other advantage of a [blowpipe](missile.md#blowpipe) is that it is silent, and a well-hidden shooter can fire the darts undetected.*

**Range:** 8"<br>**Strength:** 1

##### Special Rules

**Poison:** The needles fired by a [blowpipe](missile.md#blowpipe) are coated in a venom very similar in its effects to the [Black Lotus](miscellaneous-equipment.md#black-lotus) (if you roll a 6 on the To [Hit roll](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#hit-roll), the victim is automatically wounded). A [blowpipe](missile.md#blowpipe) cannot cause [critical hits](../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#critical-hits). This weapon has a positive [armour save](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#armour) modifier, so a model that normally has a save of 5+ will get a save of 4+ against a [blowpipe](missile.md#blowpipe) dart. Even models that normally do not have an [armour save](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#armour) will get a 6+ save to take into account protection offered by clothes, fur or the like.

**Stealthy:** A model armed with a [blowpipe](missile.md#blowpipe) can fire while hidden without revealing his position to the enemy. The target model can take an Initiative test in order to try to spot the firing model. If the test is successful, the model no longer counts as hidden.

### Bow

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [10 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#missile-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Common

*The [bow](missile.md#bow) is carried by most races and used extensively in warfare. It is a compact yet powerful weapon, that is cheap to make and easy to maintain.*

**Range:** 24"<br>**Strength:** 3

### Crossbow

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [25 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#missile-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Common

*A [crossbow](missile.md#crossbow) consists of a short, strong bowstave mounted on a wooden or steel stock. The [crossbows](missile.md#crossbow) of the Empire are made of steel and often include a winding mechanism to draw back the string. It takes a long time to prepare a [crossbow](missile.md#crossbow), but a bolt fired from one has a tremendous range and can penetrate [armour](armour.md#armour) easily.

[Crossbows](missile.md#crossbow) take much longer than other bows to make, so they are expensive and relatively rare weapons. Still, they are the preferred weapon of many in Mordheim because of their power and long range.*

**Range:** 30"<br>**Strength:** 4

##### Special Rules

**Move or Fire:** You may not move and fire a [crossbow](missile.md#crossbow) on the same turn, other than to pivot on the spot to face your target or to stand up.

### Crossbow pistol

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [35 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#missile-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Rare 9

*[Crossbow pistols](missile.md#crossbow-pistol) are masterpieces made by expert weaponsmiths. They are miniature [crossbows](missile.md#crossbow) with all the power and accuracy of the real thing. As these weapons may be easily concealed, they are the favoured weapon of assassins.*

**Range:** 10"<br>**Strength:** 4

##### Special Rules

**Shoot In [Hand-To-Hand Combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#who-can-fight):** A model armed with a [crossbow pistol](missile.md#crossbow-pistol) may shoot it in the first round of a [hand-to-hand combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#who-can-fight) and this shot is always resolved first, before any blows are struck. This shot has an extra -2 to hit penalty. Use model's Ballistic Skill to see whether it hits or not. ^❓This bonus attack is in addition to any [close combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat) weapon attacks.^✏️

**Hand-To-Hand:** [Pistols](blackpowder.md#pistol) can be used in [hand-to-hand combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#who-can-fight) as well as for [shooting](../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#shooting). A model armed with a [pistol](blackpowder.md#pistol) and another [close combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat) weapon gains +1 Attack^❓, which is resolved at Strength 4. This bonus attack can be used only once per combat. If you are firing a brace of [pistols](blackpowder.md#pistol), your model can fight with 2 Attacks in the first turn of [close combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat). These attacks are resolved with a model's Weapon Skill like any normal [close combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat) attack and likewise may be parried.^✏️ Successful hits are resolved at Strength 4, regardless of the firer's Strength.^❓

### Elf Bow

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [35 + 3D6 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#missile-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Rare 12

*Elven bows are the finest [missile weapons](../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/spoils-of-war.md#missile-weapons) of their kind. Constructed from ithilmar or wood from the Elf forests, with strings woven from the hair of Elf maidens, Elven bows are far superior to any [missile weapons](../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/spoils-of-war.md#missile-weapons) made by other races. In the hands of an Elven archer, the [Elf bow](missile.md#elf-bow) is a truly potent weapon, its long range and penetrating power making it far superior to any [bow](missile.md#bow) made by humans.*

**Range:** 36"<br>**Strength:** 3

##### Special Rules

**Save Modifier:** An [Elf bow](missile.md#elf-bow) has a -1 save modifier on [armour](armour.md#armour) saves against it.

### Long Bow

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [15 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#missile-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Common

*A [long bow](missile.md#long-bow) is made of alternating layers of either yew or elm. A skilled archer can hit a chosen leaf on a tree from three hundred paces with this weapon.

The [long bow](missile.md#long-bow) is favoured by experienced archers due to its great reach and accuracy.*

**Range:** 30"<br>**Strength:** 3

### Repeater Crossbow

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [40 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#missile-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Rare 8

*[Repeater crossbows](missile.md#repeater-crossbow) are extremely complex devices, expensive to acquire and difficult to make. While this makes them rare, they certainly have their uses: they can rain a deadly hail of bolts on enemies, and a warrior using one may move quite fast and still fire his weapon.*

**Range:** 24"<br>**Strength:** 3

##### Special Rules

**Fire twice:** A model armed with a [repeater crossbow](missile.md#repeater-crossbow) may choose to fire twice per turn with an extra -1 to hit penalty on both shots.

### Short Bow

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [5 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#missile-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Common

*[Short bows](missile.md#short-bow) are small, short-ranged bows that are cheap and require little strength to use. Some cavalry carry a shortened [bow](missile.md#bow) which is easier to shoot from horseback than a larger [bow](missile.md#bow). Halflings also use [short bows](missile.md#short-bow), as they lack the strength and height required to use a [long bow](missile.md#long-bow).*

**Range:** 16"<br>**Strength:** 3

### Sling

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [2 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#missile-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Common

*[Slings](missile.md#sling) are rarely used, mainly because they are no more powerful than bows and have a shorter range. A [sling](missile.md#sling) is little more than a looped strip of cloth or leather into which a stone is placed. The [sling](missile.md#sling) is whirled about the slinger's head and the [sling](missile.md#sling) stone is then released towards the target.

While this weapon is looked down upon by most archers, a skilled slinger can slay a man from a considerable distance, and the ammunition is easy to find: rocks are everywhere and free!*

**Range:** 18"<br>**Strength:** 3

##### Special Rules

**Fire twice at half range:** A slinger may fire twice in the [shooting phase](../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#who-can-shoot) if he does not move in the [movement phase](../core-rules/rules/movement.md#moving). He cannot shoot over half range (9") though, if he fires twice.^❓ If the model fires twice then each shot is at -1 to hit.^✏️

### Throwing Knives/Stars

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [15 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#missile-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Rare 5

*[Throwing stars](missile.md#throwing-knivesstars) are used mainly by the assassins of the sinister House of Shadows, or by street thugs who specialise in ambushing the unwary. A perfectly balanced knife thrown from behind has ended the life of many a noble and merchant in Mordheim. [Throwing knives](missile.md#throwing-knivesstars) are not suitable for [close combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat), as their balance makes them unwieldy in close quarters.*

**Range:** 6"<br>**Strength:** As user

##### Special Rules

**Thrown Weapon:** Models using [throwing stars](missile.md#throwing-knivesstars) or knives do not suffer penalties for range or moving as these weapons are perfectly balanced for throwing. They cannot be used in [close combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat).
