# Close-combat Weapons

### Axe

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [5 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#hand-to-hand-combat-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Common

*The [axe](close-combat.md#axe) is the traditional weapon of Empire woodsmen, and is also used as a weapon in poorer rural areas. Axes have a heavy blade and, if swung by a strong man, can cause a lot of damage. The blade of an [axe](close-combat.md#axe) can easily cut through [armour](armour.md#armour), though it requires considerable strength from the wielder.

Of all the warriors in the Old World, Dwarfs are the most adept at making axes. Their axes are invaluable to the warriors of the Old World and are some of the most sought after weapons.*

**Range:** [Close Combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat)<br>**Strength:** As user

##### Special Rules

**Cutting Edge:** An [axe](close-combat.md#axe) has an extra save modifier of -1, so a model with Strength 4 using an [axe](close-combat.md#axe) has a -2 save modifier when he hits an opponent in [hand-to-hand combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#who-can-fight).

### Dagger

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [1st free/2 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#hand-to-hand-combat-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Common

*[Daggers](close-combat.md#dagger) and knives are extremely common, and men are allowed to carry them in enclaves where weapons are otherwise forbidden. Many a warrior in Mordheim has died with a [dagger](close-combat.md#dagger) in his back.*

**Range:** [Close Combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat)<br>**Strength:** As user

##### Special Rules

**+1 Enemy [armour save](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#armour):** [Daggers](close-combat.md#dagger) are not the best weapons to use for penetrating an enemy model's [armour](armour.md#armour). An enemy wounded by a [dagger](close-combat.md#dagger) gains a +1 bonus to his [armour save](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#armour), and a 6+ [armour save](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#armour) if he has none normally.

### Double-handed weapon

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [15 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#hand-to-hand-combat-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Common

*A blow from a double-handed [axe](close-combat.md#axe) or [sword](close-combat.md#sword) can cut a foe in half and break [armour](armour.md#armour) apart. It takes a long time to learn how to use these weapons and even then only extremely strong men are able to wield them effectively.*

**Range:** [Close Combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat)<br>**Strength:** As user +2

##### Special Rules

**Two-handed:** A model armed with a [double-handed weapon](close-combat.md#double-handed-weapon) may not use a [shield](armour.md#shield), [buckler](armour.md#buckler) or additional weapon in [close combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat). If the model is equipped with a [shield](armour.md#shield) he will still get a +1 bonus to his [armour save](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#armour) against [shooting](../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#shooting).

**Strike last:** [Double-handed weapons](close-combat.md#double-handed-weapon) are so heavy that the model using them always strikes last, even when charging.

### Fighting Claws

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [35 gc per pair](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#hand-to-hand-combat-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Rare 7 ( Skaven only )

*The martial arts practised by Clan Eshin employ many unusual weapons. The most famous of these are the Eshin [Fighting Claws](close-combat.md#fighting-claws): sharp metal blades attached to the paws of a Skaven warrior. It takes a real expert to use them effectively, but an adept of Clan Eshin is a [fearsome](../skills/standard-skills.md#fearsome) opponent when armed this way.*

**Range:** [Close Combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat)<br>**Strength:** As user

##### Special Rules

**Pair:** [Fighting Claws](close-combat.md#fighting-claws) are traditionally used in pairs, one in each hand. A warrior armed with [Fighting Claws](close-combat.md#fighting-claws) gets an additional attack.

**Climb:** A Skaven equipped with [Fighting Claws](close-combat.md#fighting-claws) can add +1 to his Initiative when making [Climbing](../core-rules/rules/movement.md#climbing) tests.

**[Parry](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#parry):** A Skaven armed with [Fighting Claws](close-combat.md#fighting-claws) may [parry](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#parry) blows and can re-roll a failed attempt once, in the same way as a model armed with a [sword](close-combat.md#sword) and [buckler](armour.md#buckler).

**Cumbersome:** A model armed with [Fighting Claws](close-combat.md#fighting-claws) may not use any other weapons in the entire battle.

### Fist

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Availability:** Common

*The truly desperate, who don't even own a knife, have to fight with their bare hands. Needless to say, their chances of survival are comparable to Halflings going without food for eight hours! 

Note: The following rules **only** apply to warriors who have lost their weapons. Creatures such as Zombies, animals, etc, ignore these rules.^❓*

**Range:** [Close Combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat)<br>**Strength:** As user -1

##### Special Rules

Warriors using their [fists](close-combat.md#fist) can only ever make **1** attack.

**+1 Enemy [armour save](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#armour):** An enemy wounded by a [fist](close-combat.md#fist) gains a +1 bonus to his [armour save](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#armour), and a 6+ [armour save](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#armour) if he normally has none.

### Flail

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [15 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#hand-to-hand-combat-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Common

*The [flail](close-combat.md#flail) is a heavy weapon wielded with both hands. It normally consists of heavy weights, often spiked, attached to a pole or handle by means of heavy chains. [Flails](close-combat.md#flail) drain the user's stamina quickly, but are awesomely destructive in the hands of a skilled (or unhinged) warrior.*

**Range:** [Close Combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat)<br>**Strength:** As user +2

##### Special Rules

**Heavy:** A [flail](close-combat.md#flail) is extremely tiring to use and thus the +2 Strength bonus applies only in the first turn of each [hand-to-hand combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#who-can-fight).

**Two-handed:** As a [flail](close-combat.md#flail) requires two hands to use, a model using a [flail](close-combat.md#flail) may not use a [shield](armour.md#shield), [buckler](armour.md#buckler) or additional weapon in [close combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat). If the model has a [shield](armour.md#shield) he still gets a +1 bonus to his [armour save](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#armour) against [shooting](../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#shooting).

### Gromril Weapon

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [4 x Price](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#hand-to-hand-combat-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Rare 11

*Only a Dwarf Runesmith can forge a weapon from gromril, a rare meteoric iron. A blade fashioned from this metal will stay keen for a thousand years.*

##### Special Rules

**Gromril:** A [gromril weapon](close-combat.md#gromril-weapon) has an extra -1 save modifier, and costs four times the price of a normal weapon of its kind. You may choose which type of hand-to-hand weapon is offered to you as explained in the [Trading](../campaigns/trading.md#trading) section.^❓

### Halberd

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [10 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#hand-to-hand-combat-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Common

*The [halberd](close-combat.md#halberd)'s heavy blade is mounted upon a sturdy shaft of oak or steel and has a point like a [spear](close-combat.md#spear) and a cutting edge like an [axe](close-combat.md#axe). Since it can be used to chop as well as thrust, it is an adaptable weapon, but is difficult to use inside buildings.*

**Range:** [Close Combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat)<br>**Strength:** As user +1

##### Special Rules

**Two-handed:** A model armed with a [halberd](close-combat.md#halberd) may not use a [shield](armour.md#shield), [buckler](armour.md#buckler) or additional weapon in [close combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat). If the model has a [shield](armour.md#shield) he still gets a +1 bonus to his [armour save](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#armour) against [shooting](../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#shooting).

### Hammer, Staff, Mace or Club

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [3 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#hand-to-hand-combat-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Common

*Perhaps the simplest type of weapon, these brutal, bludgeoning instruments range from primitive wooden [clubs](close-combat.md#hammer-staff-mace-or-club) to elaborately forged Dwarf [hammers](close-combat.md#hammer-staff-mace-or-club) made from the finest steel. A blow from a [mace](close-combat.md#hammer-staff-mace-or-club) can easily crush a skull or knock a man unconscious.*

**Range:** [Close Combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat)<br>**Strength:** As user

##### Special Rules

**Concussion:** [Hammers](close-combat.md#hammer-staff-mace-or-club) and other bludgeoning weapons are excellent to use for striking your enemy senseless. When using a [hammer](close-combat.md#hammer-staff-mace-or-club), [club](close-combat.md#hammer-staff-mace-or-club) or [mace](close-combat.md#hammer-staff-mace-or-club), a roll of 2-4 is treated as *[stunned](../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned)* when rolling to see the extent of a model's injuries.

### Ithilmar Weapon

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [3 x Price](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#hand-to-hand-combat-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Rare 9

*Elven blades are forged from priceless ithilmar, an extremely light but strong metal, found only in the fabled Elven kingdoms. A few of these weapons are occasionally found in the Old World and these are normally spoils of war, taken by [the Norse](../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning.md#the-norse) raiders who pillage the coastal settlements of [the Elves](../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning.md#the-elves).*

##### Special Rules

**Ithilmar:** An [ithilmar weapon](close-combat.md#ithilmar-weapon) gives its user +1 Initiative in [hand-to-hand combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#who-can-fight), and costs three times the price of a normal weapon of its kind. You may choose which hand-to-hand weapon is offered to you as explained in the [Trading](../campaigns/trading.md#trading) section.^❓

### Lance

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [40 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#hand-to-hand-combat-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Rare 8

*[Lances](close-combat.md#lance) are long, heavy [spears](close-combat.md#spear) used by mounted shock troops to rip through [armour](armour.md#armour) and fling their foes to the ground. They are the chosen weapons of Knights Templar and other wealthy warriors. To use a [lance](close-combat.md#lance) requires great skill and strength, and only the richest warriors ride the heavy warhorses needed to wield these mighty weapons effectively.*

**Range:** [Close Combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat)<br>**Strength:** As user +2 when charging on horseback

##### Special Rules

**Cavalry weapon:** A warrior must own a warhorse to use a [lance](close-combat.md#lance), as it can only be used whilst he is on horseback.

**Cavalry bonus:** If using [optional rules](../campaigns/campaign-settings/the-nemesis-crown/scenarios/last-orders.md#optional-rules) for mounted models, a warrior armed with a [lance](close-combat.md#lance) receives a +2 Strength bonus when he charges.^❓ This bonus only applies for that turn.

### Morning Star

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [15 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#hand-to-hand-combat-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Common

*A [morning star](close-combat.md#morning-star) consists of a wooden or steel shaft with heavy chains that have spiked steel balls attached. It is very destructive and requires great skill to wield effectively.*

**Range:** [Close Combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat)<br>**Strength:** As user +1

##### Special Rules

**Heavy:** The [morning star](close-combat.md#morning-star) is extremely tiring to use, so its +1 Strength bonus applies only in the first turn of each [hand-to-hand combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#who-can-fight).

**Difficult to use:** A model with a [morning star](close-combat.md#morning-star) may not use a second weapon or [buckler](armour.md#buckler) in his other hand because it requires all his skill to wield it. He may carry a [shield](armour.md#shield) as normal though.

### Sigmarite Warhammer

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [15 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#hand-to-hand-combat-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Common ( [Sisters of Sigmar](../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/warbands-and-hirelings.md#sisters-of-sigmar) only )

*One of the traditional weapons of the Sisterhood, the warhammer echoes Ghal-Maraz, the great [hammer](close-combat.md#hammer-staff-mace-or-club) of Sigmar himself.*

**Range:** [Close Combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat)<br>**Strength:** As user +1

##### Special Rules

**Concussion:** Warhammers are excellent at striking people senseless. When using a warhammer in [close combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat) a roll of 2-4 is treated as [stunned](../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned) when rolling on the Injury chart.

**Holy Weapon:** Each warhammer is blessed by the High Matriarch herself before it is handed to the Sisters. The warhammer has a +1 bonus on all to wound rolls against any Possessed or Undead models.^❓ Note that you will still need to score a 6 before any modifiers in order to cause a [critical hit](../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#critical-hits). Only Matriarchs and Sister Superiors may carry two [Sigmarite warhammers](close-combat.md#sigmarite-warhammer).

### Spear

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [10 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#hand-to-hand-combat-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Common

*[Spears](close-combat.md#spear) range from sharpened sticks used by Goblins to the impressive cavalry [spears](close-combat.md#spear) typical of [the Elves](../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning.md#the-elves).*

**Range:** [Close Combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat)<br>**Strength:** As user

##### Special Rules

**Strike first:** A warrior with a [spear](close-combat.md#spear) 'strikes first' in the first turn of [hand-to-hand combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#who-can-fight).^✏️

**Unwieldy:** A warrior with a [spear](close-combat.md#spear) may only use a [shield](armour.md#shield) or a [buckler](armour.md#buckler) in his other hand. He may not use a second weapon.^✏️

**Cavalry bonus:** If using the rules for mounted models, a mounted warrior armed with a [spear](close-combat.md#spear) receives a +1 Strength bonus when he charges. This bonus only applies for that turn.

### Spiked Gauntlet

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [15 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#hand-to-hand-combat-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Common ( Pit Fighters only )

*The [spiked gauntlet](close-combat.md#spiked-gauntlet) counts as an additional hand weapon and a [buckler](armour.md#buckler). And no, your Heroes cannot learn to use it!*

**Range:** [Close Combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat)<br>**Strength:** As user

##### Special Rules

**[Parry](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#parry):** A model equipped with a [Spiked Gauntlet](close-combat.md#spiked-gauntlet) may [parry](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#parry) the first blow in each round of [hand-to-hand combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#who-can-fight). When his opponent scores a hit, a model with a [Spiked Gauntlet](close-combat.md#spiked-gauntlet) may roll 1D6. If the score is greater than the highest to hit score of his opponent, the model has parried the blow, and that attack is discarded. A model may not [parry](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#parry) attacks made with double or more its own Strength – they are simply too powerful to be stopped. If your model is armed with a [Spiked Gauntlet](close-combat.md#spiked-gauntlet) and a [sword](close-combat.md#sword), you may re-roll any failed parries once. 

### Steel Whip

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [10 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#hand-to-hand-combat-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Common ( [Sisters of Sigmar](../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/warbands-and-hirelings.md#sisters-of-sigmar) and Chaos Dwarfs only )

*Another weapon unique to the Sisterhood is the [steel whip](close-combat.md#steel-whip), made from barbed steel chains.*

**Range:** [Close Combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat)<br>**Strength:** As user

##### Special Rules

**Cannot be parried:** The [steel whip](close-combat.md#steel-whip) is a flexible weapon and the Priestesses use it with great expertise. Attempts to [parry](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#parry) its strikes are futile. A model attacked by a [steel whip](close-combat.md#steel-whip) may not make parries with [swords](close-combat.md#sword) or [bucklers](armour.md#buckler).

**Whipcrack:** When the wielder charges they gain +1A for that turn. This bonus attack is added after any other modifications. When the wielder is charged they gain +1A that they may only use against the charger. This additional attack will 'strike first'. If the wielder is simultaneously charged by two or more opponents they will still only receive a total of +1A. If the wielder is using two whips at the same time then they get +1A for the additional hand weapon, but only the first whip gets the whipcrack +1A.^✏️

### Sword

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [10 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#hand-to-hand-combat-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Common

*The [sword](close-combat.md#sword) is often referred to as the 'king of weapons'. The most common [sword](close-combat.md#sword) available, the [broadsword](../warbands/grade-1c/bretonnian-chapel-guard.md#hand-to-hand-combat-weapons) of the Empire, is a masterpiece by the standards of any smith: four full feet of gleaming steel, double-edged and razor-sharp.

[Swords](close-combat.md#sword) are much more effective weapons than crude [clubs](close-combat.md#hammer-staff-mace-or-club) and axes, though learning to use a [sword](close-combat.md#sword) is a long and difficult process. It takes years to truly master this weapon – most warriors in Mordheim die long before they get this far!*

**Range:** [Close Combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat)<br>**Strength:** As user

##### Special Rules

**[Parry](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#parry):** [Swords](close-combat.md#sword) offer an excellent balance of defence and offence. A model armed with a [sword](close-combat.md#sword) may [parry](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#parry) blows. When his opponent rolls to hit, the model armed with a [sword](close-combat.md#sword) may roll a D6. If the score is greater than the highest to hit score of his opponent, the model has parried the blow, and that attack is discarded. A model may not [parry](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#parry) attacks made with double or more its own Strength – they are simply too powerful to be stopped.

### Weeping Blades

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [50 gc per pair](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#hand-to-hand-combat-weapons)<br>**Availability:** Rare 9 ( Skaven only )

*The adepts of Clan Eshin use weapons called [Weeping Blades](close-combat.md#weeping-blades), murderous [swords](close-combat.md#sword) constructed with a small amount of warpstone in their structure. A [Weeping Blade](close-combat.md#weeping-blades) constantly sweats a deadly corrosive venom.*

**Range:** [Close Combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat)<br>**Strength:** As user

##### Special Rules

**Pair:** [Weeping Blades](close-combat.md#weeping-blades) are traditionally used in pairs, one in each hand. A warrior armed with [Weeping Blades](close-combat.md#weeping-blades) gets an additional attack.

**Venomous:** The venom of [Weeping Blades](close-combat.md#weeping-blades) will enter the blood of the victim and ravage his organs and muscles. These weapons count as being permanently coated in [black lotus](miscellaneous-equipment.md#black-lotus). No additional poison may be applied to [Weeping Blades](close-combat.md#weeping-blades).

**[Parry](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#parry):** [Weeping Blades](close-combat.md#weeping-blades) are [swords](close-combat.md#sword) and can be used for parrying.
