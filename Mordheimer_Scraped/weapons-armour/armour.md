# Armour

When fighting in [close combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#close-combat), a good suit of [armour](armour.md#armour) may mean the difference between life and death. The finest [armour](armour.md#armour) in the known world is made in Dwarf forges, where the secrets of steel and fire are well understood. [Hardened leather](../warbands/grade-1b/pit-fighters-old.md#armour) jackets are often worn by the hunters of Osterland, while city soldiers prefer mail coats and steel breastplates. The forges of the Empire have many skilled smiths capable of forging fine [armour](armour.md#armour), for the humans learned this craft from Dwarf master smiths in the early days of the Empire.

In Mordheim, only the most wealthy and powerful are able to afford the luxury of owning a suit of [armour](armour.md#armour) (the less well-off have to make do with leather tunics and occasionally [helmets](armour.md#helmet) and wooden [shields](armour.md#shield)). The richest leaders of the most successful warbands wear suits of high quality [armour](armour.md#armour) and a good suit of [armour](armour.md#armour) is just as much a symbol of wealth and power as it is protection against weapons.

### Barding

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [80 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#armour)<br>**Availability:** Rare 8 ( Warhorses only )

**Save:** +1 to existing save<br>

##### Special Rules

**[Movement](../core-rules/rules/movement.md#movement) Penalty:** [Armour](armour.md#armour), called [barding](armour.md#barding), may be purchased for a warhorse. It adds a further +1 bonus to the model's [armour save](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#armour), but subtracts one from its [Movement](../core-rules/rules/movement.md#movement). A normal horse may not wear [barding](armour.md#barding). 

**Durability:** A barded warhorse is only killed on a serious injury roll of ‘1' if the model goes out of action.

### Buckler

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [5 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#armour)<br>**Availability:** Common

*[Bucklers](armour.md#buckler) are small, round [shields](armour.md#shield) designed for parrying or deflecting blows. They are usually made of steel for they need to be tremendously durable to survive the brutal blows of [hand-to-hand combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#who-can-fight). Using a [buckler](armour.md#buckler) requires great skill, but a [nimble](../skills/standard-skills.md#nimble) warrior can protect himself from blows which would otherwise cripple him.*

##### Special Rules

**[Parry](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#parry):** A model equipped with a [buckler](armour.md#buckler) may [parry](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#parry) the first blow in each round of [hand-to-hand combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#who-can-fight). When his opponent scores a hit, a model with a [buckler](armour.md#buckler) may roll 1D6. If the score is greater than the highest to hit score of his opponent, the model has parried the blow, and that attack is discarded. A model may not [parry](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#parry) attacks made with double or more its own Strength – they are simply too powerful to be stopped.

### Gromril Armour

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [150 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#armour)<br>**Availability:** Rare 11

*Gromril is the rarest and strongest metal known of in the Old World. Only a very few Dwarf smiths know the secret of forging gromril, and a suit of [armour](armour.md#armour) made from it fetches a huge price.*

**Save:** 4+<br>

##### Special Rules

**[Movement](../core-rules/rules/movement.md#movement) Penalty:** [Gromril armour](armour.md#gromril-armour)^❓ gives the wearer a 4+ basic save, and does not slow him down if he is also armed with a [shield](armour.md#shield).

### Heavy Armour

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [50 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#armour)<br>**Availability:** Common

*Typical [heavy armour](armour.md#heavy-armour) is made from metal links and is called chain mail. Forging chain mail is a laborious and time consuming process, as the blacksmith must put together hundreds, sometimes thousands, of metal links. This makes chain mail expensive, but this type of [armour](armour.md#armour) provides excellent protection for anyone who can afford it.

There are other types of [heavy armour](armour.md#heavy-armour) as well, of which the best known are the steel breastplates and greaves worn by the foot knights of the Templar orders.*

**Save:** 5+<br>

##### Special Rules

**[Movement](../core-rules/rules/movement.md#movement) Penalty:** A warrior that is armed with both [heavy armour](armour.md#heavy-armour) and a [shield](armour.md#shield) suffers a -1 [Movement](../core-rules/rules/movement.md#movement) penalty

### Helmet

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [10 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#armour)<br>**Availability:** Common

*From the shining steel [helmets](armour.md#helmet) of Bretonnian knights to the leather caps of the Skaven, all sensible warriors try to protect the most vulnerable part of their body – their head. Even the most vain fighters still use a [helmet](armour.md#helmet), as it can be festooned with plumes, horns and other decorations.

[Helmets](armour.md#helmet) come in varying shapes and sizes, but their basic function remains the same.*

##### Special Rules

**Avoid Stun:** A model that is equipped with a [helmet](armour.md#helmet) has a special 4+ save on a D6 against being [stunned](../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned). If the save is made, treat the [stunned](../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned) result as [knocked down](../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down) instead. This save is not modified by the opponent's Strength.

### Ithilmar Armour

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [90 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#armour)<br>**Availability:** Rare 11

*Ithilmar is a silvery metal which is as light as silk and stronger than steel. Elves are experts at fashioning weapons and [armour](armour.md#armour) from ithilmar, and the Elven kingdom of Caledor is the only place in the world where this metal can be found.*

**Save:** 5+<br>

##### Special Rules

**[Movement](../core-rules/rules/movement.md#movement) Penalty:** [Ithilmar armour](armour.md#ithilmar-armour)^❓ gives the wearer a 5+ basic save, and does not slow him down if he is also armed with a [shield](armour.md#shield).

### Light Armour

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [20 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#armour)<br>**Availability:** Common

*[Light Armour](armour.md#light-armour) encompasses a wide variety of materials from [hardened leather](../warbands/grade-1b/pit-fighters-old.md#armour) tunics to chain shirts forged from steel. It does not offer complete protection against arrows or [swords](close-combat.md#sword), but it is better than having nothing at all. [Light armour](armour.md#light-armour) does not inhibit [movement](../core-rules/rules/movement.md#movement).*

**Save:** 6+<br>

##### Special Rules

**[Movement](../core-rules/rules/movement.md#movement) Penalty:** No penalty when worn with a [shield](armour.md#shield).

### Shield

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [5 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#armour)<br>**Availability:** Common

*There are two types of [shield](armour.md#shield) common to the warriors of Mordheim: the first is made of wood, occasionally reinforced with metal plates. This basic type of [shield](armour.md#shield), although strong, does tend to splinter, but this can sometimes save the user's life as his enemy's weapon can get trapped allowing him to strike back whilst his enemy struggles to free his weapon.

Metal [shields](armour.md#shield) are heavy and cumbersome, but last much longer and can take a battering. A typical Empire [shield](armour.md#shield) is either round or triangular, and carries the emblem of the province or city of its owner.*

**Save:** 6+<br>

##### Special Rules

**Combined Save:** As explained in the [Armour](armour.md#armour) section, [shields](armour.md#shield) combine with [armour](armour.md#armour) to give +1 to the save.

**[Movement](../core-rules/rules/movement.md#movement) Penalty:** There is no [movement](../core-rules/rules/movement.md#movement) penalty.
