# Miscellaneous Equipment

This section covers all the strange and unusual equipment your warriors can find in the ruins or buy from the merchants and pedlars in the settlements and villages around Mordheim.

Only **Heroes** may buy and carry the equipment described in this section. You may **not** give it to Henchmen unless the rules specifically say so.

### Blessed Water

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [10 + 3D6 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#miscellaneous)<br>**Availability:** Rare 6 ( May not be bought by Undead, Common for Warrior-Priests and [Sisters of Sigmar](../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/warbands-and-hirelings.md#sisters-of-sigmar) )

*The priests of Ulric, Sigmar, Mórr and Manann hold great power over evil. Pure water from a clear fountain, blessed by one of these priests, is said to burn things of darkness and evil.*

##### Special Rules

A vial of [blessed water](miscellaneous-equipment.md#blessed-water) contains enough liquid for just one use, and has a thrown range of twice the thrower's Strength in inches. Roll to hit using the model's BS. No modifiers for range or moving apply. [Blessed water](miscellaneous-equipment.md#blessed-water) causes 1 wound on Undead, Daemon or Possessed models automatically. There is no [armour save](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#armour). Undead or Possessed models may not use [blessed water](miscellaneous-equipment.md#blessed-water).

### Bugman's Ale

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [50 + 3D6 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#miscellaneous)<br>**Availability:** Rare 9

*Of all the Dwarf brewmasters, Josef Bugman is the most famous. His ale is known throughout the Old World, and widely regarded as the best.*

##### Special Rules

A warband that drinks a barrel of Bugman's before a battle will be immune to [fear](../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#fear) for the whole of the battle. Elves may not drink [Bugman's ale](miscellaneous-equipment.md#bugmans-ale) as they are far too delicate to cope with its effects. There is only enough ale to supply the warband for one battle.^✏️

### Cathayan Silk Clothes

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [50 + 2D6 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#miscellaneous)<br>**Availability:** Rare 9

*Some rich warband leaders like to flaunt their wealth and purchase clothes made out of silk from distant Cathay. This silk is the most expensive fabric in the known world, and wearing such clothes is a sure way to attract attention – especially thieves and assassins!*

##### Special Rules

Any Mercenary warband whose leader is wearing silk clothes may re-roll the first failed [Rout test](../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#the-rout-test). However, after each battle in which the leader is taken out of action, roll a D6. On a roll of 1-3 the clothes are ruined and must be discarded.

### Elven Cloak

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [100 + D6 x 10 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#miscellaneous)<br>**Availability:** Rare 12

*Made from the hair of Elven maidens and interwoven with living tree leaves, an [Elven cloak](miscellaneous-equipment.md#elven-cloak) is a wonder to behold. A warrior wearing such a cloak will blend into the shadows, making it very difficult to shoot at them with [missile weapons](../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/spoils-of-war.md#missile-weapons). [Elven cloaks](miscellaneous-equipment.md#elven-cloak) rarely come up for sale, but are sometimes recovered from dead warriors or offered by Elves as [rewards](../campaigns/scenarios/archive-pestilen/defend-the-village.md#rewards) to men who have served them in some way.*

##### Special Rules

A warrior aiming a missile weapon at a warrior wearing an [Elven cloak](miscellaneous-equipment.md#elven-cloak) suffers -1 on his to [hit roll](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#hit-roll).

### Garlic

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [1 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#miscellaneous)<br>**Availability:** Common ( May not be bought by Undead )

*[Garlic](miscellaneous-equipment.md#garlic) is a common herb grown in most gardens of the Empire. It is said to ward off Vampires and other denizens of the dark.*

##### Special Rules

A Vampire must pass a [Leadership test](../core-rules/rules/characteristics.md#leadership-tests) or it will be unable to charge a model carrying a clove of [garlic](miscellaneous-equipment.md#garlic). [Garlic](miscellaneous-equipment.md#garlic) lasts for the duration of one battle only, whether it is used or not.

### Halfling Cookbook

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [30 + 3D6 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#miscellaneous)<br>**Availability:** Rare 7 ( Undead or the Carnival of Chaos may not use this item )

*All Halfling chefs have their own secret recipes, and these are recorded in tomes handwritten in Mootland, the home country of the Halflings. Food prepared according to these recipes will attract warriors during these lean times.*

##### Special Rules

The maximum number of warriors allowed in your warband is increased by +1^❓ (note that neither an Undead warband nor a Carnival of Chaos warband can use this item).^✏️

### Holy (Unholy) Relic

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [15 + 3D6 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#miscellaneous)<br>**Availability:** Rare 8 ( Rare 6 for Warrior-Priests and [Sisters of Sigmar](../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/warbands-and-hirelings.md#sisters-of-sigmar) )

*In this age of superstition and religious fanaticism, holy objects are an important part of life. Relics abound in the Old World: hairs of Sigmar, pieces from Ulric's [hammer](close-combat.md#hammer-staff-mace-or-club), teeth of Daemon Princes, all are sold to men needing encouragement before battle and as charms against [sorcery](../skills/standard-skills.md#sorcery).*

##### Special Rules

A model with a holy relic will automatically pass the first [Leadership test](../core-rules/rules/characteristics.md#leadership-tests) he is required to make in the game. If worn by the leader, it will allow him to automatically pass the first [Rout test](../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#the-rout-test) if he has not taken any [Leadership tests](../core-rules/rules/characteristics.md#leadership-tests) before. 

You can only ignore the first [Leadership test](../core-rules/rules/characteristics.md#leadership-tests) in any single game – owning two or more holy relics will not allow you to ignore second and subsequent tests.

### Holy Tome

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [100 + D6 x 10 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#miscellaneous)<br>**Availability:** Rare 8 ( Warrior-Priests and [Sisters of Sigmar](../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/warbands-and-hirelings.md#sisters-of-sigmar) only )

*Books of prayers and descriptions of the holy deeds of religious heroes like Sigmar Heldenhammer are copied by hand in the scriptoriums of Sigmar and Ulric, and given or sold to the faithful. Of these tomes, the Deus Sigmar is the most common and well known, but other texts such as the Scriptures of Sigmar are also sold to those who follow the faith. A holy man can recite his prayers from such a book, strengthening his faith and belief.*

##### Special Rules

A Warrior Priest or Sister of Sigmar with a [holy tome](miscellaneous-equipment.md#holy-tome) can add +1 to the score when determining whether he (or she) can recite a spell successfully or not.

### Hunting Arrows

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [25 + 1D6 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#miscellaneous)<br>**Availability:** Rare 8

*The best [hunting arrows](miscellaneous-equipment.md#hunting-arrows) are made by the hunters of Drakwald forest. They have sharp, barbed arrowheads which cause excruciating pain when they hit their target. A skilled archer can severely injure his target with a single arrow.*

##### Special Rules

A model using a [Short Bow](missile.md#short-bow), [Bow](missile.md#bow), [Long Bow](missile.md#long-bow) or [Elf Bow](missile.md#elf-bow) may use these arrows. They add +1 to all Injury rolls.^❓

### Lantern

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [10 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#miscellaneous)<br>**Availability:** Common

##### Special Rules

A model that is in possession of a [lantern](miscellaneous-equipment.md#lantern) may add +4" to the distance from which he is able to spot hidden enemies.

### Lucky Charm

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [10 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#miscellaneous)<br>**Availability:** Rare 6

*These take many shapes, but the most common are symbolic [hammers](close-combat.md#hammer-staff-mace-or-club) that a pious Sigmarite Priest has touched, or carved heads of ancient Dwarf gods.*

##### Special Rules

The first time a model with a [lucky charm](miscellaneous-equipment.md#lucky-charm) is hit in a battle they roll a D6. On a 4+ the hit is discarded and no damage is suffered. Owning two or more charms does not confer any extra benefits, the model may still only try to discard the first hit.^✏️

### Mordheim Map

*Some survivors of the cataclysm still remain in the many settlements around Mordheim, and make a living by preparing maps of the city from memory. Many of these maps are faked, and even real ones are often crude and inaccurate.*

##### Special Rules

A map can help a warband find their way through the confusing maze of streets and into areas with rich buildings to loot.

### Net

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [5 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#miscellaneous)<br>**Availability:** Common

*Steel nets, such as those used by Pit Fighters, can be used in battles.*

##### Special Rules

Once per game, the [net](miscellaneous-equipment.md#net) may be thrown in the [shooting phase](../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#who-can-shoot) instead of the model [shooting](../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#shooting) a missile weapon. Treat the [net](miscellaneous-equipment.md#net) as a missile weapon in all respects with a range of 8". Use the model's BS to determine whether the [net](miscellaneous-equipment.md#net) hits or not – there are no [movement](../core-rules/rules/movement.md#movement) or range penalties. If it hits, the target must immediately roll a D6. If the result is equal to, or lower than his Strength, he rips the [net](miscellaneous-equipment.md#net) apart. If the result is higher, he may not move, shoot or cast spells in his next turn, although he is not otherwise affected. In either case the [net](miscellaneous-equipment.md#net) is lost.^❓

##### Net

"All one use only items are clearly identified as such. [Net](miscellaneous-equipment.md#net) is not, so it can be recovered between battles. As indicated in the colour text, it can indeed be torn, cut etc. during battle, but the warrior will simply repair it. I mean [swords](close-combat.md#sword) get bent, [shields](armour.md#shield) splintered, [armour](armour.md#armour) battered, [spear](close-combat.md#spear) points get blunted during a typical Mordheim battle, and we simply consider the warriors have the basic skills to repair such damage. Same goes for the nets."<br>- Tuomas Pirinen, Facebook

### Poisons and Drugs

The use of poison is almost universally abhorred, but in the ruthless and brutal battles fought in Mordheim, desperate warbands often resort to the use of envenomed blades.

Poison may not be used with [blackpowder weapons](../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/appendix/advanced-rules.md#blackpowder-weapons). When you buy a vial of poison, there is always only enough to last the duration of one battle. You can only poison a single weapon with one vial of poison.

#### Black Lotus

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [10 + 1D6 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#miscellaneous)<br>**Availability:** Rare 9 ( Not available to [Witch Hunters](../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/warbands-and-hirelings.md#witch-hunters), Warrior-Priests or [Sisters of Sigmar](../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/warbands-and-hirelings.md#sisters-of-sigmar). Rare 7 for Skaven and Lizardmen, 10 gc and Common For Skink Heroes )

*In the deepest forests of the Southlands grows a plant that is extremely poisonous. It is known as [Black Lotus](miscellaneous-equipment.md#black-lotus) and is much sought after by alchemists, assassins, wizards of the Western Coast and bored wives.*

##### Special Rules

A weapon coated with the sap of the [Black Lotus](miscellaneous-equipment.md#black-lotus) will wound its target automatically if you roll a 6 to hit. Note that you can still roll a dice for every wound inflicted in this way. If you roll a 6, you will inflict a [critical hit](../core-rules/rules/shooting.md#critical-hits) with that roll. If you do not roll a 6, you will cause a normal wound. Take [armour](armour.md#armour) saves as normal.

#### Crimson Shade

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [35 + 1D6 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#miscellaneous)<br>**Availability:** Rare 8

*[Crimson Shade](miscellaneous-equipment.md#crimson-shade) is the name given by Old Worlders to the leaves of the blood oak of Estalia. It is an extremely addictive drug, but grants its users inhuman quickness and strength.*

##### Special Rules

**Effect:** A model using [Crimson Shade](miscellaneous-equipment.md#crimson-shade) has his Initiative increased by +D3 points, and [Movement](../core-rules/rules/movement.md#movement) and Strength by +1 (this effect lasts for one game). [Crimson Shade](miscellaneous-equipment.md#crimson-shade) has no effect on Undead such as Vampires and Zombies, or the Possessed.

**Side effects:** [After the battle](../campaigns/scenarios/other/mordheims-burning.md#after-the-battle), roll 2D6. On a roll of 2-3, the model becomes addicted and you must try to buy him a new batch of [Crimson Shade](miscellaneous-equipment.md#crimson-shade) before every battle from now on. If you fail to buy any, he will leave your warband. On a roll of 12 the model's Initiative is increased permanently by +1.

#### Dark Venom

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [30 + 2D6 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#miscellaneous)<br>**Availability:** Rare 8 ( Not available to [Witch Hunters](../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/warbands-and-hirelings.md#witch-hunters), Warrior-Priests or [Sisters of Sigmar](../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/warbands-and-hirelings.md#sisters-of-sigmar). Rare 6 for Dark Elves and Lizardmen, 20 gc and Common For Skink Heroes )

*This is a poison extracted from Heldrakes, gigantic sea serpents that plague the Western Ocean and the coast of Naggaroth. The slightest wound infected by [Dark Venom](miscellaneous-equipment.md#dark-venom) causes excruciating pain, incapacitating the bravest of men.*

##### Special Rules

Any hit caused by a weapon coated with [Dark Venom](miscellaneous-equipment.md#dark-venom) counts as having +1 Strength, so, for example, if a warrior with Strength 3 wielding a poisoned [sword](close-combat.md#sword) hits an opponent, he will cause a Strength 4 hit instead. [Armour](armour.md#armour) saving throws are modified to take into account the increased Strength of the attack.

#### Healing Herbs

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [20 + 2D6 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#miscellaneous)<br>**Availability:** Rare 8 ( Common for Amazons ([Lustria](../campaigns/campaign-settings/lustria.md#lustria)) )

*Certain plants that grow on the banks of the River Stir have curative properties. Herbalists collect their roots and leaves and use them to treat the sick and wounded.*

##### Special Rules

A Hero with [healing herbs](miscellaneous-equipment.md#healing-herbs) can use them at the beginning of any of his recovery phases as long as he is not engaged in [hand-to-hand combat](../core-rules/rules/close-combat.md#who-can-fight). This restores all wounds he has previously lost during the game.^❓

##### Healing Herbs

"[Healing Herbs](miscellaneous-equipment.md#healing-herbs) were meant to be under Poison and Drugs. This is an oversight. Should be one and done. But RAW, unlimited use, and therefore OP. Luckily this is Mordheim, and all players should agree this was an oversight."<br>- Tuomas Pirinen, Facebook

#### Mad Cap Mushrooms

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [30 + 3D6 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#miscellaneous)<br>**Availability:** Rare 9 ( Common if warband includes Goblins )

*The feared cult of Goblin Fanatics of the Worlds Edge Mountains use these hallucinogenic mushrooms to drive themselves into a frenzied state.*

##### Special Rules

**Effect:** Any warrior who takes [Mad Cap Mushrooms](miscellaneous-equipment.md#mad-cap-mushrooms) before a battle will be subject to [frenzy](../core-rules/rules/leadership-psychology.md#frenzy). The Mad Cap Mushroom has no effect on Undead such as Vampires and Zombies, or the Possessed.

**Side effect:** [After the battle](../campaigns/scenarios/other/mordheims-burning.md#after-the-battle), roll a D6. On a roll of a 1 the model becomes permanently stupid.

#### Mandrake Root

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [25 + 1D6 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#miscellaneous)<br>**Availability:** Rare 8

*The man-shaped [Mandrake Root](miscellaneous-equipment.md#mandrake-root) grows in the rotting swamps of Sylvania. It is a noxious, deadly plant which is highly addictive and slowly kills its users, but also allows them to shrug off almost any pain.*

##### Special Rules

**Effect:** [Mandrake Root](miscellaneous-equipment.md#mandrake-root) makes a man almost oblivious to pain. His Toughness is increased by +1 for the duration of a battle and he treats all [stunned](../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-stunned) results as [knocked down](../core-rules/rules/wounds-and-injuries.md#warriors-knocked-down) instead. [Mandrake Root](miscellaneous-equipment.md#mandrake-root) has no effect on Undead, such as Vampires and Zombies, or on the Possessed.

**Side effects:** [Mandrake Root](miscellaneous-equipment.md#mandrake-root) is highly poisonous. At the end of the battle, roll 2D6. On a roll of 2-3 the model loses 1 point of Toughness permanently.

#### Tears of Shallaya

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [10 + 2D6 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#miscellaneous)<br>**Availability:** Rare 7 ( Not available to Possessed or Undead models )

*[Tears of Shallaya](miscellaneous-equipment.md#tears-of-shallaya) are vials of water from the holy spring in Couronne. Shallaya is the goddess of healing and mercy and it is said that this water has curative properties and is proof against any poison.*

##### Special Rules

A model who drinks a vial of the [Tears of Shallaya](miscellaneous-equipment.md#tears-of-shallaya) at the beginning of a battle will be completely immune to all poisons for the duration of combat. Undead and Possessed warriors may not use the [Tears of Shallaya](miscellaneous-equipment.md#tears-of-shallaya).

There is enough liquid in a vial of the [Tears of Shallaya](miscellaneous-equipment.md#tears-of-shallaya) to last for the duration of one battle.

### Rope & Hook

*A warrior using a [rope & hook](miscellaneous-equipment.md#rope--hook) will find it much easier to move amongst the ruins of Mordheim.*

##### Special Rules

A warrior equipped with a [rope & hook](miscellaneous-equipment.md#rope--hook) may re-roll failed Initiative tests when [climbing](../core-rules/rules/movement.md#climbing) up and down.

### Superior Blackpowder

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [30 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#miscellaneous)<br>**Availability:** Rare 11

*The model has acquired a better quality of blackpowder than is normally available.*

##### Special Rules

This new batch adds +1 Strength to all [blackpowder](blackpowder.md) weapons that the model has. There is enough [superior blackpowder](miscellaneous-equipment.md#superior-blackpowder) to last for one game.

### Tome of Magic

*Mordheim Rulebook ( core )*

**Cost:** [200 + D6 x 25 gc](https://mordheimer.net/docs/trading-post#miscellaneous)<br>**Availability:** Rare 12 ( Not available to [Witch Hunters](../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/warbands-and-hirelings.md#witch-hunters) or [Sisters of Sigmar](../campaigns/campaign-settings/border-town-burning/warbands-and-hirelings.md#sisters-of-sigmar) )

*Sometimes books of forbidden lore are offered for sale in the markets and dark alleys of the settlements around Mordheim.*

##### Special Rules

If a warband includes a wizard, he will gain an extra spell from the tome, permanently. He may randomly generate this new spell from his own list or the [Lesser Magic](https://mordheimer.net/docs/magic/lesser-magic) list. See the Magic section for details. The benefits from each [Tome of Magic](miscellaneous-equipment.md#tome-of-magic) apply to only one model.^✏️❓

##### Arcane Lore

If the warband doesn't contain a wizard, a warrior with the [Arcane Lore](miscellaneous-equipment.md#arcane-lore) skill may use the [Tome of Magic](miscellaneous-equipment.md#tome-of-magic) to learn Lesser Magic
